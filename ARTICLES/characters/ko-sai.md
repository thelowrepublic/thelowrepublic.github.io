**Ko Sai** is the Kaminoan who designed the accelerated ageing of the [[Clones|clones]]. She now sells the procedure that undoes it.

## The procedure

*Main article: [[Clones#Ko Sai's procedure]]*

The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] sponsors Ko Sai's procedure for all clones working for it. It is genome work followed by months of drug therapy to make the changes hold. It's done once, and after that every man takes a maintenance course of pills priced so low that nobody notices it. Naturally, the price is not the issue, but the availability is. Even if a clone purchases the procedure with independent funds, he will need to keep returning to the Collective for refills. For an old man, the procedure halves their accelerated ageing. If done when the clone is young enough, it returns him to ordinary ageing altogether.

The procedure is priced at nine thousand credits, paid once, with a maintenance course cheap enough that most can afford it. [[Rex]] refused Ko Sai's life extension procedure until the end.

## In 2 ABY

Hundreds of thousands of clones, Collective or not, have had the procedure, and Ko Sai is raking in the profits from a steady client base.
