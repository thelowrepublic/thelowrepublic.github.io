**Ahsoka Tano** captured [[Maul]] on [[Mandalore]] in 19 BBY, as a commander with the 501st Legion, and he escaped in the confusion that followed [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine's death]]. She withdrew in 8 BBY to take care of [[Rex]], and has been missing since the sixth month of 0 ABY.

## Background

Ahsoka Tano is a Togruta, born on Shili. At the start of the Clone Wars she became the Padawan of [[Anakin Skywalker]], and as a commander in the Grand Army of the Republic she fought beside Captain [[Rex]] of the 501st Legion. She had left the Jedi Order before the end of the war. She came back to lead Republic forces at the Siege of [[Mandalore]], where she faced [[Maul]].

## Maul, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine#The escape of Maul]]*

Four days before [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine's killing]], the Zabrak warlord [[Maul]] had been captured on [[Mandalore]] by Commander Ahsoka Tano and the 501st Legion. He was put aboard a Venator-class Star Destroyer bound for [[Coruscant]].

He was the only prisoner in the galaxy who understood immediately what Palpatine's death meant. He had known his former master's identity for over a decade and had been dismissed as a lunatic every time he said so.

When the news of Palpatine's death reached the fleet, its chain of command was in disarray. There were recall orders, competing authorities, rumours of a Jedi coup, and a Senate demanding the return of every capital ship in the Core. Ahsoka was temporarily disarmed and detained. Maul escaped in the confusion, tearing apart half of the Star Destroyer in the process, and reached Mandalorian space within the month.

When life support started failing, Tano was released from confinement, but prioritised saving lives over pursuing Maul. She does not regret her choice, but still views the operation as one of the greatest failures of her life.

## Manumission, 16–6 BBY

*Main article: [[The Manumission Act]]*

The Manumission Act of 16 BBY made every [[Clones|clone]] a citizen. [[Padmé Amidala]] drafted it and [[Bail Organa]] carried it. Ahsoka Tano and Captain [[Rex]] argued it through the Senate, giving more than four hundred hours of testimony between them. It passed as much out of necessity as conscience. Eight million living beings could not be released from service into nothing at all, with no standing under any law. Two million of them were children.

Tano never went back to the Order and never took another field command. She spent seven years as the most effective campaigner in the Republic who held no seat, first as the public face of manumission alongside Rex and then of the settlements bringing Separatist worlds back in. The [[The Manumission Act#The abandonment|pension correction]] she had spent seven years arguing for passed in 6 BBY, by which point most of the men it was for were dead.

## Murkhana, 16–14 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

At [[Murkhana]] in 16 BBY, [[Anakin Skywalker]], then a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], destroyed a city, and about two hundred thousand civilians died.

The inquiry treated Murkhana as a question of fact and of who had authorised what. It never asked the other question, which is what the thing did to the man who did it. The accounts that matter were never entered into any record, because nobody was collecting them. They come from [[Padmé Amidala]], from [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] before the estrangement, from Ahsoka, and from two officers of Skywalker's staff. They agree in outline. Skywalker came out of Murkhana convinced that he was not safe to be near. Not that he had erred or that he had been set up, though he believed that too. He believed that he was, as a standing fact about himself, deadly to whatever he was closest to. After a two-year inconclusive investigation, he was discharged and he did not go home.

## Withdrawal, 8 BBY–0 ABY

Ahsoka Tano had lived quietly in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] under a name she did not advertise since 8 BBY, tired of the uselessness of Low Republic politics. [[Rex]] lived with her for three years. As he neared his biological fifties, his health began to decline from many badly treated wartime wounds. Ahsoka took care of him, until he passed away in his sleep in 5 BBY. In the sixth month of 0 ABY she left the place she had been living and has not been found since. What is known is thin and none of it is confirmed.

## In 2 ABY

She is following [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]] and she is behind him. She was on Vandor four weeks after he was and on [[Ryloth]] nine days after he was. Both were confirmed by people who recognised her and had no reason to invent it. She has not caught him. Nobody catches him. She is trying to intercept the one man in the galaxy who does not decide where he is going. She has also been to the edge of [[Dathomir|Dathomiri]] space at least twice. A ship matching no registry breached the [[The Nightsisters|Nightsister]] territory and stayed there for five hours in the ninth month of 1 ABY and then left. Nobody came out to see it. [[Asajj Ventress|Ventress]]'s people have said nothing about the matter.

There is no evidence that she has actually spoken to Ventress, to [[Merrin]] or to [[Barriss Offee]]. There is no evidence that she has reached Skywalker either. Nobody in the Republic knows what she is attempting. Investigator [[Eogun Vidoc|Vidoc]] has her filed as someone he would like to talk to, but doesn't yet have a reason to track her down.
