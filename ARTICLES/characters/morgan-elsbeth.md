**Morgan Elsbeth**, a human of [[Dathomir]] who lived through its massacre, is one of the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]]. Since 1 ABY she has been an informant for [[Republic Intelligence]].

## Dathomir, 21–3 BBY

Morgan Elsbeth is Dathomiri. She survived the [[Dathomir#The massacre|massacre of 21 BBY]] and came back in 9 BBY for the rebuilding. She joined the Nightborn for Dathomir and not for [[Maul]]. For six years she made sure that [[Asajj Ventress|Ventress]] could see what Maul was using her for, and [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire|Nightspire]] followed.

## In 2 ABY

She has fed Republic Intelligence since 1 ABY. She wants the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] gone and [[The Nightsisters|Nightsisters]] left alone.
