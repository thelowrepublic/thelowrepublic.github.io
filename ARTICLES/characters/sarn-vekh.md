**Sarn Vekh** is a Pyke, at the head of the Shadow Pykes: about a fifth of the Pyke Syndicate, which stayed loyal to [[Maul]]. For nineteen years he built the Syndicate's spice routes into the poorest systems of the Rim. His work for the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] now is taking those routes apart.

## The Pyke Syndicate

*Main article: [[The Hutt Clans#The Pykes]]*

Sarn Vekh ran spice distribution into the poorest systems of the Rim for nineteen years and was made a director for it. In 4 BBY the Collective ordered the Syndicate out of those systems entirely. [[Nadi Orl]] had decided that an addicted sector cannot be recruited from or invested in, and both of those options were more profitable in the long run. The Pyke Syndicate had been selling itself to the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] and the Collective at once since 8 BBY. Orl's order is what broke this game and resulted in two years of conflict. At the end of it in 2 BBY, the main Syndicate body went to the Hutts. Vekh took about a fifth of it and stayed loyal to Maul as the Shadow Pykes.

## In 2 ABY

The Syndicate calls the Shadow Pykes cast-offs. The Collective calls them turncoats and has never stopped watching them. Their work now is dismantling the routes Vekh spent nineteen years building. They do it system by system, against men they used to work with.
