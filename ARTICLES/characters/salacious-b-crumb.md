**Salacious B. Crumb** is a Kowakian monkey-lizard and the court jester of Jabba the Hutt, of the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutt Clans]]. His employer, his post and his duties are exactly what they were in 19 BBY. He is the most stable institution in the Outer Rim.
