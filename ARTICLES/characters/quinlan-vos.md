**Quinlan Vos** is a Kiffar Jedi Master who can read an object's past by touching it. In 1 ABY the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] embedded him inside the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]].

## Background

During the Clone Wars, Quinlan Vos and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were sent to recapture the Hutt crime lord Ziro, who had been broken out of prison on [[Coruscant]]. On Nal Hutta, Vos picked up a glass Ziro had used and sensed his memories. The trail led them to Teth, where they found Ziro dead and failed to arrest the bounty hunter Cad Bane.

## Dooku, 21 BBY

Vos went undercover against [[The Long War|Dooku]] in 21 BBY and did not come back the same man.

## Inside the Collective, 1 ABY

Vos reads the history of objects by touch, which is why the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] chose him to infiltrate the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] in 1 ABY and find out about the [[The Shadow War#What each side fields|holocrons]] they unearthed.

[[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] field operations are coordinated out of proportion by Nightborn, who know Republic doctrine, Jedi tactics, and how to stay hidden from people they often studied beside. Master Quinlan Vos was embedded inside the organisation in 1 ABY by the Templars, who did not inform [[Ossus Order|Ossus]]. He has sent information out at irregular intervals ever since. He has sent more information to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] privately. Nobody has reached a settled view if Vos is still their asset. His well-known propensity to disregard Jedi creed is troublesome, but it has given credence to his alibi as a defector. His problematic attachment to [[Asajj Ventress]], and over a dozen unsuccessful attempts to contact her, make his real loyalty and priorities an open question.

## In 2 ABY

Vos saw [[Asajj Ventress|Ventress]] die and is determined to see her alive again.

In the eastern highlands of [[Dathomir]], Asajj Ventress holds the [[The Nightsisters|old rite]], attempts a second shot at a peaceful life and diligently avoids contact with Quinlan Vos for reasons only she knows.
