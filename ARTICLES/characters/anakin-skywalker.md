**Anakin Skywalker** arrived in the Supreme Chancellor's office in 19 BBY after [[Mace Windu]] had disarmed [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]], and did not intervene. He left the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] in 17 BBY and, as a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], destroyed a city at [[Murkhana]] in 16 BBY. Discharged in 14 BBY, he did not go home to his wife, [[Padmé Amidala]], or their twins, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker|Leia]], and he has since gone where he is pulled, without plan or explanation. At [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] in 0 ABY, six hundred of the sixteen hundred survivors escaped because of him.

## Background

Anakin Skywalker was born on Tatooine, the son of Shmi Skywalker, a slave. The Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn found him there in 32 BBY, and after the Battle of [[Naboo]] he became the Padawan of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. In 22 BBY he secretly married [[Padmé Amidala]], the senator of Naboo, although the Jedi Code forbade it. In the Clone Wars he was a Jedi General, commanding the clone troopers of the 501st Legion, and he trained a Padawan of his own, [[Ahsoka Tano]].

## Palpatine's death, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine]]*

Four Jedi entered the Supreme Chancellor's private office to arrest him on suspicion of being a Sith Lord. Agen Kolar, Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were dead within nine seconds. [[Mace Windu]] duelled Palpatine alone, disarmed him, and forced him against the window.

Anakin Skywalker arrived. He did not intervene. Windu struck.

Skywalker gave his account many times over the following decades and never changed it. He described a failure to decide in time, not any noble conviction. Palpatine had promised him knowledge that would prevent his wife's death in childbirth. That promise was extinguished in front of him. [[Padmé Amidala]] survived the birth of twins four months later. Whether the danger or the promise to avoid it had ever been genuine was never established, but Skywalker did not stop considering the question.

Skywalker's testimony destroyed Palpatine's reputation within a week. It did not dismantle what Palpatine had built, because Skywalker did not know its full shape.

He could establish that Palpatine was Darth Sidious, that [[The Long War|Dooku]] had been his apprentice, and that the Separatist crisis had been steered from both ends. He knew nothing else. Not the [[The Kamino Findings#The contingency orders|Kaminoan contingency orders]]. Not the Sifo-Dyas commission. Not the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The shrine, 18 BBY|Sith shrine]] beneath the Jedi Temple. Not the name of a single one of Palpatine's people outside the Chancellery. Those were found later by [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|accountants]], and the finding of them shaped the age more than the killing had.

## Leaving the Order, 19–17 BBY

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY]]*

The Skywalker marriage became public in 19 BBY, [[Padmé Amidala|Amidala]] having been visibly pregnant all through her husband's testimony. The Council refused to make an exception. He left the Order in 17 BBY. He was then immediately re-hired as a civilian and made general in the new [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]]. He was still one of the most effective and talented commanders in the Core and was more than willing to continue the fight.

## Murkhana

As a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], not a Jedi or a Chosen One, Anakin destroyed a city. Intelligence said that there was a buried droid command node under it and that the population was complicit. The node was there. Whether the city's population knew about it is still debated to this day. About two hundred thousand civilians died and the campaign in that sector ended four months early. The intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. He said so at the inquiry. He said someone wanted him gone, but he could not prove it, and he sounded exactly like a man making excuses.

Skywalker's and [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s war theatres overlapped for three years and that was enough to sign the former Jedi's sentence. Skywalker fought to finish the war and Tarkin's position depended on the war continuing. The most effective commander in the Republic was in the wrong theatre, moving too fast, closing campaigns that had been budgeted for years. Of course, nothing has ever been proved. Outland Command has no surviving record of sending the reports. The inquiry reached no conclusion. His wife's enemies wanted a conviction and her allies wanted it buried, and the compromise between them finished both of their public careers. He was discharged with honour in 14 BBY.

## What it did to him

The inquiry treated [[Murkhana]] as a question of fact and of who had authorised what. It never asked the other question, which is what the thing did to the man who did it. The accounts that matter were never entered into any record, because nobody was collecting them. They come from [[Padmé Amidala]], from [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] before the estrangement, from [[Ahsoka Tano|Ahsoka]], and from two officers of his staff. They agree in outline. He came out of Murkhana convinced that he was not safe to be near. Not that he had erred or that he had been set up, though he believed that too. He believed that he was, as a standing fact about himself, deadly to whatever he was closest to. After a two-year inconclusive investigation, he was discharged and he did not go home.

The separation from Padmé is usually described as a political arrangement, and it partly was. Her career could not survive standing next to him, but the decision was substantially his. The reason he gave was that a man who had killed two hundred thousand people on a briefing he wasn't wise enough to double check had no business raising two children. Padmé did not agree, and has never agreed. The record of that argument survives in [[C-3PO]]'s testimony. The family was never estranged in the ordinary sense. They love him and he loves them, and he has decided that this is not the relevant consideration. He has visited the twins, but not often and never for long. By the end of 2 ABY he had not visited them for seven years.

## The prophecy

The other thing he did not recover from was the prophecy. He had been named the Chosen One and told he would bring balance to the Force. In 19 BBY the Sith were destroyed and he was not the one who destroyed them. He had stood in the room and failed to move. What that produced, over years, was not despair. It was something considerably worse. He concluded that the prophecy had not failed. It was still running. His part in it had not yet been handed to him. His task was therefore to stop deciding anything and let himself be moved.

He has done this for sixteen years. He does not plan. He does not accept commissions. He goes where he is pulled and does not explain, because he cannot. A [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Republic ranger]] on Vandor asked him in 4 BBY what he was doing there. He answered that he did not know, because it was not his choice to be there. The luxury of reason is reserved for those who choose.

## The record

He is clean, well-groomed and dressed in Jedi robes, but he is alone, and by every description given of him he is fundamentally unwell. He talks to people who are not present. Local authorities on eleven worlds have detained him and all eleven let him go. In most cases nobody could think what to charge him with. In three cases they were afraid of him. He is also entirely coherent, and this is the part that unsettles people who have met him. Everyone that knows him says he still has the same snark, humour and brashness. His reasoning is intact. His conclusions are frequently correct. He simply works from premises nobody else holds and will not argue about them. And then there is the record.

[[Dedra Meero|An analyst]] at the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Office of the Inquiry]] compiled his confirmed appearances in 1 ABY. There were forty-one of them across fifteen years, and she set them against significant events. The paper was not published and the analyst was reassigned. Skywalker reached [[Ord Mantell]] eleven days before the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] moved on the shipyards. He was in the building when the [[Coruscant]] procurement fraud broke in 6 BBY, and he was the reason the auditor got to a magistrate alive. He walked into the Cato Neimoidia hostage situation in 3 BBY with no indication that he knew there was one. He is as disruptive to the Republic as he is to the Collective, and the pattern shows no sign of caring about the difference. Four of the forty-one appearances ended the careers of Republic officials. All four of those officials were later shown to have been taking Collective money. Nobody has ever established what Anakin thought he was doing.

During the years of wandering he also wrote a twenty-page literary dissertation about the tale of Darth Plagueis the Wise, published under a pseudonym. The text has been well regarded in literary circles. The [[Ossus Order|Ossus academy]] and the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] both banned it from their curriculums. They knew who wrote it.

## The lightning

He has dreamed of [[The Death of Palpatine#The Chancellor's office, 19 BBY|Palpatine's death]] for twenty years. On the rare occasions that he described it, he said it was the same few minutes, repeating. Somewhere in that repetition he learned to do it. The first confirmed use of Force lightning by Skywalker was on Nar Shaddaa in 5 BBY, against [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] enforcers. He has done it many times since. The Jedi who have seen it do not dispute the identification. [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] have witnessed it since and also do not know what to make of it. Nobody can explain why it has done nothing to him.

Work of that kind leaves marks. It marked Palpatine, visibly, and the Sith before him. It marks the Nightborn, as they tap into the dark side, so readily available for them. Skywalker has been using the dark side freely for seven years, without technique and without restraint, and he is not corrupted by any measure anyone can apply to him. He is exactly as ruined as he was in 14 BBY and no further. The [[The Jedi Templars|Templar]] position is that this is not possible and the reports must be mistaken. The [[Ossus Order|Ossus]] position is that Skywalker is unprecedented and they will not cast judgement carelessly. An intermediary put the question to [[Barriss Offee]] in 1 ABY. She is reported to have called both of those answers comfortable. There is a third, she said. He may be entirely right about the prophecy, and that possibility should frighten people a great deal more than it does.

## Yavin, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

He arrived at [[Yavin 4]] the day before the Republic fleet. Nobody sent him. Nobody knew he was coming. He had been seen four weeks earlier on Umbara and had given no indication of any destination. He fought at the temple complex on the second day. Most of the soldiers around him did not know who he was. Some officers did, and could do nothing about it. On the third night, when the ground force came apart, he was on the eastern approach.

### What the survivors describe

The Sith nexus did not confine itself to Force-users. This is the single most important thing in the Yavin record and the Republic has never known what to do with it. Eleven thousand four hundred ordinary soldiers were on that moon. Every survivor was tested afterwards, and none showed any measurable Force sensitivity, nothing that could be trained. From the third night onward almost all of them felt it. The choking terror of the dark side.

The descriptions converge on drowning. Weight on the chest. Cold in the mouth. A pressure that came from underneath and rotted their minds. Men who had never in their lives had an unusual experience reported the same one within an hour of each other, across forty kilometres of jungle. A hundred and ninety survivors have since been diagnosed with psychiatric conditions the Republic medical service has no scientific name for.

And in the middle of all the horrors, they saw Him. Two hundred and sixty of them describe it the same way and none of them can say what they mean by it. Skywalker did not glow, and nobody claims that he did. What they say is that they knew where he was, at any distance, even through rock and rubble. The closer they got to him the more the pressure eased. Following him was the only navigation anyone had for three days. He walked them to the shuttles. Then he went back and did it again. The Jedi accounts are the ones that unsettle the Order.

Five Jedi survived. All five have said, separately and in almost the same words, that what they felt was not a Jedi. A Jedi in the Force is a light. It pushes at the dark and the dark pushes back. You can feel the effort of it. You can feel it costing something. You can feel when the light is snuffed out.

Skywalker was not pushing at anything. What they describe is emptiness. Not absence in the sense of a hole in a wall, but a space so completely serene that the darkness could not reach into it. It had no edges to press on and nothing inside to work against. He was not winning, because he was not even fighting. He was simply a space the Sith nexus could not reach, and as he moved, the people followed him out. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] has been asked about this eleven times and has answered the same. He said he had known Anakin Skywalker for thirty-two years and had never felt anything remotely like it. In private conversations that were never recorded, he said one thing: that whatever Anakin was supposed to become, everyone has just witnessed it.

### The rest of it

Obi-Wan Kenobi held the northern landing zone for nineteen hours and gave the orders that had to be given there. He was fifty-seven at the time.

On the sixth day [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most Nightborn left the moon hours earlier. [[Maul]] was already gone by day four.

Anakin got people out constantly, seemingly without partaking in any food or sleep himself. He was still getting people out on the sixth day, inside the fire zone, after the shelling order had been given and while it was being carried out. He stopped a bombardment volley mid-flight and held it still for thirty minutes.

Of the sixteen hundred who came off that moon, six hundred escaped because of him. What the Republic will never publicly admit is that he helped the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] as well. Around a hundred of them were saved, civilians and security who were only meant to protect the dig. It is the one fact out of Yavin that never reached the public. Senator [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] does not know it. If she did, the Republic would have no case left at all. Anakin met Kenobi on the third day. They spoke for about a minute and hugged. Neither has ever said what they talked about. [[Luke Skywalker]] was in the northern landing zone, and did not find his father there.

## In 2 ABY

[[R2-D2]] stayed with Anakin Skywalker through the Long War, [[Murkhana]], the discharge, the withdrawal and the years since. He is the only thing that has stayed with Skywalker for all sixteen years.

[[Padmé Amidala]] has never accepted her husband's reasoning about himself and has never stopped telling him so on the rare occasions she can. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] testified on Skywalker at the Murkhana inquiry. They have spoken perhaps six times since, most recently for about a minute on the third day at [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]].

[[Ahsoka Tano]] is following Skywalker and she is behind him. She was on Vandor four weeks after he was and on [[Ryloth]] nine days after he was. Both were confirmed by people who recognised her and had no reason to invent it. She has not caught him. Nobody catches him. She is trying to intercept the one man in the galaxy who does not decide where he is going. There is no evidence that she has reached Skywalker. Nobody in the Republic knows what she is attempting.

The [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] appeared in 1 ABY, about eight months after the last survivors came home. Their belief is straightforward: the prophecy is real. Skywalker is the Chosen One. Nobody has been able to ask him what he thinks of the Path. Two Walkers have been recorded saying that he does not need to approve of them. Approval, they said, is a thing that people who choose require.
