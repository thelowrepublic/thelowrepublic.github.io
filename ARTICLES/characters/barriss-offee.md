**Barriss Offee** teaches the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]]'s Jedi defectors and wrote its doctrine. She bombed the Jedi Temple in 19 BBY, served ten years, and was recruited by [[Maul]] the same month she was released. She has never taken a Sith or Jedi title.

## Background

Barriss Offee was a Mirialan Jedi, the Padawan of Jedi Master [[Luminara Unduli]], and fought at [[Geonosis]] in 22 BBY. In the Clone Wars she went back to Geonosis with Unduli and with [[Ahsoka Tano]], with whom she had become friends. She grew disillusioned with the Jedi's part in the war. She bombed the Jedi Temple and let Tano be framed for it. [[Anakin Skywalker]] caught her, and she confessed just before Tano was convicted.

## Trial and release, 19–9 BBY

Offee had bombed the Jedi Temple in 19 BBY and was tried for it. At her trial she argued that the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] had become an army and had stopped listening to the Force. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation of 16 BBY]] proved her right, and her life sentence was successfully appealed to a decade. She was released in 9 BBY, and within a month, [[Maul]] managed to contact her and sway her to his side. Apparently they talked over some tea. 

Barriss has not carried a lightsaber since her imprisonment and refuses to be called a Sith or a Jedi. By every account given from people around her, she genuinely believes in the Nightborn values. She is the reason the order has discipline, method and a curriculum.

## The Nightborn

*Main article: [[The Nightborn]]*

Jedi training, meditation, the lightsaber forms and a working knowledge of how the Jedi actually operate were brought in by defectors and taught by Barriss Offee.

She is the instructor of the Nightborn's Jedi defectors and author of its doctrine. She teaches all of what she knows to any Nightborn adept that asks, and personally oversees the care of all Force-sensitive children that are sent to [[Dathomir]] by the Collective.

## Luminara Unduli

[[Luminara Unduli]] trained Barriss Offee, she did not see what was forming in her student, and she has said publicly that she should have. Every Force-sensitive the Nightborn have taken in was taught by Barriss from what Unduli taught her first. A journalist asked her in 1 ABY whether the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] existed to fight the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] or to fight her former student. She said both, and that she could see no useful distinction.

## In 2 ABY

Barriss has made three public statements in the last two years. All of them addressed to the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] and all of them arguments rather than threats.

[[Leia Skywalker]] has received three private invitations from Barriss Offee, the first in 1 ABY. She has declined all three and reported all three to the SIS, which is the only reason anyone knows about them.

[[Republic Intelligence]] considers her the most influential person in the organisation. The [[Ossus Order|Ossus Council]] has met with her in secret several times, but the subjects of those meetings are unknown.
