**Vesk Tolloran** is a Quarren film-maker who directs the Agent 0006 holodramas and stars in them. They are the most lavish productions in the galaxy, and the latest, Moonraider, turned [[The Battle of Yavin|the Battle of Yavin]] into a blockbuster that earned billions of credits and was condemned almost everywhere it played. He has moved in [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s circle for twenty years.

## Agent 0006

Agent 0006, "triple-oh-six" to his audiences, is a gentleman spy with perfect manners and very few scruples. Tolloran has played him in all eleven films. The formula has never changed. Each film drops the agent into a real event of recent history, gets nearly everything about it wrong, and ends with the galaxy unaware that one man in a well-cut jacket saved it.

The agent's real name has never been given. He takes a new alias in every film and introduces himself with it. Audiences wait for the line, and guessing the next alias has become a pastime on both sides of the Union's border. Critics on [[Coruscant]] call the films loud, long and historically illiterate. Audiences keep paying to see them, in the Core as much as in the Rim. For most of the series the agent is an officer of the Strategic Information Service, better known as [[Republic Intelligence]].

## The films, 17 BBY–1 ABY

The best known of the earlier films:
- You Only Clone Twice (17 BBY) sends the agent to [[Kamino]] as Jaxx Corvane, and he uncovers the clones' chips with the help of a Kaminoan defector. The real [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|audit]] was carried out by accountants, none of whom appear in the film.
- From Raxus with Love (14 BBY) makes him Baron Delvane Morr, who seduces a Separatist delegate and rescues the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord of Raxus]] from a Coruscant senator who profits from the war going on.
- Quantum of Bacta (7 BBY) has him, as Glup Shitto, force a pension rise for [[Clones|clone]] veterans through the Senate, a year before the real correction passed. The villains are Coruscant clerks who have worked out what clone ageing saves the treasury.
- Live and Let Droid (5 BBY) is built around the [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers|droid papers]]. As Kade Varrow, the agent smuggles a self-aware battle droid out of a Republic scrapyard, against the orders of his own service.

## Moonraider, 2 ABY

Moonraider is set on [[Yavin 4]], and this time the agent is Galen Starkiller. He goes in with the Republic force and discovers that the bad briefing was Coruscant's doing. He fights his way out beside [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] who is wise, grieving and right about everything. The orbital shelling is the film's moral climax: Tarkin gives the order in tears, to save a galaxy that will never thank him. In the final scene Starkiller hands his service credentials to a Union officer and defects.

Nine thousand eight hundred Republic soldiers died at Yavin, less than two years before the film opened. The raised dead are played for cheap jumpscares and an overly long chase sequence. The film gives the agent the walk to the shuttles that the survivors remember making behind [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Veterans' associations called the film obscene, and several sector governments banned it from their holotheatres. It earned billions of credits anyway, a good share of them on Coruscant.

## Patrons

Tolloran was making cheap holodramas for the Rim circuits in 18 BBY when [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s people first took an interest in him. Nobody has established who paid for his first large production. Since 1 ABY the Union's Ministry of Culture has donated generous funding to every film, and its books list him as a grant recipient and his movies are entered into the Union's cultural heritage list. He visits the Ministry so often it might as well be his home. Its licensing office, which passes every holodrama, song and news broadcast made in the Union, approved Moonraider in a day. The news that reached Seswenna through legal channels after Yavin passed through the same office.

Watched in order, the films visibly drift in messaging. The early agent serves the Republic and the Republic deserves him. By the middle of the series his enemies are Republic clerks and senators, and the people he saves are from the Rim. In Live and Let Droid he defies his own service for the first time. In Moonraider he leaves it. Each film is a little more right about the Republic's failings than the last, even if the other ninety-percent of presented events are complete bullshit. Whether the films have followed Seswenna's mood or led it is not a question anyone in Seswenna asks aloud.

## In 2 ABY

Tolloran is shooting a twelfth film, the first in which Agent 0006 works for the Union, even as his award tour for Moonraider is still ongoing.
