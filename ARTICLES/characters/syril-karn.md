**Syril Karn** is the Sector Ranger Commissioner of [[Ferrix]]. A former deputy inspector of corporate security on Morlana One, he hunted [[Cassian Andor]] to Ferrix against orders and found [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] running its corporate offices. His report brought the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]] to Ferrix, and with it the [[Ferrix#Ferrix Massacre, 2 ABY|Ferrix Massacre]]. Nobody on Ferrix knows it was him.

## Background

Syril Karn was born on [[Coruscant]] around 40 BBY, to a strict mother, Eedy Karn, and a father who left the family. Around 10 BBY he left home, against his mother's wishes, to join the security forces of Preox-Morlana, known as Pre-Mor, the corporate conglomerate that governs the Morlani system. He moved to its security headquarters on Morlana One. He took his duties more seriously than most of his colleagues and rose to deputy inspector.

## Morlana One, 1–2 ABY

In the last months of 1 ABY, [[Cassian Andor]] came to Morlana One on [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] business. Two Pre-Mor security officers confronted him outside a brothel in the Leisure Zone, and he killed them both.

Karn's superior, Chief Inspector Hyne, wanted an accident report saying the two had died heroically, and the matter closed. Karn would not let it go. He traced the ship that had left Morlana One that night and found a witness at the brothel, and the trail led him to [[Ferrix]], a world under Pre-Mor's oversight. Hyne ordered him to drop the case. Karn kept digging, and found something larger than one killer. [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] had taken over Ferrix's corporate offices from within, and it was paying Karn's own supervisors on Morlana One to look the other way.

## The report, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[Ferrix#Ferrix Massacre, 2 ABY]]*

Karn could not report Crimson Dawn to his own superiors, so he went over their heads, to the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]. The Republic had been ignoring Ferrix for years. Cassian Andor had spent four years asking it for help and received instructional videos, and Crimson Dawn had been running the planet for at least three years since. This time, though, [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] had just seceded, and the Republic suddenly needed a foothold on the Hydian Way. It took the report, dug up Ferrix's old requests for aid as its reason, and sent the [[The Republic Corps|Corps]].

Karn went to Ferrix himself, without leave, ahead of the Corps, and he was in the middle of it when it happened. More than seven hundred civilians with no provable connection to the Collective were killed in what became known as the Ferrix Massacre.

## Commissioner of Ferrix

His superiors on Morlana One could not punish him for going to Ferrix without leave, not without explaining why the case should have been dropped, and that meant admitting to the bribes. Instead, a few days after the massacre, they transferred him to Ferrix permanently, to coordinate its local security. It was meant as a punishment.

The orders came just before the [[The Republic Corps|Corps]] left. After the massacre it did the only thing left that could save face. It withdrew, handed Ferrix to its local security forces and deputised them as [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Sector Rangers]]. Karn reported to his new post and found that it made him a [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers#Commissioners|Commissioner]], though nobody had chosen him. Commissioners are usually Ministry of Security officials, and now and then a local. Karn is neither. The Republic needs Ferrix under its control, so his office is generously funded.

## In 2 ABY

Karn is trying to keep order on a world that despises him. To the people of [[Ferrix]] he is a man wearing the badge of the Republic, whose soldiers killed their neighbours. They do not know that it was his report that brought the Corps to Ferrix. Only Karn and the Republic clerk who took it know. If Ferrix ever found out, its people would tear him apart limb from limb.
