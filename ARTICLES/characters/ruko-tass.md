**Ruko Tass** is a Gungan [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] and a field commander of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], who left [[Naboo]] at fifteen.

## Naboo

Ruko Tass left [[Naboo]] at fifteen, a world that had produced [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|a Sith Lord]] and [[Jar Jar Binks|a galactic clown]], a fact which Tass was always reminded of.

## In 2 ABY

Tass is a Nightborn and a field commander of the Collective. He is a Sith in everything but the title. He has bled his own [[The Nightborn#Magick|kyber crystal]] through the old Sith ways, mastered force lightning and trained his body until he could wrestle a Wookiee into submission through brute strength alone. [[Nyra Sull]] spares padawans. Tass has spared nobody.
