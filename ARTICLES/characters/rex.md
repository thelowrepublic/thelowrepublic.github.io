**Rex**, designated CT-7567, was a clone captain. With [[Ahsoka Tano]] he argued the [[The Manumission Act|Manumission Act]] through the Senate, and the two became the public face of manumission. He died in his sleep in 5 BBY, in Tano's care.

## Background

Rex was a clone captain of the 501st Legion, which served under [[Anakin Skywalker]]. He fought beside Skywalker's Padawan, [[Ahsoka Tano]], through most of the Clone Wars. The trooper [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|Fives]] served under him.

## Manumission

Rex's chip was removed in 18 BBY. The [[The Manumission Act|Manumission Act]] of 16 BBY made every clone a citizen. [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Captain Rex argued it through the Senate, giving more than four hundred hours of testimony between them. He testified at every inquiry that would have him and became the public face of manumission alongside Tano.

## With Tano, 8–5 BBY

Tano withdrew in 8 BBY to take care of Rex in a remote location. He was physically forty-eight by 8 BBY. Rex lived with her for three years. As he neared his biological fifties, his health began to decline from many badly treated wartime wounds.

The [[The Manumission Act#The abandonment|pension correction]] Tano had spent seven years arguing for passed in 6 BBY, by which point most of the men it was for were dead. Rex refused [[Clones#Ko Sai's procedure|Ko Sai's life extension procedure]] until the end. Ahsoka took care of him, until he passed away in his sleep in 5 BBY.

## In 2 ABY

A Republic naval academy was named after him.
