**Luke Skywalker**, the son of [[Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], is a Jedi of rare talent, taught by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Ossus]], who came through [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] alive. The record says he went rogue in 1 ABY; in truth Kenobi set him on [[Maul]]'s trail.

## Childhood

Luke Skywalker was born in 19 BBY to [[Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], with his twin, [[Leia Skywalker|Leia]]. His childhood was a relatively happy one, because his parents took care to keep their children out of their politics. After [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] his father visited the twins and their mother but did not live with them. 

When the twins were young, Anakin took them to the planet Ilum. It was the only trip he ever took them on. Luke built a lightsaber there with his sister. Together they found a fused "twin kyber crystal" and split it, and each part went into one of the siblings' lightsabers. 

Luke was sent to [[Ossus]] at twelve, where his Force sensitivity proved off the charts, like his father's. His own visits home were infrequent, and he did not resent his father's absence as much as he might have.

## Ossus and Yavin

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

Luke Skywalker is a Force prodigy, trained at [[Ossus]] under [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] from twelve. He was one of five Jedi to survive [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]].

[[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] met [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] on the third day. They spoke for about a minute and hugged. Neither has ever said what they talked about. Luke Skywalker was in the northern landing zone, and did not find his father there.

## Rogue, 1 ABY

Luke left Kenobi's command in 1 ABY and is listed as going rogue. In reality, Kenobi sent him. The [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] holds Kenobi in place, and [[Maul]] can only be effectively pursued by a free agent. Maul needs to be stopped by whatever means it takes, and Kenobi trusts Luke to know which means are the right and wrong ones.

## In 2 ABY

Luke Skywalker went rogue and is currently on [[Maul]]'s trail.

He travels the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] in the simple clothes of a moisture farmer and keeps as low a profile as he can. It comes easily to him, because he is affable and can play dumb when he needs to. He is constantly side-tracked, because he always finds someone in need of help, and he never refuses.
