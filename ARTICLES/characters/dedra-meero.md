**Dedra Meero** works for [[Republic Intelligence]]. Her study of [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s appearances, made at the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Office of the Inquiry]], was suppressed, and [[Wullf Yularen]] recruited her on the strength of it.

## Republic Intelligence

Analyst Dedra Meero, at that time employed at the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Office of the Inquiry]], meticulously tracked and compiled [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]]'s forty-one appearances in 1 ABY, and presented conclusions about their patterns and effects. The paper was suppressed and she was reassigned to a licensing desk. [[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] read her report anyway and had her recruited into [[Republic Intelligence]] within the year.

## In 2 ABY

Dedra is thorough, literal and without the ability to quit. [[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] has said she is the only one who truly reads every data point she is given. Her current file is on the price of kalkite, which has tripled since 1 ABY. Nobody asked her to look into it, but she still does.
