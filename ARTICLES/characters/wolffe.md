**Wolffe**, designated CC-3636, was a clone commander who served under General [[Plo Koon]] in the 104th. He now recruits for [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]], and more than half the clone veterans in the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] were brought in by him, one way or another.

## Background

Wolffe commanded the Wolfpack in the 104th Battalion, under [[Plo Koon]]. The Separatist warship Malevolence destroyed Plo Koon's fleet, and Wolffe was one of the few survivors. [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] rescued him along with Plo Koon.

## Discharge and Crimson Dawn

Wolffe's chip was removed in 18 BBY, and he was discharged in 12 BBY at a physical age of about forty. No civilian employer would take him. [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] did, in 7 BBY. He took the life-extension procedure and became a recruiter.

The [[The Shadow Troopers|shadow troopers]] were among the first clones to go to [[Maul]], long before Wolffe began recruiting.

## In 2 ABY

Over half the clone veterans on Collective payroll came in, directly or indirectly, through Wolffe. He arrives with a contract, a medical plan, a life extension treatment and a rank. And he is a fellow clone. He has never claimed the Collective is clean, but he says it is the only side that kept its promises to his brothers.
