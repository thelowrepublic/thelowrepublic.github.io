**Wilhuff Tarkin** heads the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]] as its Governor-General, and no court has ever convicted him of anything. Once [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]]'s protégé and a naval officer, he rose through the [[The Long War|Long War]] to hold the Outland Command, and in 17 BBY he made contact with [[Maul]], an arrangement that ended in 1 BBY. He governed the Seswenna sector from 10 BBY and commanded the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]'s operation against Maul at [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]]. Called to account for the bad briefing and the arrangement, he was shielded by Seswenna, which voted to leave the Republic in 1 ABY.

## Background

Tarkin was a human of [[Eriadu]]. During the Clone Wars he was a captain in the Republic Navy, commanding the flagship of the Jedi Master Even Piell. The Confederacy captured them both and held them in the Citadel, a prison on Lola Sayu, until a Jedi rescue team led by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] freed them. He told Skywalker that the Jedi Code kept the Jedi from going far enough to win the war. By 20 BBY he was an admiral.

## The ascent

Wilhuff Tarkin had been [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]]'s protégé but was not part of his conspiracy, having been a naval officer rather than a politician. He cooperated with the investigation immediately and completely, to the obvious ruin of several men who had thought of him as a friend. The investigators found him invaluable. It was the first of many occasions on which his usefulness outvalued anyone's willingness to look at him closely.

The [[The Long War|Long War]] was his ladder. By 17 BBY he held the Outland Command. It was a theatre posting in the Rim with unusually wide freedom over supply, over who answered to whom, and over what happened to captured Separatist property. He used it to build something no Republic officer had held in a thousand years: a personal following in the officer corps, and a group of sectors that looked to him before they looked to [[Coruscant]].

## The bargain

Tarkin made contact with [[Maul]] in 17 BBY. The earliest surviving record is a supply manifest from that year, and the arrangement was almost certainly older than the paperwork.

The deal was simple, as far as anyone could piece it together afterwards. Maul's people killed for Tarkin. Separatist field commanders, the bankers paying for them, and any Republic official standing in Tarkin's way. In exchange Tarkin gave Maul intelligence, safe passage, three sectors where nobody would come looking, and a share of captured Separatist wealth that never reached Coruscant.

What made it work was that none of the killings looked like what they were. Separatist commanders appeared to have been purged by their own side. The bankers appeared to have been dealt with by syndicates they owed money to. And the Republic officials appeared to be victims of Separatist terror. A magistrate on [[Ord Mantell]] died in 16 BBY while examining the Outland Command accounts. Two admirals who had asked about the same accounts died within the year. Senator Fang Zar of Sern Prime was shot in a transit tunnel in 15 BBY. He was due to give evidence the following week about where the captured Separatist property had gone.

Tarkin never gave an order anyone could follow back to him. He described a problem in front of the right person and let them reach their own conclusion. He seems to have understood from the start that what Maul wanted out of the arrangement was not money but leverage. He decided that was a price worth paying. And to a large extent, he was right.

## What it bought

By the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord of Raxus]], Tarkin was the most decorated commander in the Republic. He ran a group of Rim sectors in everything but name and had outlived every rival who had seriously got in his way. He had gathered nearly as much power as a victorious general might have taken by force. And he got all of it through channels that were, taken one at a time, entirely lawful.

The bargain ended in 1 BBY and neither man has moved against the other since. [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] and Seswenna sit at opposite ends of the Rim. Beyond that, each is useful to the other. Prosperous Rim sectors are the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s best argument that the Republic is not needed. A Republic coming apart is Seswenna's best argument that local authority is the best choice.

## Murkhana, 16 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

As a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], not a Jedi or a Chosen One, [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] destroyed a city. Intelligence said that there was a buried droid command node under it and that the population was complicit. The node was there. Whether the city's population knew about it is still debated to this day. About two hundred thousand civilians died and the campaign in that sector ended four months early. The intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. He said so at the inquiry. He said someone wanted him gone, but he could not prove it, and he sounded exactly like a man making excuses.

Skywalker's and Tarkin's war theatres overlapped for three years and that was enough to sign the former Jedi's sentence. Skywalker fought to finish the war and Tarkin's position depended on the war continuing. The most effective commander in the Republic was in the wrong theatre, moving too fast, closing campaigns that had been budgeted for years. Of course, nothing has ever been proved. Outland Command has no surviving record of sending the reports. The inquiry reached no conclusion.

## Seswenna, 10–1 BBY

*Main article: [[The Greater Seswenna Union#Seswenna]]*

Tarkin resigned his commission in 10 BBY, went home to [[Eriadu]], and was elected governor of the Seswenna sector. Over the following decade he drew the neighbouring frontier sectors into an efficient, prosperous and orderly bloc. Elections went on being held and stopped being competitive. He kept being re-elected term after term, through an overwhelming majority. Coruscant put up with the arrangement because Seswenna kept the shipping lanes safe, paid what it owed, and cleaned up its own droid remnants without asking for Republic troops. Director [[Wullf Yularen]] of the reformed Strategic Information Service, known to everyone as [[Republic Intelligence]] or SIS, opened a file on the Outland Command accounts in 11 BBY and did not close it for nine years.

## Yavin, 1 BBY–0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

[[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] closed his file on Tarkin in 2 BBY. In the first weeks of 1 BBY he laid the Outland Command evidence in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths. Yularen has been working with rogue Jedi Knight [[Do'Kass]] for the past decade, the Force-user's unconventional methods producing evidence that would otherwise not see the light of day. Tarkin understood his position at once. He did not deny the connection. He described it as wartime intelligence work, difficult compromises he was forced to make, and expressed regret that it had been necessary. Within four months he had become the loudest and most convincing voice in the Republic for destroying the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. He was even willing to fully fund and coordinate the manhunt for [[Maul]], as long as the Republic provided troops. He was the man who had seen the danger first, from the inside, and whose warnings had been ignored all this time.

It was an extraordinary performance for the investigators and it mostly worked. He was given command of the operation, and the whole of it would be recorded for Republic supervision. His gracious offer of funding and logistics meant it did not need formal authorisation from the Senate at all. Their hands would be clean, or so they thought.

The force went in badly briefed. What Tarkin knew about [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] capabilities was extensive, but he could not pass it on without further incriminating himself. So the briefing described a fortified criminal stronghold held by an army of [[Mandalore|Mandalorians]] and mercenaries. It did not describe a Sith nexus or the witches. The relevant pages of the intelligence file were held back by Tarkin's people on the grounds of protecting a source. So working off of this, the Republic sent a force of eleven thousand four hundred elite troopers, the best of what they had available and what they could spare. The landing on [[Yavin 4]] was unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day at minimal cost. On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify Dathomiri magick.

The sixteen hundred survivors give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the [[The Battle of Yavin#The Jedi who went|thirty-one Jedi]] were killed. All twenty-six were raised. [[Mace Windu]] was supposedly among them.

On the sixth day Tarkin ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most Nightborn left the moon hours earlier. Maul was already gone by day four.

## The secession, 1 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#Seswenna, 1 ABY]]*

The [[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing|bad briefing]] was established in the last months of 0 ABY. The investigators worked from the log of what had been withheld rather than from the missing pages themselves. The Outland Command's secret arrangement with [[Maul]] was published alongside it. Tarkin was summoned to answer for both, but Seswenna did not hand him over.

The sector had been run by Tarkin for eleven years. It was prosperous, orderly and safe. It had cleaned up its own droid remnants without Republic help. It had successfully pushed back against [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] and Hutt incursions. The people of Seswenna saw the Core's investigation as an attempt to tarnish the name of the man who had given them almost twenty years of stability. The Republic lost almost ten thousand soldiers due to its own incompetence and needed someone to blame. Most of the sector believed this sincerely, and all news that reached Eriadu through legal channels reinforced their stance. Seswenna's government voted to leave the Republic in the fourth month of 1 ABY. Seven neighbouring sectors supported it and voted for their own secessions as well, soon after forming a sector confederation, the independent [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]].

There was no clause in the charter for the Republic to point at and none to break, so there was also no real way to stop it. Tarkin had spent twenty years building the largest concentration of military strength outside the Core. The Republic was fighting a new kind of war, and mounting a retaliatory force against Seswenna would only make things worse. The Senate condemned the withdrawal, refused to recognise it, and did nothing else.

### The garrisons

*Main article: [[The Grand Union Army]]*

The [[The Republic Corps|Corps]] garrisons stationed in the eight sectors of the Greater Seswenna Union did not come home. There were about a hundred thousand commandos in them, which was much more than the sectors reasonably needed. Officers from Tarkin's old Outland Command had spent ten years arranging postings there, quietly accumulating military presence exactly where they wanted it to be. When the Union was declared, the soldiers were offered a universal one-step rank promotion and double the pay. Nobody could find a single person that rejected that offer. The Republic has never recognised the secession, so it has never listed those soldiers as deserters. In law they are still at their posts.

## In 2 ABY

Tarkin is now sixty-six. He holds the title of Governor-General of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]] and has never been convicted of anything by any court. He has given two long interviews about Yavin. In both of them he expressed deep regret but accepted no responsibility. By the end of 2 ABY, his public appearances have grown sparse.

After he resigned his commission and became governor, Tarkin underwent life extension procedures because of a popular referendum. He was already in his fifties, so they did not return him to his prime. They do mean that at sixty-six he is physically in his mid fifties and he will not be dying any time soon, despite the Republic's wishes.

Because Seswenna never left, in law, its eight sectors are still entitled to eight Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. Tarkin does not need the room anymore, though it is a weapon that is loaded for him to use.

[[Thrawn]] was found adrift in Wild Space by an Outland Command survey in 14 BBY. Recruited by Tarkin, he quickly became his protégé. He has commanded the Union's fleet since the secession and has not lost an engagement with the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]. [[Deran Kolt]] is First Marshal of the Greater Seswenna Union, commander of the [[The Grand Union Army|Grand Union Army]], and Tarkin's presumed successor. He has a better public image than Tarkin and holds the same views. Coruscant's quiet hope is that the Union falls apart when the old man dies. Kolt and Thrawn are the two reasons why it won't.
