**Obi-Wan Kenobi** has been a general of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] since 0 ABY, against his own argument, belonging to neither Jedi body. He was a Council member for eleven years and the Order's public voice all through the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|reckoning]], and he went to [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] against the Council's refusal. His commission is the most-quoted piece of evidence in the [[The Secessions#The Vess case|Vess case]].

## Background

Obi-Wan Kenobi was the Padawan of Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn. In 32 BBY, during the invasion of [[Naboo]], [[Maul]] killed Qui-Gon and Kenobi defeated Maul. Kenobi became a Jedi Knight and took [[Anakin Skywalker]] as his Padawan. In the Clone Wars he was a Jedi General and a member of the Jedi High Council, and he led the 212th Attack Battalion with Commander [[Cody]] as his second-in-command.

## Murkhana, 16 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

Kenobi testified on [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]] at the Murkhana inquiry. They have spoken perhaps six times since, most recently for about a minute on the third day at Yavin.

The inquiry treated Murkhana as a question of fact and of who had authorised what. It never asked the other question, which is what the thing did to the man who did it. The accounts that matter were never entered into any record, because nobody was collecting them. They come from [[Padmé Amidala]], from Kenobi before the estrangement, from [[Ahsoka Tano|Ahsoka]], and from two officers of Skywalker's staff. They agree in outline. Skywalker came out of Murkhana convinced that he was not safe to be near. Not that he had erred or that he had been set up, though he believed that too. He believed that he was, as a standing fact about himself, deadly to whatever he was closest to. After a two-year inconclusive investigation, he was discharged and he did not go home.

## Ossus, 10 BBY

Kenobi retired to [[Ossus]] in 10 BBY and taught. [[Luke Skywalker]], a Force prodigy, trained at Ossus under Kenobi from twelve.

## Yavin, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

The Council refused to be involved. The debate ran for four days and the refusal was not close. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] had abolished its own military rank sixteen years earlier and had spent eight years arguing about what it was for. It was not going to answer a Republic call-up. Thirty-one Jedi went anyway, as individuals, without permission of the Council.

Obi-Wan Kenobi went because he had thirty years of [[Maul]] behind him. He understood better than anyone alive what Maul would become with recovered Sith knowledge in his hands. [[Mace Windu]] went for reasons he did not give the Council but which are not hard to work out. He had acted [[The Death of Palpatine#The first weeks|once without permission]], in 19 BBY, and had been tried for two years and proved right. The Council disowned their action publicly on the day the fleet jumped. It did not expel them, but it has never taken the disavowal back.

The sixteen hundred survivors of the tragedy that was Yavin give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. Mace Windu was supposedly among them.

Obi-Wan Kenobi held the northern landing zone for nineteen hours and gave the orders that had to be given there. He was fifty-seven at the time.

[[Anakin Skywalker]], disgraced sixteen years ago, had arrived a day ahead of the fleet, uninvited and unannounced, and spent the last three days pulling people out of the collapse. On the sixth day [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] left the moon hours earlier. Maul was already gone by day four.

Anakin met Kenobi on the third day. They spoke for about a minute and hugged. Neither has ever said what they talked about. [[Luke Skywalker]] was in the northern landing zone, and did not find his father there.

## General of the Republic, 0 ABY

Obi-Wan Kenobi was made a general of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] in the eleventh week after Yavin, four weeks before the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] existed. The Republic wanted him for four reasons and was open about three of them. He was the senior surviving officer of the operation, a Jedi Master, and the only man alive who had the authority to describe what had happened on the ground. He had spent ten years as the Order's public voice in the Senate hearings, which meant that every household in the Core knew his face and had been trained by a decade of holonet transmissions to believe him. And he knew [[Maul]] better than anyone alive, having fought him multiple times across thirty years.

The fourth reason went unstated. Jedi defectors that joined the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] fought on [[Yavin 4]] and the images had been broadcast. The Republic needed a Jedi standing on its side of the line, visibly and in uniform, and it needed one before the public settled into the view that the Jedi and the Nightborn were the same thing in different colours. Kenobi was the best candidate anybody could name.

Thirty-one Jedi had gone to Yavin on a [[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing|briefing]] that had been deliberately gutted. Twenty-six of them died because nobody in the chain of command knew what a Sith nexus was, and because the officers who did know were protecting [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s reputation. If the Republic fought the rest of this war the same way, it would happen again, it would keep happening, and there was nobody else in any position to stop it.

He had held the northern landing zone for nineteen hours and given the orders that were given there. He has said once, and only once, that having done that he could not hand that burden to somebody else and go back to teaching. He understood the price and took it anyway. His appointment has been the strongest single piece of evidence in the [[The Secessions#The Vess case|Vess case]] since the day [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] stood up, and he knew it would be before he signed. He has asked to be released twice since.

## Luke Skywalker, 1 ABY

*Main article: [[Luke Skywalker]]*

[[Luke Skywalker]] left Kenobi's command in 1 ABY and is listed as going rogue. In reality, Kenobi sent him. The Republic holds Kenobi in place, and [[Maul]] can only be effectively pursued by a free agent. Maul needs to be stopped by whatever means it takes, and Kenobi trusts Luke to know which means are the right and wrong ones.

## In 2 ABY

Kenobi is not a [[The Jedi Templars|Templar]] and has refused to become one three times. He is not at [[Ossus Order|Ossus]], which regards his commission as precisely the error it left Coruscant to avoid. He holds a Republic rank. He commands Republic soldiers and a few dozen Jedi who followed him rather than either faction. He belongs to nothing.

Kenobi commands soldiers for a government he does not trust, but knows that someone has to do it.

The other difficulty for everyone in Jedi uniform is that they are fighting people they trained. [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] field operations are coordinated out of proportion by [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]], who know Republic doctrine, Jedi tactics, and how to stay hidden from people they often studied beside. Master [[Quinlan Vos]] was embedded inside the organisation in 1 ABY by the Templars, who did not inform Ossus. He has sent information out at irregular intervals ever since. He has sent more information to Kenobi privately. Nobody has reached a settled view if Vos is still their asset. His well-known propensity to disregard Jedi creed is troublesome, but it has given credence to his alibi as a defector. His problematic attachment to [[Asajj Ventress]], and over a dozen unsuccessful attempts to contact her, make his real loyalty and priorities an open question.

[[Cody]] serves on Kenobi's closest retinue, refusing any transfers or retirement. His loyalty is unwavering, as if he was atoning for a sin he never committed.
