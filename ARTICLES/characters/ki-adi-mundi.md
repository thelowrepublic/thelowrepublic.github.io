**Ki-Adi-Mundi** is one of the senior masters who came through the [[The Long War|Long War]]. In 0 ABY he went with [[Luminara Unduli]] to [[Coruscant]], and he sits on her council in the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]].

## Background

Ki-Adi-Mundi was a Cerean, and a member of the Jedi High Council in the years before the Clone Wars. In the Clone Wars he was a Jedi General. With [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Luminara Unduli]] he led the clone army that went back to [[Geonosis]] to destroy its droid foundries.

## The wars

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The survivors]]*

Because [[The Kamino Findings#The contingency orders|Order 66]] was never sent, the Order kept a generation of senior masters who would have otherwise died within an hour of one another. [[Plo Koon]], Ki-Adi-Mundi, [[Aayla Secura]], [[Shaak Ti]], [[Luminara Unduli]], [[Stass Allie]] and [[Ossus Order|Coleman Kcaj]] all came through the [[The Long War|Long War]]. The preserved knowledge and continuity of leadership made the Order's restructuring smoother than it could've otherwise been. None of it prevented what followed.

## The Templar offer, 0 ABY

Ki-Adi-Mundi and [[Stass Allie]] [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|went with]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and currently sit on her council. Both had been generals in the Clone Wars and neither had ever been reconciled to giving it up.

## In 2 ABY

Ki-Adi-Mundi and [[Stass Allie]] are both Templars, both on Unduli's council, both former generals who never made peace with the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation]] and have now reclaimed their former titles.
