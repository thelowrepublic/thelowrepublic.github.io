**Eogun Vidoc** is the chief criminal investigator of the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces]]' central office. Before that he was a bounty hunter for twenty-two years.

## Bounty hunter

Vidoc hunted bounties for twenty-two years first, and that is where he learned the law. He knows which actions need a warrant and which do not.

## The central office

The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers are run from the ground. Only one part of either force answers to Coruscant directly. It is a small office for crimes that cross sector lines, led by chief criminal investigator Eogun Vidoc.

He will do very nearly anything to the guilty and nothing at all to anyone else. He moves faster than the Ministry can authorise and has never broken a law in his life. The [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Office]] has audited him four times and found nothing. The [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s bounty on his head has seven digits.

## In 2 ABY

In the sixth month of 0 ABY [[Ahsoka Tano]] left the place she had been living and has not been found since. Nobody in the Republic knows what she is attempting. Investigator Vidoc has her filed as someone he would like to talk to, but doesn't yet have a reason to track her down.
