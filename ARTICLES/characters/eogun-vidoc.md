**Eogun Vidoc** is the chief criminal investigator of the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces]] and heads the Judicial Oversight Office, the central office that oversees them. Before that he was a bounty hunter for twenty-two years.

## Bounty hunter

Vidoc hunted bounties for twenty-two years first, and that is where he learned the law. He knows which actions need a warrant and which do not.

## The central office

The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers are run from the ground. The Judicial Forces work under the Judicial Department, which answers to the Ministry of Security and keeps a separate branch in every sector for local cases. Vidoc leads the Judicial Oversight Office, the small central office that oversees those branches and handles the crimes that cross sector lines. It works alongside the central Department but does not answer to it, only to the Ministry of Security. Technically, that makes him the head of all the Judicial Forces. The Department itself is not his, and is run by Republic bureaucrats.

He will do very nearly anything to the guilty and nothing at all to anyone else. He moves faster than the Ministry can authorise and has never broken a law in his life. The [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Office]] has audited him four times and found nothing. The [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s bounty on his head has seven digits.

## In 2 ABY

In the sixth month of 0 ABY [[Ahsoka Tano]] left the place she had been living and has not been found since. Nobody in the Republic knows what she is attempting. Investigator Vidoc has her filed as someone he would like to talk to, but doesn't yet have a reason to track her down.
