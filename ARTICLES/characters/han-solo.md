**Han Solo** is a Corellian pilot who flew for [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] for six years. In 1 BBY he flew four sealed loads out to the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]], and a year later learned from the Yavin reports whom he had been working for. He quit within the week, and Crimson Dawn has kept a standing price on him since.

## The Navy

Han Solo, Corellian, orphaned, enlisted in the new [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] Navy at eighteen and was thrown out fourteen months later for insubordination and stealing a patrol craft. He met the Wookiee Chewbacca, a [[Kashyyyk]] veteran of the Clone Wars, during the same incident.

## Crimson Dawn

He flew for [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] for six years under [[Qi'ra]], whom he had known before either of them worked for anyone. He was good at it, paid well, and did not ask what was in the sealed crates. In 1 BBY he ran four such loads out to the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]]. He found out who he had been smuggling for a year later, from the Yavin reports. He left the syndicate that week, and Crimson Dawn has had a standing price on him ever since.

## In 2 ABY

Han Solo no longer flies for either the Collective or the Republic, mostly flying for the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] nowadays.
