**Mon Mothma** was Chancellor of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] from 11 BBY to 0 ABY. She governed under the new charter from its start, and lost office to [[Sevrin Dal]] after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]].

## Background

Mon Mothma represented Chandrila in the Senate during the Clone Wars. She opposed the war. With [[Padmé Amidala]] and [[Bail Organa]], she worked for a peaceful end to it.

## Chancellor, 11 BBY – 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#Administrations]]*

She followed Ars Vehn, the only Chancellor the Senate has ever managed to remove. Mon Mothma was the first Chancellor to serve under the new [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|charter]]. She put the new charter into practice with complete competence, and lost the office six weeks after Yavin to [[Sevrin Dal]]. Dal was elected six weeks after Yavin on an open promise of revenge.

## In 2 ABY

Mon Mothma is of the [[Three Stances of the Senate|Republic Reformist]] stance.
