**Qi'ra** has been at the head of [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]], the trading arm of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], since 6 BBY. [[Han Solo]], whom she had known before either of them worked for anyone, flew for her for six years.

## Crimson Dawn

Qi'ra runs Crimson Dawn since 6 BBY, the Shadow Collective's trading arm and its main source of money. She has met [[Maul]] in person on eleven recorded occasions and has outlasted three predecessors who met him more often than that.

## Han Solo

*Main article: [[Han Solo]]*

[[Han Solo]] flew for Crimson Dawn for six years under Qi'ra, whom he had known before either of them worked for anyone. He was good at it, paid well, and did not ask what was in the sealed crates. In 1 BBY he ran four such loads out to the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]]. He found out who he had been smuggling for a year later, from the Yavin reports. He left the syndicate that week, and Crimson Dawn has had a standing price on him ever since.
