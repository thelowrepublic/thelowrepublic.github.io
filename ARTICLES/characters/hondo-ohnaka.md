**Hondo Ohnaka** is a Weequay pirate who has sold to the Confederacy, the Republic, the Hutts and the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], often to several at once. These days he runs salvage permits out of Florrum.

## Background

Hondo Ohnaka is a Weequay pirate who led the Ohnaka Gang from Florrum. In the Clone Wars he captured [[The Long War|Count Dooku]], and then [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], and failed to ransom them.

## Career

Hondo Ohnaka is a pirate. He has sold to the [[The Long War|Confederacy]], the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]], the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] and the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]], in that order and often at once. [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] priced him out of four routes in 3 BBY and he couldn't really fight it.

## In 2 ABY

Ohnaka runs salvage permits out of Florrum now, entirely legally. He has informed for [[Republic Intelligence]] eleven times, always for a generous reward.
