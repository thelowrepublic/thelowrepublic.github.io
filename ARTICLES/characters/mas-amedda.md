**Mas Amedda** was Vice Chair of the Senate when [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]] died in 19 BBY. He came through the aftermath unprosecuted and has made a new career advising on the constitution.

## Background

Mas Amedda is a Chagrian. He served as Vice Chair of the Senate under Chancellor Valorum, and then under Palpatine. He was at Palpatine's side through the Separatist crisis and the Clone Wars.

## Acting Chancellor, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine]]*

Vice Chair Mas Amedda took over as acting Chancellor. He spent his six weeks in the post bargaining for his own protection from prosecution. He did it with such patient self-interest that he held up the business of replacing Palpatine by two months. [[Bail Organa]] followed him as Chancellor.

Amedda testified, escaped prosecution, and reinvented himself as an adviser on constitutional questions.

## In 2 ABY

Amedda is universally disliked and continuously employed. He survived a [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] assassination attempt. He is the last living person who worked in Palpatine's office.
