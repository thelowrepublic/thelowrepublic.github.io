It was **Jar Jar Binks** whose motion of 22 BBY handed [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]] his emergency powers. Sent home by [[Naboo]] in 18 BBY, he went from a galactic laughing stock to an object of pity.

## Background

Jar Jar Binks was once an outcast among the Gungans for his clumsiness. When the Trade Federation invaded Naboo, he helped bring the Gungans' leader, Boss Nass, into alliance with Queen [[Padmé Amidala]]. He later sat in the Senate as a Junior Representative of Naboo, beside Senator Amidala. In 22 BBY, with Amidala away from Coruscant, he stood in for her.

## The motion and after

Binks moved the motion that gave Palpatine emergency powers in 22 BBY. He was recalled by Naboo in 18 BBY. He became a figure of galactic ridicule and then of pity. He performed for children in Otoh Gunga, turned down every interview request, and never spoke publicly about the war.

[[Padmé Amidala]] had to fight from the senatorial seat of Naboo, a place that has been [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|more scorned by the day]], a place that produced Palpatine and Binks. A Sith Lord who nearly destroyed the Republic and a fool who made it possible due to his own idiocy. [[Ruko Tass]] left Naboo at fifteen, a world that had produced a Sith Lord and a galactic clown, a fact which Tass was always reminded of.

## In 2 ABY

Every serious historian of the period has concluded he was a minor figure who was used. It has made no difference at all.
