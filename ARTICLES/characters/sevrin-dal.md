**Sevrin Dal** is a Rodian, and Chancellor of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] since 0 ABY. He is a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Traditionalist]].

## Election, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#Administrations]]*

Dal was elected six weeks after Yavin on an open promise of revenge. He took the office from [[Mon Mothma]]. He won with one sentence: the Republic would find the people responsible for Yavin and finish them.

## Emergency powers

*Main article: [[The Shadow War#The Republic at war]]*

Chancellor Sevrin Dal asked for emergency powers eight days after the last survivors came home from Yavin. The Senate granted them and has renewed them eight times. He is blatantly pushing for war against [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]], but has not managed to sway the Senate just yet.

The [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|ninety-day rule]] has worked exactly as written on every single occasion. [[Padmé Amidala]], who wrote it, has said that the rule works and the politics beat it. She has not proposed changing it, on the grounds that she cannot think of a change that would have made any difference.

A Chancellor can be removed by the Senate on a two-thirds vote. Three motions have been filed against Dal in two years. The best of them reached forty-one percent. All three came from the [[Three Stances of the Senate#The Rim self-rule bloc|Rim self-rule bloc]], and all three raised the number of sectors thinking about leaving.

## The Traditionalist stance

*Main article: [[Three Stances of the Senate#The Traditionalists]]*

The Traditionalists hold that the Republic works. Sevrin Dal is one of them. The charter is sound and the Convention fixed what needed fixing. The only reason anything fails now is that sectors keep walking out. Bring them back and the problem ends.

They are right that the charter works as written and right that the secessions broke the budget. They speak of the Rim as territory mislaid rather than as members who left. What they do not say is that bringing sectors back by force means a full civil war, and it means ruling by fear and threat.

## In 2 ABY

Two years on, Dal has finished nobody. He understood the shape of this war inside six months and has only ever said so in committee. He has renewed the emergency powers eight times, never relenting in his warmongering.
