**Ilai Vess** is the senator for the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] sector, which contains [[Dathomir]], and a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]]. A Dathomirian raised on [[Coruscant]], she practised administrative law before she took the seat. In the second month of 2 ABY she read the Yavin 4 dig permit into the Senate record, and within the month Quelii had seceded from the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]].

## Early life

Ilai Vess was evacuated off [[Dathomir]] at two and raised on [[Coruscant]], and has returned to Dathomir only since becoming its senator. She is not Force-sensitive and has no tie to either order on her world. She practised administrative law for six years and qualified for the Senate seat on the technicality of her birth-citizenship.

## The Vess case, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The Vess case]]*

Senator Ilai Vess of the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] sector, which contains Dathomir, stood up in the Senate in the second month of 2 ABY and read the [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] permit for the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]] into the record. Her case ran as follows. The site had been legally established for five years, registered, renewed and fully in order. No war had been declared on it. Nobody had obtained a warrant. Nobody had been given notice. A Republic fleet under Republic officers had attacked a licensed dig in a sector close to her own with no legal authority to do it. The people at the site had defended themselves with what they had, which she admitted was unusual, but which she would not condemn. The people of Yavin 4 were victims of unprovoked aggression and were within their right to repel the aggressors by whatever means necessary.

She then read more into the record. The [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] findings. The Outland Command arrangement. The deaths of [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Senator Fang Zar and the Ord Mantell magistrate]]. The [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers|droid papers]] of 6 BBY, which she read out in full, and which took most of an afternoon. And the names of the [[The Battle of Yavin#The Jedi who went|thirty-one rogue Jedi]]. The Republic's answer was that the Jedi at Yavin had been private individuals acting alone and that the Order had disowned them within hours. Vess replied that the Republic had made their surviving commander [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|a general eleven weeks later]], and that it had since put twenty-one hundred more Jedi into uniform and handed them the [[Coruscant#The Jedi Temple|Coruscant Temple]]. She said that a government which promotes the officers of an operation it calls unauthorised, and then raises an army out of their colleagues, has already told the Senate what it really thinks of that operation.

She argued the Yavin 4 case as the Gordian Reach's retained counsel rather than as its representative, which is why it could not be dismissed as a sector pleading for itself.

The Republic has not found an effective reply. It cannot prove a registered permit was not registered or that the attack was authorised when no order authorising it exists. It cannot claim that Murkhana and Ord Mantell did not happen. It cannot object to the use of the Force in war while employing Obi-Wan Kenobi as a general, and that is before anyone mentions the extensive use of Jedi during the Clone Wars. The Republic can say that Quelii's government is exploited by a criminal organisation. This seems entirely true yet impossible to prove, and forty years of Republic negligence in the Outer Rim would only make that argument a statement of the Republic's own incompetence.

Despite being an advocate for Outer Rim self-reliance, [[Lux Bonteri]] has said publicly that he does not accept Senator Vess's case but knows why it is winning. In his words, Vess's case is a blatant scrapbook of convenient excuses, all at the behest of a crime lord.

## Secession, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY]]*

Quelii voted to leave the Republic the same month as Vess's speech. The declaration named several [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] assets as mercenary armies in the pay of the new state. Additionally, on behalf of the Gordian Reach sector as its contracted legal representative, Vess demanded the interdiction fleet be withdrawn immediately and asked for damages in a sum widely described as theatrical. The sum has not come down since. 

A month later the Gordian Reach itself seceded as well, quietly and without fanfare. The two sectors formed a defensive and economic alliance, the Dathomir-Yavin Accord, in the sixth month of 2 ABY. Three months later, the Thesme, D'Astan and Kalamith sectors (which lie between Quelii and the Gordian Reach) stopped remitting taxes towards the Republic. None of the three has been given a seat in the Senate for decades. Rumours circulate of increased Collective presence in those sectors and an expanded alliance pact being drafted by Oleth Kar, senator of the Gordian Reach. Everyone knows what this means, but the Republic won't acknowledge it, because it would mean the Republic just lost control of another vital hyperspace lane.

## In the Senate

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The standoff]]*

Because Seswenna never left, in law, its nine sectors are still entitled to nine Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. Quelii and the Gordian Reach are in the same situation and have taken the opposite view. Vess and [[Oleth Kar|Kar]] sit, speak, table motions and vote. All of it on the strength of a technicality they helped create and describe in public as an absurdity. Neither sector has remitted a credit of tax since it declared secession.

Nearly every [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] the Republic has arrested was taken during a Collective operation. They are charged as criminals or as terrorists, like anyone else on the raid. The exceptions are few and they are the ones that matter. A small number were arrested for what they were and not for anything they had done. Those are the cases Senator Vess keeps track of.

[[Leia Skywalker]] is the youngest member of the committee overseeing the war, and the only one who has met Senator Vess privately multiple times.

## In 2 ABY

Vess does not work for the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. She was born a Dathomirian, but was raised on Coruscant and never joined the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] in any capacity. She is a legitimately elected senator, and she was the Gordian Reach's counsel in a case that sector was entitled to bring. What she did with the facts afterwards was her own decision. She has made a judgement about who can actually protect the people who elected her, and she has never once faltered in that stance.

Four inquiries have gone looking for what is underneath her veneer. All four found a competent Coruscanti lawyer working for a sector that was never hers. Since the Republic never recognised the secessions, Vess still holds her seat on a technicality, and uses it brazenly.
