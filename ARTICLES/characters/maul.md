**Maul** is the Zabrak warlord at the head of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], a galaxy-spanning criminal empire. A son of [[Dathomir]], he escaped Republic custody in the confusion after [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine's death]], spending the next 9 years building up the largest crime syndicate the galaxy has seen in millennia. The earliest surviving record of his bargain with [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] dates from 17 BBY, and the bargain ended in 1 BBY.

## Background

Maul was a Dathomirian Zabrak, born on [[Dathomir]]. He was taken from the planet as a child by the Sith Lord [[The Death of Palpatine|Sidious]] and trained as his apprentice. In 32 BBY, during the invasion of [[Naboo]], he killed the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and was cut in half by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. He survived. During the Clone Wars he and his brother Savage Opress took over [[Mandalore]] with Death Watch, and he gathered Black Sun and the Pyke Syndicate into a criminal alliance, the Shadow Collective.

## The escape, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine#The escape of Maul]]*

Four days before Palpatine's killing, the Zabrak warlord Maul had been captured on [[Mandalore]] by Commander [[Ahsoka Tano]] and the 501st Legion. He was put aboard a Venator-class Star Destroyer bound for [[Coruscant]]. He was the only prisoner in the galaxy who understood immediately what Palpatine's death meant. He had known his former master's identity for over a decade and had been dismissed as a lunatic every time he said so.

When the news of Palpatine's death reached the fleet, its chain of command was in disarray. There were recall orders, competing authorities, rumours of a Jedi coup, and a Senate demanding the return of every capital ship in the Core. Ahsoka was temporarily disarmed and detained. Maul escaped in the confusion, tearing apart half of the Star Destroyer in the process, and reached Mandalorian space within the month.

When life support started failing, Tano was released from confinement, but prioritised saving lives over pursuing Maul. She does not regret her choice, but still views the operation as one of the greatest failures of her life.

## The bargain with Tarkin, 17–1 BBY

*Main article: [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]*

[[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] made contact with Maul in 17 BBY. The earliest surviving record is a supply manifest from that year, and the arrangement was almost certainly older than the paperwork.

The deal was simple, as far as anyone could piece it together afterwards. Maul's people killed for Tarkin. Separatist field commanders, the bankers paying for them, and any Republic official standing in Tarkin's way. In exchange Tarkin gave Maul intelligence, safe passage, three sectors where nobody would come looking, and a share of captured Separatist wealth that never reached Coruscant.

Tarkin never gave an order anyone could follow back to him. He described a problem in front of the right person and let them reach their own conclusion. He seems to have understood from the start that what Maul wanted out of the arrangement was not money but leverage. He decided that was a price worth paying. And to a large extent, he was right.

The bargain ended in 1 BBY and neither man has moved against the other since. Quelii and Seswenna sit at opposite ends of the Rim. Beyond that, each is useful to the other. Prosperous Rim sectors are the Collective's best argument that the Republic is not needed. A Republic coming apart is Seswenna's best argument that local authority is the best choice.

## Dathomir, 10–3 BBY

### The return

*Main article: [[Dathomir]]*

[[Dathomir]] was massacred by General [[The Long War|Grievous]] in 21 BBY. The Nightsister clans were finished as an organised power and the survivors scattered. Maul was a son of that world, taken from it as a child and raised as a weapon by a master who threw him away. He came back in 10 BBY and spent four years rebuilding. He cleared the ruins and found survivors across a dozen sectors. The most important of them was [[Merrin]], who had been seven years old when the massacre happened and had spent eleven years as the last practising witch of her clan.

### The Nightborn

The Nightsister tradition had been closed to men for four thousand years. The Nightbrothers of the Dathomiri villages had been breeding stock and labour for it. Maul himself had been born into that arrangement, one he would not be willing to fund, so Merrin opened the practice to them. She chose [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] because Nightsister no longer described what she was building, and the word spread outward from her branch to cover the whole order. Three traditions were braided together. The magick came from Merrin: illusion, spirit-work, ichor, and the raising of the dead. Sith techniques came from Maul and from what he dug up at over a dozen ancient ruins. Jedi training, meditation, the lightsaber forms and a working knowledge of how the Jedi actually operate were brought in by defectors and taught by [[Barriss Offee]].

Barriss Offee was released in 9 BBY, and within a month, Maul managed to contact her and sway her to his side. Apparently they talked over some tea.

### Ventress and Nightspire

*Main article: [[The Nightsisters]]*

[[Asajj Ventress]] was reported killed during a Jedi operation in 19 BBY. She reappeared in 9 BBY, revealed to have been operating as a bounty hunter under multiple aliases for all this time. The same year she received an invitation from Maul. The rationale was simple. She and Maul were the same thing, Dathomiri weapons made by one hierarchy and thrown away by another. She was a bounty hunter because she had nowhere else to be and nothing to strive for. Maul could give her both. She accepted. She was one of the order's founders and for six years its best field leader.

The arrangement failed for the reason such arrangements usually fail. Maul had built it on a promise that nobody in it would be used, and used her exactly as [[The Long War|Dooku]] had. They fought at [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire|Nightspire]] in 3 BBY. Neither died. She left the same night with twenty Dathomiri witches of the fifty present. Two hundred others went with her, Collective personnel, civilians and a handful of former Jedi. She set herself up in the eastern highlands, where she took back the name Nightsisters and the whole of the old practice with it: the closed rite, the female line, the four-thousand-year tradition Merrin had broken. She holds that the Nightborn are not a restoration of Dathomir but a second theft of it. Not because of the inclusion of men in the rites, but because they don't realise that Maul will forsake all of their principles the moment he needs an army. Neither Maul nor the Republic has been able to shift her, and both have decided it is not worth what it would cost to try.

[[Morgan Elsbeth]] joined the Nightborn for Dathomir and not for Maul. For six years she made sure that Ventress could see what Maul was using her for, and Nightspire followed.

## Yavin, 5 BBY–0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

Maul registered a dig on the [[Yavin 4|fourth moon of Yavin Prime]] with the Republic [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] in 5 BBY. It went through a shell company, on the correct forms, with the correct fees, renewed every year. It was one of eleven hundred such permits active in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] that year and drew no attention whatsoever. The Massassi temples there were raised by the Sith of Naga Sadow's day and abandoned for four thousand years. They are also where [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Exar Kun]] put his spirit at the end of the Great Sith War. He escaped the destruction of his body by pouring himself into the stone, and he stayed there for four thousand years. Maul had not gone to Yavin for riches or power. He had gone to find out how Kun did it.

In the first weeks of 1 BBY [[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] laid the Outland Command evidence in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths. Tarkin understood his position at once. He did not deny the connection. He described it as wartime intelligence work, difficult compromises he was forced to make, and expressed regret that it had been necessary. Within four months he had become the loudest and most convincing voice in the Republic for destroying the Shadow Collective. He was even willing to fully fund and coordinate the manhunt for Maul, as long as the Republic provided troops. He was the man who had seen the danger first, from the inside, and whose warnings had been ignored all this time.

The assault on Yavin failed miserably, as the desperate Nightborn used the Sith nexus to amplify their own magick and fight the Republic. The first five days of the Battle of Yavin were nothing but mass confusion and death, broadcast to the entire galaxy over HoloNet. On the sixth day of the battle Tarkin ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most Nightborn left the moon hours earlier. Maul was already gone by day four.

## The search

Maul is fifty-six as of 2 ABY. He has named no successor and has turned the question aside for a decade. His own lieutenants read this as vanity or weakness. It is in fact both, in a sense. Naming a successor admits there will need to be one. What he found at Yavin is a method for immortality for a price he will not pay. What Kun did held him to a place. Four thousand years fixed to a temple he could not leave is not survival as Maul understands the word. It is an eternity of imprisonment.

He has spent seven years looking for a version of the rite that does not carry that price. However, he has still uncovered troves of Sith knowledge for the Nightborn's benefit. Nobody in the Shadow Collective knows Maul's end goal. Neither Merrin nor Barriss Offee knows it. Both have privately concluded that he is simply afraid to name an heir because it would mean acknowledging his own age, and neither of them is wrong about that. As Maul's case proves, the legacy of paranoia does seem to run from master to apprentice among the Sith.

## In 2 ABY

The Shadow Collective is Maul's whole organisation and his own name for it. It takes in the criminal syndicates, meaning Black Sun, the trading arm that works under the name Crimson Dawn and formerly the Pyke Syndicate, now its cast-offs known as Shadow Pykes. It also takes in the Mandalorian Death Watch under [[Gar Saxon]], salvaged droid formations, clone legions saved from ageing by black market procedures, hired ground troops, and the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]].

[[Qi'ra]] runs Crimson Dawn since 6 BBY, the Shadow Collective's trading arm and its main source of money. She has met Maul in person on eleven recorded occasions and has outlasted three predecessors who met him more often than that. [[Sarn Vekh]] took about a fifth of the Pyke Syndicate and stayed loyal to Maul as the Shadow Pykes.

Maul does not regulate the temperament of the syndicate bosses and does not care to. Every one of them is a criminal, and the only thing that can be asked of a criminal is if the money is right. And it always is. [[Nadi Orl]] audits all of them quarterly and checks if the ledgers are in order. If anything is amiss, they are warned once and severely fined. If they fail again, they are eliminated and replaced. Maul has never overruled Orl.

The [[The Hutt Clans|Hutt Clans]] deal with the Shadow Collective constantly and have never once submitted to it. Jabba Desilijic Tiure has done business with Crimson Dawn for eighteen years. He defers to Maul in nothing and treats the whole relationship as trade between equals, which infuriates Maul and is probably the point.

A fifty-six-year-old Maul amasses power on Dathomir, when he is there at all. He presides over six hundred people who have stopped needing him. His order was founded on a promise that none of its members would ever be a tool. It is run day to day by Merrin who merely wants her people to be allowed to exist, and by Barriss Offee who has been making the same argument towards the Jedi Order since she was nineteen. He has told neither of them what he was looking for at Yavin. 

[[Luke Skywalker]] left [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]]'s command in 1 ABY and is listed as going rogue. In reality, Kenobi sent him. The Republic holds Kenobi in place, and Maul can only be effectively pursued by a free agent. Maul needs to be stopped by whatever means it takes, and Kenobi trusts Luke to know which means are the right and wrong ones. What little Luke has picked up of Maul's trail has lately been moving deeper towards the Core, not away from it, and this disturbs him.
