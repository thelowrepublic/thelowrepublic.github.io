**Plo Koon** is the Jedi Master at the head of the [[Ossus Order]], so far as it has a head. His two-sentence refusal of the [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|Templar offer]] is all he has ever said on the matter.

## Background

Plo Koon was a Jedi Master and a member of the Jedi High Council in the years before the Clone Wars. In the Clone Wars he was a Jedi General in command of the 104th Battalion, with Commander [[Wolffe]]. When the Separatist warship Malevolence destroyed his fleet, he escaped in a pod with Wolffe and two other troopers. He told his men that their lives mattered and that rescue would come. [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] rescued them.

## The wars

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The survivors]]*

Because [[The Kamino Findings#The contingency orders|Order 66]] was never sent, the Order kept a generation of senior masters who would have otherwise died within an hour of one another. Plo Koon, [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Aayla Secura]], [[Shaak Ti]], [[Luminara Unduli]], [[Stass Allie]] and [[Ossus Order|Coleman Kcaj]] all came through the [[The Long War|Long War]]. The preserved knowledge and continuity of leadership made the Order's restructuring smoother than it could've otherwise been. None of it prevented what followed.

## The schism, 14–8 BBY

Plo Koon was the reformists' most persuasive voice through the schism years and the man who did most to get acknowledged bonds into the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Compact]]. His grounds were straightforward. He had spent a war caring about the people under his command, took severe risks to save them, and had never once found that it made him 'corrupted' or worse at the job.

## The Templar offer, 0 ABY

Plo Koon declined the [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|Templar offer]] to join in a written reply of two sentences, which has been widely circulated since: "The path you are determined to re-tread will destroy you. We will not follow." He has never expanded on them.

[[Shaak Ti]] stayed with him. [[Ossus Order|Coleman Kcaj]] also stayed, and teaches. [[Aayla Secura]] has long since departed to [[Voss]] before any of this happened and knows nothing about it.

## In 2 ABY

Plo Koon presides over the Ossus Order, insofar as anyone does. He continued leading Jedi missions to protect people of the Rim, until his venerable age made excursions unsafe. He currently teaches new generations of Jedi.
