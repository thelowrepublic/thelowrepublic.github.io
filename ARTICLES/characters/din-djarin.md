**Din Djarin** is of the [[Mandalore#The Children of the Watch|Children of the Watch]], the sect that is now the most valuable military asset of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. Orphaned in the [[The Long War|Long War]] and raised in the creed from infancy, he has been trained from his first steps to kill Force-users.

## The Children of the Watch

*Main article: [[Mandalore#The Children of the Watch]]*

With the rising presence of Jedi Templars in the Shadow War, the Death Watch went back to re-learn techniques for killing Force-users that had survived only because a handful of isolationist sects had never stopped practising them. One of those sects is the Children of the Watch, who take in orphans and raise them in the creed from infancy. They have done this without a break since before the Clone Wars. Until about 0 ABY most Mandalorians regarded them as a mild embarrassment. They are now the most valuable military asset the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] possesses.

Din Djarin was orphaned in the [[The Long War|Long War]] and taken in by the Children as an infant. He is twenty-one. He has been trained since he could walk to kill people who can predict the future and bend minds, and he is extremely good at it. The [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] confirm eight Jedi dead by his hand and privately suspect a good many more. He was born the same year as [[Luke Skywalker]]. They have not met yet.
