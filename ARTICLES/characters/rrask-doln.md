**Rrask Doln** is a Togorian, the third minister of the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]] and a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]]. He was a Taldot shipping clerk before the war and a militia quartermaster during it.

## The Coalition

*Main article: [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition]]*

The [[The Long War|Long War]] was fought hardest in the [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]]. The Taldot sector lost most of its industry and fourteen of its worlds were left completely uninhabitable. The damage spread into the Mytaranor, Lantillian, Halla and Kastolar sectors. Halla and Kastolar border Hutt Space, and the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. The Governor of Taldot called the other four together in 14 BBY. They agreed to pool what was left. Shipping, yards, food, and whatever militia each of them could still raise. It took two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar. Then they all went to the Senate and asked for their taxes to be cut to a fraction. They got it, because the Perlemian Trade Route runs through their territories and the Senate could not risk them blockading it. The Coalition became official.

## In 2 ABY

Doln is the Coalition's third minister. The first was [[Jhena Varrin]], Governor of Taldot, who served in the same militia during the war. In public he calls the Coalition loyal and says it has no complaints. He also keeps reminding everyone about the Perlemian Trade Route that runs through the Coalition's territories. And about the opinionated Wookiees that are a part of the Coalition.
