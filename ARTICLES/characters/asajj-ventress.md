**Asajj Ventress** leads the restored [[The Nightsisters|Nightsisters]] from the eastern highlands of [[Dathomir]]. A bounty hunter who had been reported killed, she accepted [[Maul]]'s invitation in 9 BBY and was the best field leader of the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] for six years, until the two fought at [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire|Nightspire]].

## Background

Asajj Ventress was a Dathomirian Zabrak, a Nightsister, taken from her family on [[Dathomir]] as an infant. In the Clone Wars she was Count [[The Long War|Dooku]]'s assassin and led Separatist forces against the Republic, fighting [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Dooku abandoned her on the orders of [[The Death of Palpatine|Sidious]], and she went back to the Nightsisters on Dathomir. After Grievous massacred them she wandered the galaxy and became a bounty hunter. She helped [[Ahsoka Tano]] when Tano was framed for the bombing of the Jedi Temple.

## The Nightborn, 9–3 BBY

*Main article: [[The Nightsisters]]*

Asajj Ventress was reported killed during a Jedi operation in 19 BBY. She reappeared in 9 BBY, revealed to have been operating as a bounty hunter under multiple aliases for all this time. The same year she received an invitation from [[Maul]]. The rationale was simple. She and Maul were the same thing, Dathomiri weapons made by one hierarchy and thrown away by another. She was a bounty hunter because she had nowhere else to be and nothing to strive for. Maul could give her both. She accepted. She was one of the order's founders and for six years its best field leader.

## Nightspire, 3 BBY

*Main article: [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire]]*

The arrangement failed for the reason such arrangements usually fail. Maul had built it on a promise that nobody in it would be used, and used her exactly as Dooku had. They fought at Nightspire in 3 BBY. Neither died. She left the same night with twenty Dathomiri witches of the fifty present. Two hundred others went with her, Collective personnel, civilians and a handful of former Jedi. She set herself up in the eastern highlands, where she took back the name Nightsisters and the whole of the old practice with it: the closed rite, the female line, the four-thousand-year tradition [[Merrin]] had broken. She holds that the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] are not a restoration of Dathomir but a second theft of it. Not because of the inclusion of men in the rites, but because they don't realise that Maul will forsake all of their principles the moment he needs an army. Neither Maul nor the Republic has been able to shift her, and both have decided it is not worth what it would cost to try.

[[Morgan Elsbeth]] joined the Nightborn for Dathomir and not for Maul. For six years she made sure that Ventress could see what Maul was using her for, and Nightspire followed.

## In 2 ABY

Forty kilometres away from Maul in the eastern highlands, Asajj Ventress holds the old rite, attempts a second shot at a peaceful life and diligently avoids contact with [[Quinlan Vos]] for reasons only she knows. She recovered her lightsabers at some point.

[[Ahsoka Tano]] has been to the edge of Dathomiri space at least twice. A ship matching no registry breached the Nightsister territory and stayed there for five hours in the ninth month of 1 ABY and then left. Nobody came out to see it. Ventress's people have said nothing about the matter, which is what they say about everything.

There is no evidence that Tano has spoken to Ventress, to Merrin or to [[Barriss Offee]].
