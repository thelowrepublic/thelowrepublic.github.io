**Baylan Skoll** is a former Jedi Master and general of the Clone Wars, and a former member of the [[Order of Voss]]. He left the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] with the withdrawalists in 8 BBY in search of Peridea, a place he had once taken for a fairy tale. In 2 ABY he fled Voss with something taken from its mystics, broke into the Jedi Temple on [[Ossus]], and disappeared.

## Background

Baylan Skoll was a human Force-sensitive, brought into the Jedi Order as a child. He rose to Jedi Knight and then Jedi Master, and served as a Jedi General in the Clone Wars. He knew [[Anakin Skywalker]], and the old droid Huyang. Somewhere in the Order's archives he came across the old stories of Peridea, which he took for fairy tales.

## The Long War and the Reckoning

The Clone Wars did not end for Skoll in 19 BBY. He fought on through the [[The Long War|Long War]], and kept his command until the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation]] of 16 BBY took it from him. Then he watched the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|schism]] that followed tear the Order apart. Asked years later what he had learned from it, he said that he had watched everything he knew fall apart, and that history only ever repeats itself.

He came to believe that the Order's fall was no accident but the turn of a cycle, one that comes for every institution that grows old serving power, and that nothing inside the galaxy could break it. So he began to look outside it. The old stories of Peridea stopped seeming like fairy tales.

## Voss, 8 BBY–2 ABY

*Main article: [[Order of Voss]]*

Skoll was one of the six hundred and thirty-two Jedi who resigned after the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Ossus Compact]] in 8 BBY. He gave up his rank and his lightsaber like the others, and the saber stayed with the Order on [[Ossus]]. He was among the roughly two hundred who crossed to [[Voss]], drawn like the rest to a place in the Force where attention would not go. Unlike the rest, he went looking for what lay behind it.

Voss gave him no answers, only more questions, but also clues. In 2 ABY he took something from the Voss mystics and fled. The [[Order of Voss]] has never said what it was.

## Ossus, 2 ABY

The Order of Voss then did something it had never done before. It sent word to the [[Ossus Order]] and the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]], warning them to be wary of Skoll. It is the only message the Order of Voss has ever sent either of them.

It reached [[Ossus]] too late. Skoll had already broken into the Temple. He harmed no one, and left with his own old lightsaber and a stone tablet from the Archives. Nothing else was missing.

## In 2 ABY

Nobody knows where Skoll is. He speaks little and carries himself with a gravity few can match. He still believes the galaxy has to break before anything new can be built, and that whatever comes next lies beyond its edge. Both Jedi bodies are looking for him.
