**Nadi Orl** is a Twi'lek, the chief of accounts for the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. Every syndicate under [[Maul]] answers to her audits, and [[Republic Intelligence]] would rather catch her than anyone else in the organisation.

## Chief of accounts

A child prodigy, Nadi Orl was a clerk in the Banking Clan at fifteen, until the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord]] took its Senate seats and its licences. What followed were layoffs numbering in the millions, and Orl was one of them. After every lawful structure in her life failed her, it did not take long for her to start creating structures that prioritised efficiency over lawfulness. In 14 BBY, she already created a huge smuggling network, which ran so smoothly and efficiently, that [[Maul]] only discovered it through a series of freak coincidences that both of them recount with a smile nowadays. Instead of merely dismantling or absorbing the network, Maul personally met with Orl and recruited her, seeing the young woman's immense potential.

Ever since then, Orl sets wages, funds pensions, approves capital projects, and audits every syndicate under Maul quarterly. Maul does not regulate the temperament of the syndicate bosses and does not care to. Every one of them is a criminal, and the only thing that can be asked of a criminal is if the money is right. And it always is. Nadi Orl audits all of them quarterly and checks if the ledgers are in order. If anything is amiss, they are warned once and severely fined. If they fail again, they are eliminated and replaced. Since 4 BBY the audits have counted more than money. A boss who bleeds a world dry is wasting something the Collective could recruit from or invest in, and Orl fines them for it as she would for a missing credit.

## The Pykes, 4–2 BBY

*Main article: [[The Hutt Clans#The Pykes]]*

[[Sarn Vekh]] ran spice distribution into the poorest systems of the Rim for nineteen years and was made a director for it. In 4 BBY the Collective ordered the Syndicate out of those systems entirely. Nadi Orl had decided that an addicted sector cannot be recruited from or invested in, and both of those options were more profitable in the long run. The Pyke Syndicate had been selling itself to the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] and the Collective at once since 8 BBY. Orl's order is what broke this game and resulted in two years of conflict. At the end of it in 2 BBY, the main Syndicate body went to the Hutts. Vekh took about a fifth of it and stayed loyal to Maul as the Shadow Pykes. Profit was the reason she gave Maul. Those who have worked closely with her doubt it was the only one.

## In 2 ABY

Thanks to Orl, the Collective pays on time in hundreds of systems, and over eighty thousand [[Clones|clones]] receive good pay, expert medical oversight, and retirement to comfortable lives when their contracts run out. Maul has never overruled her. Republic Intelligence rates her the highest-value target in the organisation.

She has since come back to her homeworld of [[Ryloth]], using the immense wealth she accrued from running the Collective to set herself up publicly as a philanthropist entrepreneur. She has invested immense funds into the city of Kala'uun, turning it from a meagre starport into a bustling resort metropolis, with her as the head of the ruling council. Most in the Collective simply regard this as 'Orl's pet project', but the woman herself seems to be completely genuine in her desire to elevate Ryloth into prominence, outright banning Collective activity on Ryloth unless she authorises it personally, as well as sending very violent warnings to several corporations that wished to extract Ryloth's natural resources. She even attempts to integrate indigenous Twi'lek clans into her plans as tourist guides and spiritual gurus for hire, though she has immense difficulties trying to come to any sort of agreement with the Clan Assembly.
