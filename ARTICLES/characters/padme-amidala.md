**Padmé Amidala** drafted the [[The Manumission Act|Manumission Act]] and chaired the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]], and is the most consequential lawmaker of the age without ever holding high office. She is married to [[Anakin Skywalker]], and their twins, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker|Leia]], were born in 19 BBY.

## Background

Padmé Amidala was the elected Queen of [[Naboo]] in 32 BBY, under the name Amidala, when the Trade Federation invaded it. She freed her world with the help of the Jedi and the Gungans, and then became its senator. In 22 BBY she secretly married [[Anakin Skywalker]], although the Jedi Code forbade it. Through the Clone Wars she argued in the Senate for a peaceful end to the war.

## The twins, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine]]*

The [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]] marriage became public in 19 BBY, Amidala having been visibly pregnant all through her husband's [[The Death of Palpatine#The limits of what he knew|testimony]].

Palpatine had promised Anakin Skywalker knowledge that would prevent his wife's death in childbirth. That promise was extinguished in front of him. Padmé Amidala survived the birth of twins four months later. Whether the danger or the promise to avoid it had ever been genuine was never established, but Skywalker did not stop considering the question.

## The Manumission Act, 16 BBY

*Main article: [[The Manumission Act]]*

The Manumission Act of 16 BBY made every [[Clones|clone]] a citizen. Padmé Amidala drafted it and [[Bail Organa]] carried it. [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Captain [[Rex]] argued it through the Senate, giving more than four hundred hours of testimony between them. It passed as much out of necessity as conscience. Eight million living beings could not be released from service into nothing at all, with no standing under any law. Two million of them were children.

## Murkhana, 16–14 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

As a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], not a Jedi or a Chosen One, [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] destroyed a city. Intelligence said that there was a buried droid command node under it and that the population was complicit. The node was there. Whether the city's population knew about it is still debated to this day. About two hundred thousand civilians died and the campaign in that sector ended four months early. The intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. He said so at the inquiry. He said someone wanted him gone, but he could not prove it, and he sounded exactly like a man making excuses.

Skywalker's and [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s war theatres overlapped for three years and that was enough to sign the former Jedi's sentence. Skywalker fought to finish the war and Tarkin's position depended on the war continuing. The most effective commander in the Republic was in the wrong theatre, moving too fast, closing campaigns that had been budgeted for years. Of course, nothing has ever been proved. Outland Command has no surviving record of sending the reports. The inquiry reached no conclusion. His wife's enemies wanted a conviction and her allies wanted it buried, and the compromise between them finished both of their public careers. He was discharged with honour in 14 BBY.

The separation from Padmé is usually described as a political arrangement, and it partly was. Her career could not survive standing next to him, but the decision was substantially his. The reason he gave was that a man who had killed two hundred thousand people on a briefing he wasn't wise enough to double check had no business raising two children. Padmé did not agree, and has never agreed. The record of that argument survives in [[C-3PO]]'s testimony. The family was never estranged in the ordinary sense. They love him and he loves them, and he has decided that this is not the relevant consideration. He has visited the twins, but not often and never for long. By the end of 2 ABY he had not visited them for seven years.

## The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY]]*

It met under a [[The Republic Remade#Administrations|Security government]], which is the main reason it produced rules with teeth instead of a statement of good intentions.

Padmé Amidala chaired it. Through it all, she had to fight from the senatorial seat of [[Naboo]], a place that has been more scorned by the day, a place that produced Palpatine and [[Jar Jar Binks|Binks]]. A Sith Lord who nearly destroyed the Republic and a fool who made it possible due to his own idiocy. Add to it the inquiry into her husband's conduct at [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]], and all this has put the Chancellorship permanently out of her reach. So she ended up writing the rules that other people would govern under.

What it achieved: emergency powers now expired automatically after ninety days unless the Senate voted to keep them, with no exceptions written in for any reason. Corporations were barred from holding Senate seats. The army and navy became permanent and answered to a civilian ministry rather than to the Chancellor. The courts were put out of the Chancellor's reach. And a standing [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]] was set up with the power to demand any record the government held.

What it failed at: giving the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] more seats, which the Core worlds voted down; getting rid of appointed sector governors; freeing the Jedi from the ordinary courts; and writing any law against slavery that could actually be enforced in or near [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt space]].

The Convention also chose to say nothing whatsoever about how a sector might leave the Republic. The argument that won was that writing down a way out is an invitation to use it. So nothing was written down, keeping the status quo of no legal way to secede from the Republic. This turned out to prevent nothing.

## Naboo, 9 BBY

Her career ended in 9 BBY when the [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] compromise finally caught up with her. She went home to [[Naboo]], remained in the Senate as Naboo's planetary representative, and raised the twins largely alone.

She and Anakin took care to keep their children out of their politics. [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] was sent to [[Ossus]] at twelve. [[Leia Skywalker|Leia]] did not leave Naboo. She lived with her mother full-time until adulthood.

## In 2 ABY

Padmé Amidala holds the planetary representative seat in the Senate, the second Chommell seat. Her daughter, [[Leia Skywalker|Leia]], holds the first Chommell seat as a sector representative.

Chancellor [[Sevrin Dal]] asked for emergency powers eight days after the last survivors came home from [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]]. The Senate granted them and has renewed them eight times. He is blatantly pushing for war against [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]], but has not managed to sway the Senate just yet.

The [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|ninety-day rule]] has worked exactly as written on every single occasion. Padmé Amidala, who wrote it, has said that the rule works and the politics beat it. She has not proposed changing it, on the grounds that she cannot think of a change that would have made any difference.

Padmé Amidala wrote the charter that Leia rages against. Her daughter spends most of her working life arguing that it is strangling the thing it was built to protect.

She has never accepted [[Anakin Skywalker|her husband]]'s reasoning about himself and has never stopped telling him so on the rare occasions she can.
