**Director Gideon** is in charge of recruitment and training for the [[The Corporate Security Alliance|Corporate Security Alliance]]. Every soldier the Alliance has fielded has come through his programme.

## Before the Alliance

Little of Gideon's earlier career is on record. He is known to have spent the [[The Long War|Long War]] and the years after it designing programmes that produced unusually effective soldiers, of one kind or another, for whoever was paying. [[Republic Intelligence]] has a very thin file on him.

## The Alliance, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Corporate Security Alliance#The numbers]]*

When the [[The Corporate Security Alliance|Alliance]] was signed in the fourth month of 2 ABY, Gideon was made its Director of Recruitment and Training. Its first troops were in the field two months later, armed, armoured and drilled, and by the end of the year it had declared fifty thousand. Asked at a shareholder meeting where so many recruits had come from, Gideon answered that the Alliance pays well, and took the next question.

## In 2 ABY

Gideon answers to the Alliance's board and to no one else. He has never given an interview. [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]] officers who have met him describe him as courteous, precise and entirely uninterested in their questions.
