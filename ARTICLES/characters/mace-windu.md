**Mace Windu** was the Jedi who killed [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]] in 19 BBY and handed himself over to the courts on the first morning. He was acquitted, ruined politically, a teacher at [[Ossus]] for thirteen years, and killed on the fourth day at [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] in an operation his own Order had forbidden.

## Background

Mace Windu was a Jedi Master and a senior member of the Jedi High Council in the years before the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY he led the Jedi strike team to [[Geonosis]] to rescue [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala]]. Through the Clone Wars he served as a Jedi General, and he oversaw the campaign to retake [[Ryloth]] from the Separatists.

## The Chancellor's office, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine#The Chancellor's office, 19 BBY]]*

Four Jedi entered the Supreme Chancellor's private office to arrest him on suspicion of being a Sith Lord. Agen Kolar, Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were dead within nine seconds. Mace Windu duelled [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]] alone, disarmed him, and forced him against the window.

[[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived. He did not intervene. Windu struck.

## The first morning

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine#The first weeks]]*

Mace Windu presented himself at the Judiciary Building at dawn and handed over his lightsaber. He appears to have considered this an obvious duty rather than a political act. Any other course of action, in his mind, would've resulted in open conflict between the Jedi and the Republic. What he did settled an important question before anyone could ask it. The Jedi would answer to ordinary courts like anyone else. The Order never managed to take that back.

## The trial, 19–17 BBY

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The Windu trial, 19–17 BBY]]*

Two years, and the most closely watched trial in Republic history. Windu was acquitted on the grounds that the threat had been immediate. The defence rested on three dead Jedi, the evidence from Palpatine's chambers, [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]]'s testimony, and in the final year the [[The Kamino Findings|Kamino findings]]. The verdict was sound and changed few minds. What lasted was the precedent, and Windu had set it himself on the first morning without meaning to. He resigned from the Council in 17 BBY and became a teacher and instructor at [[Ossus]] after the Order moved there.

## Yavin, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

The Council refused to be involved. The debate ran for four days and the refusal was not close. The Order had abolished its own military rank sixteen years earlier and had spent eight years arguing about what it was for. It was not going to answer a Republic call-up. Thirty-one Jedi went anyway, as individuals, without permission of the Council.

Mace Windu went for reasons he did not give the Council but which are not hard to work out. He had acted [[The Death of Palpatine#The first weeks|once without permission]], in 19 BBY, and had been tried for two years and proved right. The Council disowned their action publicly on the day the fleet jumped. It did not expel them, but it has never taken the disavowal back.

The landing on [[Yavin 4]] was unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day at minimal cost. On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify Dathomiri magick.

The sixteen hundred survivors give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. Mace Windu was supposedly among them.

## In 2 ABY

The Ossus memorial gives Windu's date of death as the fourth day. That was a deliberate decision of the Council and it was argued over.
