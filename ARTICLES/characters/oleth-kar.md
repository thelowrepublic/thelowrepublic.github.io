**Oleth Kar** is an Ithorian who both governs the Gordian Reach and sits for it in the Senate, and [[Yavin 4]] lies in his sector. The [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Dathomir-Yavin Accord]] that allied it with Quelii was his work.

## The Yavin 4 case, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The Vess case]]*

Oleth Kar is Governor-Senator of the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Gordian Reach]], which contains [[Yavin 4]]. He retained [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] rather than argue the case himself, on the grounds that a governor pleading for his own sector persuades nobody.

Senator Ilai Vess of the Quelii sector, which contains [[Dathomir]], stood up in the Senate in the second month of 2 ABY and read the [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] permit for the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]] into the record. Quelii voted to leave the Republic the same month. The declaration named several [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] assets as mercenary armies in the pay of the new state. Additionally, on behalf of the Gordian Reach sector as its contracted legal representative, Vess demanded the interdiction fleet be withdrawn immediately and asked for damages in a sum widely described as theatrical. The sum has not come down since.

## Secession and the Accord, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY]]*

Gordian Reach seceded in the third month of 2 ABY quietly and without fanfare. In the following months, Oleth Kar authored the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Dathomir-Yavin Accord]].

Quelii and the Gordian Reach officially signed the Accord in the sixth month of 2 ABY, forming a defensive and economic alliance. Three months later, the Thesme, D'Astan and Kalamith sectors (which lie between Quelii and the Gordian Reach) stopped remitting taxes towards the Republic. None of the three has been given a seat in the Senate for decades. Rumours circulate of increased Collective presence in those sectors and an expanded alliance pact being drafted by Kar. Everyone knows what this means, but the Republic won't acknowledge it, because it would mean the Republic just lost control of another vital hyperspace lane.

## In 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The standoff]]*

Because Seswenna never left, in law, its nine sectors are still entitled to nine Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. Quelii and the Gordian Reach are in the same situation and have taken the opposite view. [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] and Kar sit, speak, table motions and vote. All of it on the strength of a technicality they helped create and describe in public as an absurdity. Neither sector has remitted a credit of tax since it declared secession.

The Republic cannot bill them. Demanding levies from either sector means asserting that they are members in good standing, and a member in good standing is owed an answer on the reparations. Withdrawing the interdiction fleet from [[Yavin 4]] would be read everywhere as recognition, and recognition concedes that the fleet had been sitting in foreign territory for two years. Leaving it there costs nothing and admits nothing, so it stays. Oleth Kar's invitation to remove it is not just a taunt. It is an offer he knows cannot be accepted.
