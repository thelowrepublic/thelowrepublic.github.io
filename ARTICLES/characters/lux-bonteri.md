**Lux Bonteri**, a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]], has sat in the Senate for [[Raxus]] since 14 BBY. In 15 BBY he led the negotiations that ended [[The Long War|the Long War]].

## Background

Lux Bonteri is from Onderon. His mother, Mina Bonteri, sat in the Separatist Senate on Raxus. She pressed for peace talks with the Republic. [[The Long War|Dooku]] had her killed in secret and blamed the Republic. Lux later fought with the rebels who freed Onderon from Separatist rule.

## The Concord of Raxus

*Main article: [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY]]*

The Long War ended in a bargain rather than a surrender. Bonteri led the negotiations, because he was the only person there willing to acknowledge Separatist misgivings as legitimate. His mother had been murdered for arguing for exactly this.

Under the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord]], systems coming back into the Republic kept the right to run their own affairs. The great corporations lost their Senate seats for good. The promises of self-government were kept, but taxes were still collected.

## Senator for Raxus

Bonteri has been Senator for Raxus since 14 BBY, leader of the Rim self-rule bloc, and a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]]. The Core Isolationists agree that the system is broken and add that it is too small for what it carries. Their answer is full withdrawal of the Republic to the Core and the Inner Rim, focus shift towards self-sufficiency of the Core, and free trade with everything beyond it. They want the Mid and Outer Rim to gain full autonomy. The Rim self-rule bloc votes with them constantly. Their case is the most honest about capacity and the least honest about consequence. None of them says aloud what everyone understands. Free trade between the technologically superior Core and the underdeveloped Rim is not equalising. It will result in flagrant abuse, just without laws to protect from it.

He has voted against every renewal of the [[The Shadow War#The Republic at war|emergency powers]] since 0 ABY.

## In 2 ABY

Despite being an advocate for Outer Rim self-reliance, Bonteri has said publicly that he does not accept Senator [[Ilai Vess|Vess]]'s case but knows why it is winning. In his words, [[The Secessions#The Vess case|Vess's case]] is a blatant scrapbook of convenient excuses, all at the behest of a crime lord.
