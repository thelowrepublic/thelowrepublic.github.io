**Luminara Unduli** is at the head of the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]], who hold the Coruscant Temple once more. She was one of the senior masters who came through the [[The Long War|Long War]], and she went back to [[Coruscant]] when the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] split in 0 ABY.

## Background

Luminara Unduli was a Mirialan Jedi Master. In 22 BBY she fought at [[Geonosis]] in [[Mace Windu]]'s strike team. Her Padawan was [[Barriss Offee]]. In the Clone Wars she commanded the 41st Elite Corps, and she went back to Geonosis with Barriss, beside [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to destroy its droid foundries.

## The wars

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The survivors]]*

Because [[The Kamino Findings#The contingency orders|Order 66]] was never sent, the Order kept a generation of senior masters who would have otherwise died within an hour of one another. [[Plo Koon]], [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Aayla Secura]], [[Shaak Ti]], Luminara Unduli, [[Stass Allie]] and [[Ossus Order|Coleman Kcaj]] all came through the [[The Long War|Long War]]. The preserved knowledge and continuity of leadership made the Order's restructuring smoother than it could've otherwise been. None of it prevented what followed.

## The Templar offer, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Jedi Templars#The offer]]*

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the Republic made the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|traditionalists]] were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to [[Coruscant]]. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on [[Ossus]].

## Leading the Templars

Luminara Unduli leads the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]], taking on the old title of [[Yoda|Grand Master]]. She has never pretended her reasons are purely institutional. She trained [[Barriss Offee]], she did not see what was forming in her student, and she has said publicly that she should have. Every Force-sensitive the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] have taken in was taught by Barriss from what Unduli taught her first. A journalist asked her in 1 ABY whether the Templars existed to fight the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] or to fight her former student. She said both, and that she could see no useful distinction. [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Stass Allie]] went with her and currently sit on her council. Both had been generals in the Clone Wars and neither had ever been reconciled to giving it up.

## In 2 ABY

Luminara Unduli leads the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] from the reclaimed [[Coruscant#The Jedi Temple|Coruscant Temple]].
