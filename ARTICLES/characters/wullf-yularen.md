**Wullf Yularen** has directed [[Republic Intelligence]] since 12 BBY. Most accounts give him the credit for bringing [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s wartime dealings to light.

## Background

Yularen was an admiral of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. He was assigned to [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s fleet, and his flagship was the Resolute. At Ryloth he was wounded in the fight against the Separatist blockade. He backed the plan of Skywalker's Padawan, [[Ahsoka Tano]], that broke it.

## Director, 12 BBY

Yularen became director of the reformed [[Republic Intelligence]] in 12 BBY. It is also called the Strategic Information Service, or the SIS.

## The Outland Command file, 11–1 BBY

[[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] resigned his commission in 10 BBY, went home to [[Eriadu]], and was elected governor of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union#Seswenna|Seswenna sector]]. Over the following decade he drew the neighbouring frontier sectors into an efficient, prosperous and orderly bloc. Coruscant put up with the arrangement because Seswenna kept the shipping lanes safe, paid what it owed, and cleaned up its own droid remnants without asking for Republic troops. Yularen opened a file on the Outland Command accounts in 11 BBY and did not close it for nine years.

Yularen closed his file on Tarkin in 2 BBY. In the first weeks of 1 BBY he laid the Outland Command evidence in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths. Yularen has been working with rogue Jedi Knight [[Do'Kass]] for the past decade, the Force-user's unconventional methods producing evidence that would otherwise not see the light of day.

Tarkin understood his position at once. He did not deny the connection. He described it as wartime intelligence work, difficult compromises he was forced to make, and expressed regret that it had been necessary. Within four months he had become the loudest and most convincing voice in the Republic for destroying the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. He was even willing to fully fund and coordinate the manhunt for [[Maul]], as long as the Republic provided troops. He was the man who had seen the danger first, from the inside, and whose warnings had been ignored all this time.

It was an extraordinary performance for the investigators and it mostly worked. He was given command of the operation, and the whole of it would be recorded for Republic supervision. His gracious offer of funding and logistics meant it did not need formal authorisation from the Senate at all. Their hands would be clean, or so they thought.

## In 2 ABY

Yularen is generally credited as the man who exposed Tarkin's machinations during the Long War. Several colleagues privately blame him for a share of [[The Battle of Yavin#The decision|Yavin]], on the argument that when cornered in 1 BBY, someone like Tarkin was always going to do something. Yularen has never disputed that accusation, yet did not resign from his post.

[[Do'Kass]], a Jedi Knight, works for Yularen on no contract anyone has produced. [[Dedra Meero]] meticulously tracked and compiled Anakin Skywalker's [[Anakin Skywalker#The record|forty-one appearances]] in 1 ABY, and presented conclusions about their patterns and effects. The paper was suppressed and she was reassigned to a licensing desk. Yularen read her report anyway and had her recruited into Republic Intelligence within the year. Yularen has said she is the only one who truly reads every data point she is given.
