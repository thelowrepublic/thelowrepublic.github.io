**R2-D2** is the astromech droid who has never left [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s side, from the Long War to the present day. So many unlicensed hands have reworked him that nothing can be read from his memory any more.

## Background

R2-D2 is an astromech droid. He began his service with Queen [[Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]], and in the Clone Wars he served [[Anakin Skywalker]], often with [[C-3PO]].

## With Skywalker, 19 BBY–2 ABY

R2-D2 stayed with [[Anakin Skywalker]] through the [[The Long War|Long War]], [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]], the discharge, the withdrawal and the years since. He has been modified far past anything the specifications allow by a long succession of unlicensed technicians. His memory core is an unrecoverable mess and nobody was able to get coherent data out of him.
