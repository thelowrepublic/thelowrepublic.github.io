**Merrin** is the Dathomirian witch who founded the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] and opened the closed Nightsister practice to men. She runs the order day to day with [[Barriss Offee]].

## The massacre and the return, 21–10 BBY

*Main article: [[Dathomir]]*

Dathomir was massacred by General [[The Long War|Grievous]] in 21 BBY. The Nightsister clans were finished as an organised power and the survivors scattered. [[Maul]] was a son of that world, taken from it as a child and raised as a weapon by a master who threw him away. He came back in 10 BBY and spent four years rebuilding. He cleared the ruins and found survivors across a dozen sectors. The most important of them was Merrin, who had been seven years old when the massacre happened and had spent eleven years as the last practising witch of her clan.

## The Nightborn

*Main article: [[The Nightborn]]*

The order's name was a statement of intent. The Nightsister tradition had been closed to men for four thousand years. The Nightbrothers of the Dathomiri villages had been breeding stock and labour for it. Maul himself had been born into that arrangement, one he would not be willing to fund, so Merrin opened the practice to them. She chose Nightborn because Nightsister no longer described what she was building, and the word spread outward from her branch to cover the whole order. Three traditions were braided together. The [[The Nightborn#Magick|magick]] came from Merrin: illusion, spirit-work, ichor, and the raising of the dead. Sith techniques came from Maul and from what he dug up at over a dozen ancient ruins. Jedi training, meditation, the lightsaber forms and a working knowledge of how the Jedi actually operate were brought in by defectors and taught by [[Barriss Offee]].

## The Nightsisters, 3 BBY

*Main article: [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire]]*

Maul and [[Asajj Ventress]] fought at Nightspire in 3 BBY. Neither died. She left the same night with twenty Dathomiri witches of the fifty present. Two hundred others went with her, Collective personnel, civilians and a handful of former Jedi. She set herself up in the eastern highlands, where she took back the name [[The Nightsisters|Nightsisters]] and the whole of the old practice with it: the closed rite, the female line, the four-thousand-year tradition Merrin had broken. She holds that the Nightborn are not a restoration of Dathomir but a second theft of it. Not because of the inclusion of men in the rites, but because they don't realise that Maul will forsake all of their principles the moment he needs an army. Neither Maul nor the Republic has been able to shift her, and both have decided it is not worth what it would cost to try.

## In 2 ABY

Merrin has not left Dathomiri space in eight years, and has never made an official declaration of loyalty towards the Collective. There is an entire generation of Dathomiri witches that know their ways only thanks to what Merrin taught them.
