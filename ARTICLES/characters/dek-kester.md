**Dek Kester** is a scene analyst for the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]] on [[Coruscant]]. He is also the Walker behind most of the [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]]'s killings on Coruscant, and only [[Do'Kass]] suspects him.

## Scene analyst

Kester has held his post with the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]] for nine years. His casework is exemplary.

## Walker

Kester is a Walker. The [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] holds that the [[Anakin Skywalker#The prophecy|prophecy]] is real and that [[Anakin Skywalker]] is the Chosen One. Its Walkers have killed at least sixty Republic officials in two years. Of the sixty-odd killings, investigators have gone on to establish that roughly seven in ten of the dead were taking money, running something, or covering for someone who was.

Twenty-four of the thirty-odd Path killings on Coruscant are Kester's work. The Path runs at seven in ten accuracy. Kester runs at ten in ten because he doesn't trust the Force; he trusts in his own skill to find a monster to hunt.

## In 2 ABY

Kester has processed four Path scenes in his official job, including one of his own. Nobody suspects him but [[Do'Kass]], who cannot yet prove anything.
