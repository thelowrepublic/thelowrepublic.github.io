**Leia Skywalker** is a senator for the Chommell sector, the daughter of [[Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], and the twin of [[Luke Skywalker]]. By 2 ABY she is the most quoted junior senator in the chamber and one of the most disliked.

## Childhood

Leia Skywalker was born in 19 BBY to [[Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], with her twin, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]]. Their parents took care to keep their children out of their politics. After [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] her father visited the twins and their mother but did not live with them.

Luke was sent to [[Ossus]] at twelve. Leia did not leave [[Naboo]]. She lived with her mother full-time until adulthood, and saw at first hand how random and rare her father's visits were. She grew to resent him much more than Luke did.

Leia is Force-sensitive and not a Jedi by her own choice. She did not pursue full Jedi training at Ossus, but she still received some tutelage, roughly what a youngling would learn and not beyond that. When the twins were young Anakin took them to the planet Ilum, which was the only trip he ever took them on. Leia built a lightsaber there with her brother. Together they found a fused "twin kyber crystal" and split it, and each part went into one of the siblings' lightsabers. Leia has never used her lightsaber in combat, but she still carries it, with a blaster as backup.

## Senator for Chommell

Leia Skywalker took the first Chommell seat three months before Yavin, at eighteen, in 1 BBY. [[Bail Organa]], Chancellor from 19 to 14 BBY, is her political mentor. By 2 ABY, she has supported all three secessions in public and by name, and has voted against every motion condemning them. Her position is that a sector which can feed itself, police itself and defend itself has earned much greater autonomy, and that the Republic's insistence on supervising such sectors is not care but greed.

She has voted against renewing the [[The Shadow War#The Republic at war|emergency powers]] all eight times and argued each time that it should not be necessary. She is the youngest member of the committee overseeing the war, and the only one who has met Senator [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] privately multiple times.

## The Skywalker position

Leia Skywalker is a firm proponent of radical change within the Republic. Her argument is not that the Republic is corrupt. She has read the same reports as everyone else and she thinks the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|charter]] works. That is the whole problem, as she puts it. The rules function exactly as designed, but the thing they are attached to is simply too large and hungry. Every year the machine gets slower, and every year more of the Rim decides it can do better alone. Both of those things are true at once, and neither is anybody's direct fault, which is why nobody will fix it. The goal should always have been to grow sectors into realms that could stand on their own, she says. Instead the Republic built a system that supervises forever and calls it care. [[Padmé Amidala]] wrote the charter that Leia rages against. Her daughter spends most of her working life arguing that it is strangling the thing it was built to protect.

She supports the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Gordian Reach]] reparations in principle, though she has also called the sum theatrical. Her position on the reparations is simple. If the money had gone to the Outer Rim when the Outer Rim needed it, there would have been nothing there for the Collective to build upon. The Outer Rim is becoming self-sufficient and rebellious because the Collective is providing absent services at a fraction of the cost the Republic would demand for it. Paying the reparation, even its vastly exaggerated sum, is the cheapest thing the Republic has been asked to do in twenty years.

She does not defend the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] and has never been careless about the distinction. She calls them criminals in every speech, usually twice. The [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] are harder. She has said publicly that an order which has never been outlawed is not at war with anybody, and that the Republic should say so out loud before it stops being true. She has received three private invitations from [[Barriss Offee]], the first in 1 ABY. She has declined all three and reported all three to the [[Republic Intelligence|SIS]], which is the only reason anyone knows about them.

## The three stances

*Main article: [[Three Stances of the Senate]]*

The [[Three Stances of the Senate|three stances]] pull in three directions and nothing moves. Every emergency renewal passes because the traditionalists want the war and the other two cannot agree on an alternative. Leia Skywalker votes with the reformists on every law. She votes with the isolationists on every question of who should govern the Rim. She has never officially joined any political camp.

## In 2 ABY

[[Quarsh Panaka]], the Governor of Naboo, filed the Chommell sector secession petition seven weeks after [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] left and has never explained it past the filing. The petition is one of the sixty in review. Leia Skywalker holds one of the sector's two Senate seats, and would be expelled from the Senate if the secession ever comes through.

[[C-3PO]], the protocol droid built by Anakin Skywalker, works in Leia Skywalker's office and has been barred from three committee rooms.
