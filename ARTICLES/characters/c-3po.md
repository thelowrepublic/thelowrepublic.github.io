**C-3PO** is a protocol droid built by [[Anakin Skywalker]], who remembers everything ever said in the Skywalker household. That memory was used at the [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] inquiry, and he now serves in [[Leia Skywalker]]'s office.

## Background

C-3PO is a protocol droid that [[Anakin Skywalker]] built from spare parts as a boy on Tatooine. During the Clone Wars he served [[Padmé Amidala]].

## The Murkhana inquiry, 15 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

C-3PO was never memory-wiped. He has a perfect recall of every private conversation the Skywalkers had in their own home. The Murkhana inquiry called him in 15 BBY and data pulled from him ended two careers. He has referred to the proceedings ever since only as "the difficulty."

## In 2 ABY

C-3PO works in [[Leia Skywalker]]'s office and has been barred from three committee rooms.
