**Orson Krennic** runs advanced projects for the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]]. He ran the Geonosis Programme for the Ministry of Security until it was exposed in 11 BBY. He was demoted and moved to Eriadu the following year.

## The Ministry of Security

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#The Geonosis Programme]]*

Superweapon plans of Geonosian design, recovered during the occupation in 18 BBY, became a secret programme under the Ministry of Security, paid for out of a budget line marked planetary defence.

The new [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]] exposed it in 11 BBY at roughly eleven percent built. Chancellor Ars Vehn was immediately impeached. The programme's director, Orson Krennic, was demoted and reassigned to Outland Command. The hull was scrapped. The plans and some Geonosian-made components were never recovered.

## In 2 ABY

Krennic is the director of advanced projects for the Greater Seswenna Union. His work now is moving kalkite through Republic customs or finding something to replace it.
