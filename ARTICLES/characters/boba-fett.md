**Boba Fett** is a bounty hunter, and nobody in his trade has done better in this age. His work grew with the Confederate fugitives and the indicted executives, and grew again in 0 ABY, when the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] put out a price list for [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] personnel.

## Background

Boba Fett is an unaltered clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, born on [[Kamino]] and raised by Jango as his son. Jango was killed by [[Mace Windu]] at the Battle of [[Geonosis]] in 22 BBY. As a boy in the Clone Wars, Boba tried to take revenge on Windu and went on to work as a bounty hunter.

## In 2 ABY

Boba Fett is the most successful bounty hunter of the age, in a market swollen by Confederate fugitives, indicted executives, and since 0 ABY a Republic price list for Shadow Collective personnel that pays better than any government contract in history. The list does not ask what a name on it has done, only whom it works for, and Fett has collected on dock clerks and clinic staff as readily as on killers. He is possibly the happiest man alive.
