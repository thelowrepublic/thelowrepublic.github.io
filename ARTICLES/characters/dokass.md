**Do'Kass** is a Nautolan Jedi Knight who works for [[Wullf Yularen]] of [[Republic Intelligence]], on no contract anyone has produced. Since 1 ABY nearly all his work has been on the [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] killings on [[Coruscant]].

## Jedi Knight

Do'Kass has held no rank in either Jedi body since [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|the demilitarisation]]. He is a rogue Jedi Knight.

## Work for Yularen

Yularen has been working with rogue Jedi Knight Do'Kass for the past decade, the Force-user's unconventional methods producing evidence that would otherwise not see the light of day. In the first weeks of 1 BBY Yularen laid the Outland Command evidence against [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths.

Do'Kass walks into a room and simply knows who is lying. He is rarely wrong, but his 'hunch' is not viable evidence for a report.

## In 2 ABY

Since 1 ABY Do'Kass has worked almost only on the [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] case on [[Coruscant]]. He does not think it is a network. He thinks that most of it is one person.

Twenty-four of the thirty-odd Path killings on Coruscant are the work of [[Dek Kester]]. He is a scene analyst of the [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]]. Nobody suspects him but Do'Kass, who cannot yet prove anything.
