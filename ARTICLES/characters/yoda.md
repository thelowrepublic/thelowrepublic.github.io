**Yoda**, the Jedi Master who came round to the reformists in the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|schism]], died on [[Ossus]] in 7 BBY at close to nine hundred years of age. The last thing he did in public was to turn down the title of Grand Master.

## Background

Yoda was a Jedi Master of great age who sat on the Jedi High Council, and he had trained Jedi for centuries. [[The Long War|Dooku]] had been his Padawan. In 22 BBY Yoda took command of the clone army on [[Kamino]] and led it to [[Geonosis]], where the Clone Wars began. He fought Dooku there, and Dooku fled.

## The schism, 14–8 BBY

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY]]*

Yoda moved towards the reformists over five years. His argument was that the Order's rigidity had been a worse failure than any particular teaching.

## Death, 7 BBY

Yoda died at [[Ossus]], a few years short of nine hundred. His last public act was to refuse the title of Grand Master, on the argument that the Order should never again have one voice speaking for it. He died believing the reforms had held.
