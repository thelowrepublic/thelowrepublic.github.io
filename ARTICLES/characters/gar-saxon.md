**Gar Saxon** commands Death Watch, the Mandalorian part of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. Since [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] took the north of [[Mandalore]] in 1 ABY, his Death Watch has opened the old armouries again and put an army into beskar.

## Background

Gar Saxon is a Human, a Mandalorian of Clan Saxon. He was a member of Death Watch, and during the Clone Wars he commanded the Mandalorian super commandos of [[Maul]]'s Shadow Collective. He commanded Maul's forces on Mandalore during the Siege of Mandalore.

## Death Watch

*Main article: [[Mandalore#The armouries]]*

The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] takes in the Mandalorian Death Watch under Gar Saxon. Gar Saxon's Death Watch fought over the southern half of [[Mandalore]], which is the half that contains the most ruins, and had been working the old vaults without much spare time since 4 BBY.

[[Bo-Katan Kryze]] fully took the north in 1 ABY. It was a real choice, made with her eyes open, and she has never doubted her reasoning. The north holds three quarters of Mandalore's people and most of the industrial infrastructure. In every respect except one, that was the right decision. However, with nobody left to contest the south, Death Watch spent the next year doing what they didn't have the leisure to do in a generation. It reopened the armouries. It refitted an army in beskar. It went back to re-learn techniques for killing Force-users that had survived only because a handful of isolationist sects had never stopped practising them.

## In 2 ABY

Gar Saxon is commander of Death Watch, and holds the southern half of Mandalore and every armoury on it. As conflict on [[Mandalore]] reached a stalemate, Saxon's forces have been aiding Collective operations in greater numbers. Especially on operations that are likely to run afoul of the Templars.
