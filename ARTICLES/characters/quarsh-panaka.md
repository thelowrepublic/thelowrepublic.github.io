**Quarsh Panaka** is the Governor of [[Naboo]] and a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]]. It was he who asked for the Chommell sector to leave the Republic, and he has given no reason since.

## Background

Quarsh Panaka was a captain of the Royal Naboo Security Forces, and head of security to Queen [[Padmé Amidala]]. When the Trade Federation invaded Naboo in 32 BBY, he went with the Queen to Tatooine and Coruscant. He came back with her to Naboo to free it.

## The Chommell petition, 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The petitions]]*

Senator [[Ilai Vess]]'s sector, [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]], voted to leave the Republic in the second month of 2 ABY. Panaka filed the Chommell sector secession petition seven weeks after Quelii left and has never explained it past the filing.

By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review, nearly all of them from Outer Rim sectors. Each of them is being reviewed, because refusing to review them would itself be a decision. The Chommell sector, containing Naboo, filed one of those petitions, and is the only [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]] sector to do so.

## In 2 ABY

[[Leia Skywalker]] holds one of the sector's two Senate seats.
