**Aayla Secura** left the Jedi Order with the withdrawalists in 8 BBY and is among the [[Order of Voss]]. Nobody on [[Ossus]] or among the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] has heard from her since.

## Background

Aayla Secura was a Twi'lek Jedi. In 22 BBY she fought at [[Geonosis]] in [[Mace Windu]]'s strike team. In the Clone Wars she was a Jedi General in command of the 327th Star Corps, with Commander Bly as her second-in-command.

## The withdrawalists, 8 BBY

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY]]*

The [[Order of Voss#The withdrawalists|withdrawalists]] lost outright and did not argue further. Six hundred and thirty-two Jedi resigned in the eleven weeks after the Compact. They gave up their rank, their access, and their lightsabers, and scattered without saying where they were going. Among them was Master Aayla Secura. She had spent six years attached to the men she commanded and had been ordered to leave them. She came out of that decision agreeing with every word the withdrawalists said. A quarter of all the Jedi that survived the war left gradually over the whole reform period, not including the withdrawalist separation. Some became teachers, farmers and freight pilots. Some eventually found their way to [[Dathomir]]. The withdrawalists went somewhere else entirely, and it was five years before anyone found out where.

## The Order of Voss

Master Aayla Secura is among the [[Order of Voss]] and is understood to have some standing there. She has not been seen in Republic space since 8 BBY and has sent no word to anyone she left behind.

## In 2 ABY

Aayla Secura has long since departed to [[Voss]] before the [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|split of 0 ABY]] happened and knows nothing about it. She has sent no word to anyone on [[Ossus]] or in the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] for ten years.
