**Tavia Orn** is a Cathar who joined the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] with their first intake of adults. A former navy gunner, she was found almost by chance in the Force testing after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]].

## Career

Tavia Orn is a [[The Jedi Templars|Templar]] of the first adult intake, with minimal Force-sensitivity. She was a [[The Galactic Republic|Republic Navy]] gunnery officer for six years and was tested on a whim during the post-[[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] sweeps. [[Ossus Order|Ossus]] would not have found her and would not have taken her.

## In 2 ABY

Despite her minimal Force-sensitivity, Orn handles a lightsaber like a woman who trained with it since childhood. Her raw talent outskilled most of her instructors before she even met them, and she has been one of the first people the Templars send into the field when a confrontation with the Nightborn is likely.
