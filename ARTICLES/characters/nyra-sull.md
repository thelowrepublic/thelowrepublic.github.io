**Nyra Sull** is a Twi'lek [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] and a former Jedi, who reached [[Dathomir]] in [[Barriss Offee]]'s first intake. In 16 BBY the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation]] took her from [[Nal Kaddan]], a world of the Kastolar sector in what became the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]].

## Nal Kaddan, 16 BBY

Nyra Sull was serving on [[Nal Kaddan]] when the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation]] pulled her off it with four days' notice. She spent two of those days explaining to the population that it was an administrative decision. Nal Kaddan fell to the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] a year after she left it.

## Leaving the Order, 16–9 BBY

She went back to the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] and spent six years watching it fall apart from within. She left without warning in 10 BBY and reached [[Dathomir]] in 9 BBY, in Barriss' first intake.

## In 2 ABY

Regarded as one of the best field commanders the Nightborn have available. She can mobilise other Nightborn into action like no other, but she has never taken an assignment from the Collective that did not fully align with her own moral outlook. She has halted a field operation twice to prevent deaths of Jedi padawans.
