**The Kamino Findings** came out of the Republic's audit of the Kaminoan clone contract in 18 BBY. Chief among them was Order 66, a command hidden in every [[Clones|clone]] trooper to turn on his Jedi officers.

## The audit

The investigation into Palpatine was exhaustive and it was carried out by accountants. Every contract, payment and appointment made under his authority across thirteen years was opened up again. The largest single item in the Republic's history was the Kaminoan clone contract. It had been ordered in the name of the murdered Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas and run after his death by [[The Long War|Dooku]].

Republic auditors reached [[Kamino]] in 18 BBY with the power to demand any record they wanted. Jedi Master [[Shaak Ti]] went with them. She read the specifications for what the Republic's army had been built out of. She had overseen clone production for three years, and in 21 BBY she had been assured that the trooper Fives was suffering from a tumour, which caused a psychotic episode.

The Kaminoans had broken no law of their own. They kept meticulous paperwork proving they had followed their client's instructions exactly, and they produced those instructions.

## The contingency orders

Every clone trooper carried a small organic chip put in during growth, holding one hundred and fifty standing orders. Order 66 told the troops to kill every Jedi officer over them. At once, on a single transmission, with no confirmation and no appeal.

The finding was published within six days over the objections of the Ministry of Defence and without their approval. To this day, nobody has been able to establish who gave the order to release the evidence to the public.

Kamino findings went a long way towards restoring the Jedi in the public mind, since the Order had been one signal away from extinction. It arrived during [[Mace Windu|Windu]]'s [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The Windu trial, 19–17 BBY|trial]], where it did more for his defence than anything his lawyers managed. The defence rested on three dead Jedi, the evidence from Palpatine's chambers, [[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]]'s testimony, and in the final year the Kamino findings.

It also settled one thing permanently. The Grand Army of the Republic had been a Sith weapon built to a Sith design, and no amount of surgery would make it anything else. What it also meant is that many people in the galaxy would never trust a clone again.

Fives was cleared posthumously in 17 BBY. Nobody was punished, since every officer involved in his death had been acting on a faked medical report.

## The Reconstitution, 18–12 BBY

*Main article: [[The Manumission Act]]*

The chips came out between 18 and 17 BBY and every surviving trooper was cleared. Production was stopped in 17 BBY and the Kaminoan contract was cancelled the same year.

## The ones still growing

Although the main production contract was cancelled, the Kaminoans were kept on under a stripped-down agreement. Accusations and allegations about the immorality of their cloning practices were quietly dropped, in exchange for the Kaminoans taking on the duty to see the [[The Manumission Act#The ones still growing|underaged clones]] to adulthood, because they already had the facilities and know-how to raise them. The work was done at cost and without enthusiasm on either side.
