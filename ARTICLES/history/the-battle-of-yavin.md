**The Battle of Yavin** was the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]'s assault on [[Maul]]'s dig on [[Yavin 4]] in 0 ABY, under the command of [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. A force of eleven thousand four hundred elite troopers went in badly briefed, and thirty-one [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi]] went without the permission of the Council. In six days nine thousand eight hundred of the troops died, over a thousand of them killed by Republic guns, and twenty-six of the Jedi were killed and raised. Yavin 4 was never retaken, and the calendar was reset within the year.

## The dig

[[Maul]] registered a dig on the [[Yavin 4|fourth moon of Yavin Prime]] with the Republic [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] in 5 BBY. It went through a shell company, on the correct forms, with the correct fees, renewed every year. It was one of eleven hundred such permits active in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] that year and drew no attention whatsoever. The Massassi temples there were raised by the Sith of Naga Sadow's day and abandoned for four thousand years. They are also where Exar Kun put his spirit at the end of the Great Sith War. He escaped the destruction of his body by pouring himself into the stone, and he stayed there for four thousand years. Maul had not gone to Yavin for riches or power. He had gone to find out how Kun did it.

Maul is fifty-six as of 2 ABY. He has named no successor and has turned the question aside for a decade. His own lieutenants read this as vanity or weakness. It is in fact both, in a sense. Naming a successor admits there will need to be one. What he found at Yavin is a method for immortality for a price he will not pay. What Kun did held him to a place. Four thousand years fixed to a temple he could not leave is not survival as Maul understands the word. It is an eternity of imprisonment.

He has spent seven years looking for a version of the rite that does not carry that price. However, he has still uncovered troves of Sith knowledge for the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]]'s benefit. Nobody in the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] knows Maul's end goal. Neither [[Merrin]] nor [[Barriss Offee]] knows it. Both have privately concluded that he is simply afraid to name an heir because it would mean acknowledging his own age, and neither of them is wrong about that. As Maul's case proves, the legacy of paranoia does seem to run from master to apprentice among the Sith.

## The decision

[[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] closed his file on [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] in 2 BBY. In the first weeks of 1 BBY he laid the Outland Command evidence in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths. Yularen has been working with rogue Jedi Knight [[Do'Kass]] for the past decade, the Force-user's unconventional methods producing evidence that would otherwise not see the light of day. Tarkin understood his position at once. He did not deny the connection. He described it as wartime intelligence work, difficult compromises he was forced to make, and expressed regret that it had been necessary. Within four months he had become the loudest and most convincing voice in the Republic for destroying the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. He was even willing to fully fund and coordinate the manhunt for [[Maul]], as long as the Republic provided troops. He was the man who had seen the danger first, from the inside, and whose warnings had been ignored all this time.

It was an extraordinary performance for the investigators and it mostly worked. He was given command of the operation, and the whole of it would be recorded for Republic supervision. And no doubt, what they hoped would be future propaganda. His gracious offer of funding and logistics meant it did not need formal authorisation from the Senate at all. Their hands would be clean, or so they thought.

## The Jedi who went

The Council refused to be involved. The debate ran for four days and the refusal was not close. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] had abolished its own military rank sixteen years earlier and had spent eight years arguing about what it was for. It was not going to answer a Republic call-up. Thirty-one Jedi went anyway, as individuals, without permission of the Council.

[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] went because he had thirty years of [[Maul]] behind him. He understood better than anyone alive what Maul would become with recovered Sith knowledge in his hands. [[Mace Windu]] went for reasons he did not give the Council but which are not hard to work out. He had acted [[The Death of Palpatine#The first weeks|once without permission]], in 19 BBY, and had been tried for two years and proved right. The Council disowned their action publicly on the day the fleet jumped. It did not expel them, but it has never taken the disavowal back.


## Six days

### The briefing

The force went in badly briefed. What [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] knew about [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] capabilities was extensive, but he could not pass it on without further incriminating himself. So the briefing described a fortified criminal stronghold held by an army of [[Mandalore|Mandalorians]] and mercenaries. It did not describe a Sith nexus or [[The Nightborn|the witches]]. The relevant pages of the intelligence file were held back by Tarkin's people on the grounds of protecting a source. So working off of this, the Republic sent a force of eleven thousand four hundred elite troopers, the best of what they had available and what they could spare. The landing on [[Yavin 4]] was unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day at minimal cost. On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify [[The Nightborn#Magick|Dathomiri magick]].

[[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived at Yavin 4 the day before the Republic fleet. Nobody sent him. Nobody knew he was coming. He had been seen four weeks earlier on Umbara and had given no indication of any destination. He fought at the temple complex on the second day. Most of the soldiers around him did not know who he was. Some officers did, and could do nothing about it. On the third night, when the ground force came apart, he was on the eastern approach.

### What the survivors describe

The sixteen hundred survivors give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. [[Mace Windu]] was supposedly among them.

The Sith nexus did not confine itself to Force-users. This is the single most important thing in the Yavin record and the Republic has never known what to do with it. Eleven thousand four hundred ordinary soldiers were on that moon. Every survivor was tested afterwards, and none showed any measurable Force sensitivity, nothing that could be trained. From the third night onward almost all of them felt it. The choking terror of the dark side.

The descriptions converge on drowning. Weight on the chest. Cold in the mouth. A pressure that came from underneath and rotted their minds. Men who had never in their lives had an unusual experience reported the same one within an hour of each other, across forty kilometres of jungle. A hundred and ninety survivors have since been diagnosed with psychiatric conditions the Republic medical service has no scientific name for.

And in the middle of all the horrors, they saw Him. Two hundred and sixty of them describe it the same way and none of them can say what they mean by it. Skywalker did not glow, and nobody claims that he did. What they say is that they knew where he was, at any distance, through rock. The closer they got to him the more the pressure eased. Following him was the only navigation anyone had for three days. He walked them to the shuttles. Then he went back and did it again. The Jedi accounts are the ones that unsettle the Order.

Five Jedi survived. All five have said, separately and in almost the same words, that what they felt was not a Jedi. A Jedi in the Force is a light. It pushes at the dark and the dark pushes back. You can feel the effort of it. You can feel it costing something. You can feel when the light is snuffed out.

Skywalker was not pushing at anything. What they describe is emptiness. Not absence in the sense of a hole in a wall, but a space so completely serene that the darkness could not reach into it. It had no edges to press on and nothing inside to work against. He was not winning, because he was not even fighting. He was simply a space the Sith nexus could not reach, and as he moved, the people followed him out. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] has been asked about this eleven times and has answered the same. He said he had known Anakin Skywalker for thirty-two years and had never felt anything remotely like it. In private conversations that were never recorded, he said one thing: that whatever Anakin was supposed to become, everyone has just witnessed it.

### The rest of it

Obi-Wan Kenobi held the northern landing zone for nineteen hours and gave the orders that had to be given there. He was fifty-seven at the time.

On the sixth day Tarkin ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most Nightborn left the moon hours earlier. [[Maul]] was already gone by day four.

Anakin got people out constantly, seemingly without partaking in any food or sleep himself. He was still getting people out on the sixth day, inside the fire zone, after the shelling order had been given and while it was being carried out. He stopped a bombardment volley mid-flight and held it still for thirty minutes.

Of the sixteen hundred who came off that moon, six hundred escaped because of him. What the Republic will never publicly admit is that he helped the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] as well. Around a hundred of them were saved, civilians and security who were only meant to protect the dig. It is the one fact out of Yavin that never reached the public. Senator [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] does not know it. If she did, the Republic would have no case left at all. Anakin met Kenobi on the third day. They spoke for about a minute and hugged. Neither has ever said what they talked about. [[Luke Skywalker]] was in the northern landing zone, and did not find his father there.

## Aftermath

Nine thousand eight hundred elite Republic troops were dead, of whom over a thousand were killed by Republic guns. [[Yavin 4]] was never retaken and is still surrounded by an interdiction fleet. The tragedy was devastating because it was recorded and the footage was all over HoloNet within hours. For the past nineteen years, the Republic tried to build an image of itself in which a great danger had been faced, defeated and legislated against. Yavin ended that narrative in six days, which the Republic will never recover from. The calendar was reset within the year, first by the news services and finally by law. There was no longer any useful way to describe the period that had just closed except as the time before this tragedy.
