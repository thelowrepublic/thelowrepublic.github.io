**The Secessions** are the departures from the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] that followed [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]]. In the fourth month of 1 ABY Seswenna voted to leave, seven neighbouring sectors followed, and together they formed the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]]. In 2 ABY Quelii and the Gordian Reach left after Senator [[Ilai Vess]] read the Yavin 4 dig permit into the Senate record, and they formed the Dathomir-Yavin Accord. The Republic will not recognise any of them, and by the end of 2 ABY over sixty more petitions to leave sat in review.

## Seswenna, 1 ABY

The [[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing|bad briefing]] was established in the last months of 0 ABY. The investigators worked from the log of what had been withheld rather than from the missing pages themselves. The Outland Command's secret arrangement with [[Maul]] was published alongside it. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] was summoned to answer for both, but Seswenna did not hand him over.

The sector had been run by Tarkin for eleven years. It was prosperous, orderly and safe. It had cleaned up its own droid remnants without Republic help. It had successfully pushed back against Collective and Hutt incursions. The people of Seswenna saw the Core's investigation as an attempt to tarnish the name of the man who had given them almost twenty years of stability. The Republic lost almost ten thousand soldiers due to its own incompetence and needed someone to blame. Most of the sector believed this sincerely, and all news that reached Eriadu through legal channels reinforced their stance. Seswenna's government voted to leave the Republic in the fourth month of 1 ABY. Seven neighbouring sectors supported it and voted for their own secessions as well, soon after forming a sector confederation, the independent [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]].

There was no clause in the charter for the Republic to point at and none to break, so there was also no real way to stop it. Tarkin had spent twenty years building the largest concentration of military strength outside the Core. The Republic was fighting a new kind of war, and mounting a retaliatory force against Seswenna would only make things worse. The Senate condemned the withdrawal, refused to recognise it, and did nothing else. Almost all of the Core still trades with the Union, as the Eriadu hyperspace crossroads is too vital to simply abandon. The trade runs mostly one way. The Union buys more shipments from the Inner Rim every quarter, much of it rare minerals. 

Everyone with adequate political acumen knows why the Union works, and it is not because it's an autocratic military state (though many wannabe tyrants claim it is). It succeeds because it is younger than the Republic, its structures are appropriate for its smaller size, and it has focused on internal subjugation instead of outward expansion. So far.

Tarkin is now sixty-six. He holds the title of Governor-General of the Greater Seswenna Union and has never been convicted of anything by any court. He has given two long interviews about Yavin. In both of them he expressed deep regret but accepted no responsibility. In the realm of galactic politics, everyone noticed the Seswenna precedent. By the end of 1 ABY, at least seventy-eight sectors had formally asked their own lawyers and Senate representatives whether they could do the same. None has gone further than asking at that time. Except two.

## Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY

### The Vess case

Senator [[Ilai Vess]] of the Quelii sector, which contains [[Dathomir]], stood up in the Senate in the second month of 2 ABY and read the [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] permit for the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]] into the record. Her case ran as follows. The site had been legally established for five years, registered, renewed and fully in order. No war had been declared on it. Nobody had obtained a warrant. Nobody had been given notice. A Republic fleet under Republic officers had attacked a licensed dig in a sector close to her own with no legal authority to do it. The people at the site had defended themselves with what they had, which she admitted was unusual, but which she would not condemn. The people of Yavin 4 were victims of unprovoked aggression and were within their right to repel the aggressors by whatever means necessary.

She then read more into the record. The [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] findings. The Outland Command arrangement. The deaths of [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Senator Fang Zar and the Ord Mantell magistrate]]. The [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers|droid papers]] of 6 BBY, which she read out in full, and which took most of an afternoon. And the names of the [[The Battle of Yavin#The Jedi who went|thirty-one rogue Jedi]]. The Republic's answer was that the Jedi at Yavin had been private individuals acting alone and that the Order had disowned them within hours. Vess replied that the Republic had made their surviving commander [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|a general eleven weeks later]], and that it had since put twenty-one hundred more Jedi into uniform and handed them the [[Coruscant#The Jedi Temple|Coruscant Temple]]. She said that a government which promotes the officers of an operation it calls unauthorised, and then raises an army out of their colleagues, has already told the Senate what it really thinks of that operation.

Quelii voted to leave the Republic the same month. The declaration named several [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] assets as mercenary armies in the pay of the new state. Additionally, on behalf of the Gordian Reach sector as its contracted legal representative, Vess demanded the interdiction fleet be withdrawn immediately and asked for damages in a sum widely described as theatrical. The sum has not come down since. A month later the Gordian Reach itself seceded as well, quietly and without fanfare. The two sectors formed a defensive and economic alliance, the Dathomir-Yavin Accord, in the sixth month of 2 ABY. Three months later, the Thesme, D'Astan and Kalamith sectors (which lie between Quelii and the Gordian Reach) stopped remitting taxes towards the Republic. None of the three has been given a seat in the Senate for decades. Rumours circulate of increased Collective presence in those sectors and an expanded alliance pact being drafted by [[Oleth Kar]], senator of the Gordian Reach. Everyone knows what this means, but the Republic won't acknowledge it, because it would mean the Republic just lost control of another vital hyperspace lane.

The Republic has not found an effective reply. It cannot prove a registered permit was not registered or that the attack was authorised when no order authorising it exists. It cannot claim that Murkhana and Ord Mantell did not happen. It cannot object to the use of the Force in war while employing Obi-Wan Kenobi as a general, and that is before anyone mentions the extensive use of Jedi during the Clone Wars. The Republic can say that Quelii's government is exploited by a criminal organisation. This seems entirely true yet impossible to prove, and forty years of Republic negligence in the Outer Rim would only make that argument a statement of the Republic's own incompetence.

Vess does not work for the Shadow Collective. She was born a Dathomirian, but was raised on [[Coruscant]] and never joined the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] in any capacity. She is a legitimately elected senator, and she was the Gordian Reach's counsel in a case that sector was entitled to bring. What she did with the facts afterwards was her own decision. She has made a judgement about who can actually protect the people who elected her, and she has never once faltered in that stance.

### The petitions

By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review, nearly all of them from [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] sectors. Over a dozen of them either directly border the Greater Seswenna Union or are in close proximity to it, most notably the Javin sector. The rest are isolated. Most are from systems that could not hold their own space for a week, not without the Collective's help at least. Each of them is being reviewed, because refusing to review them would itself be a decision. The Chommell sector, containing [[Naboo]], filed one of those petitions, and is the only [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]] sector to do so.

## The standoff

The Republic will not recognise any of it. The refusal cost nothing in 1 ABY and now has become the most expensive decision the Republic has made. Because Seswenna never left, in law, its eight sectors are still entitled to eight Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] does not need the room anymore, though it is a weapon that is loaded for him to use. Quelii and the Gordian Reach are in the same situation and have taken the opposite view. [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] and [[Oleth Kar|Kar]] sit, speak, table motions and vote. All of it on the strength of a technicality they helped create and describe in public as an absurdity. Neither sector has remitted a credit of tax since it declared secession.

The Republic cannot bill them. Demanding levies from either sector means asserting that they are members in good standing, and a member in good standing is owed an answer on the reparations. Withdrawing the interdiction fleet from [[Yavin 4]] would be read everywhere as recognition, and recognition concedes that the fleet had been sitting in foreign territory for two years. Leaving it there costs nothing and admits nothing, so it stays. Oleth Kar's invitation to remove it is not just a taunt. It is an offer he knows cannot be accepted.

The declaration is the part that cannot be conceded. Seswenna, Quelii and the Gordian Reach did not petition. They announced. Sixty sectors are currently asking permission, and the day the Republic recognises a sector that never asked, asking stops being necessary. Every course open to the Senate arrives at the same place at a different speed. It has chosen the one that arrives slowest.
