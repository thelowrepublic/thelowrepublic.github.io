**The Death of Palpatine** took place in 19 BBY, in the Supreme Chancellor's private office. [[Mace Windu]] killed the Chancellor there while [[Anakin Skywalker]] stood by.

## The Chancellor's office, 19 BBY

Four Jedi entered the Supreme Chancellor's private office to arrest him on suspicion of being a Sith Lord. Agen Kolar, Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were dead within nine seconds. [[Mace Windu]] duelled Palpatine alone, disarmed him, and forced him against the window.

[[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived. He did not intervene. Windu struck.

Skywalker gave his account many times over the following decades and never changed it. He described a failure to decide in time, not any noble conviction. Palpatine had promised him knowledge that would prevent his wife's death in childbirth. That promise was extinguished in front of him. [[Padmé Amidala]] survived the birth of twins four months later. Whether the danger or the promise to avoid it had ever been genuine was never established, but Skywalker did not stop considering the question.

## The limits of what he knew

[[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]]'s testimony destroyed Palpatine's reputation within a week. It did not dismantle what Palpatine had built, because Skywalker did not know its full shape.

He could establish that Palpatine was Darth Sidious, that [[The Long War|Dooku]] had been his apprentice, and that the Separatist crisis had been steered from both ends. He knew nothing else. Not the [[The Kamino Findings#The contingency orders|Kaminoan contingency orders]]. Not the Sifo-Dyas commission. Not the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The shrine, 18 BBY|Sith shrine]] beneath the Jedi Temple. Not the name of a single one of Palpatine's people outside the Chancellery. Those were found later by [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|accountants]], and the finding of them shaped the age more than the killing had.

## The first weeks

[[Mace Windu]] presented himself at the Judiciary Building at dawn and handed over his lightsaber. He appears to have considered this an obvious duty rather than a political act. Any other course of action, in his mind, would've resulted in open conflict between the Jedi and the Republic. What he did settled an important question before anyone could ask it. The Jedi would answer to [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The Windu trial, 19–17 BBY|ordinary courts]] like anyone else. The Order never managed to take that back.

Vice Chair [[Mas Amedda]] took over as acting Chancellor. He spent his six weeks in the post bargaining for his own protection from prosecution. He did it with such patient self-interest that he held up the business of replacing Palpatine by two months.

For eleven hours on the second night, the Coruscant Guard ringed the [[Coruscant#The Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] while the Senate argued about whether it had just watched a coup. A later inquiry established that the Guard's commander had received no orders of any kind. He had acted on his own initiative to make sure nobody would try to escalate the situation.

Palpatine's body showed that the dark side had worked itself into him over many years. A red lightsaber was recovered at the scene. Roughly a third of the galactic population never accepted any of it, and still does not. Most of them never saw a Jedi in their life, but Palpatine's face and promises were on every holoscreen.

Roughly a third of the galaxy still holds that the Jedi murdered a Chancellor and invented a Sith Lord afterwards to explain it. That view is not treated as fringe. It has senators, and it has had a great many more of them since [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]].

## The escape of Maul

Four days before the killing, the Zabrak warlord [[Maul]] had been captured on [[Mandalore]] by Commander [[Ahsoka Tano]] and the 501st Legion. He was put aboard a Venator-class Star Destroyer bound for [[Coruscant]].

He was the only prisoner in the galaxy who understood immediately what Palpatine's death meant. He had known his former master's identity for over a decade and had been dismissed as a lunatic every time he said so.

When the news of Palpatine's death reached the fleet, its chain of command was in disarray. There were recall orders, competing authorities, rumours of a Jedi coup, and a Senate demanding the return of every capital ship in the Core. Ahsoka was temporarily disarmed and detained. Maul escaped in the confusion, tearing apart half of the Star Destroyer in the process, and reached Mandalorian space within the month.

When life support started failing, Tano was released from confinement, but prioritised saving lives over pursuing Maul. She does not regret her choice, but still views the operation as one of the greatest failures of her life.
