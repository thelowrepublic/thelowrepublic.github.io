The **Jedi Templars** are the Jedi who went back to [[Coruscant]] in 0 ABY, accepting the terms the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] offered the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]]. [[Luminara Unduli]] leads them, and in two years they have changed their teaching a great deal.

## The offer

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] made the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|traditionalists]] were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to [[Coruscant]]. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on [[Ossus]].

## Name and doctrine

The Coruscant body took the name Jedi Templars within the year. It began as an insult from [[Ossus Order|Ossus]]. Once it was clear the insult was not going away, they took it as their own. Their doctrine has moved a long way in two years and is still moving. Rank is formal again and worn. Training has been reweighted towards combat, with the older contemplative programme cut back to bare necessities. A new addition to the curriculum, mandatory training in criminology, interrogation techniques and Force-forensics, has produced Jedi that are expert at combating the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]].

The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Ossus Compact]] has been set aside entirely. The Templars hold that loosening the attachment rules produced defections that reinforced the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]], and they harshly enforce the old ways of discipline. However, they have begun taking adult recruits, mostly from Republic service families, which the Order has not done in eight hundred years. The Templar recruiters are constantly scouring the galaxy for Jedi that left the Order in the decade of 18 to 8 BBY, trying to convince them to protect the Republic once again.

They also moved back into a [[Coruscant#The Jedi Temple|Temple]] with a [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The shrine, 18 BBY|Sith shrine]] under it. The shrine was sealed and reconsecrated in the first month and the Templars consider the matter closed. Ossus raises it in every communication between the two bodies without fail, and has been asked to stop pestering.

The Compact stood for eight years and people used it. The Jedi at Ossus married. Others found the families they had been taken from. Many of them are still living with them. The Templars who came out of that generation either made no such ties or left them behind to go to Coruscant. It is not unheard of for a Templar to eventually have to face down their own resentful family, now wearing Nightborn robes. To the Templars, such tragedies only reinforce their stance on attachments being a slow-acting poison.

## Leadership

[[Luminara Unduli]] leads the Templars, taking on the old title of [[Yoda|Grand Master]]. She has never pretended her reasons are purely institutional. She trained [[Barriss Offee]], she did not see what was forming in her student, and she has said publicly that she should have. Every Force-sensitive the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] have taken in was taught by Barriss from what Unduli taught her first. A journalist asked her in 1 ABY whether the Templars existed to fight the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] or to fight her former student. She said both, and that she could see no useful distinction. [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Stass Allie]] went with her and currently sit on her council. Both had been generals in the Clone Wars and neither had ever been reconciled to giving it up.

## In 2 ABY

[[Tavia Orn]] is a Templar of the first adult intake, with minimal Force-sensitivity. She was a Republic Navy gunnery officer for six years and was tested on a whim during the post-Yavin sweeps. [[Ossus Order|Ossus]] would not have found her and would not have taken her.

[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] is not a Templar and has refused to become one three times. He is not at Ossus, which regards his commission as precisely the error it left Coruscant to avoid. He holds a Republic rank. He commands Republic soldiers and a few dozen Jedi who followed him rather than either faction. He belongs to nothing.

[[Quinlan Vos]] reads the history of objects by touch, which is why the Templars chose him to infiltrate the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] in 1 ABY and find out about the [[The Shadow War#What each side fields|holocrons]] they unearthed. He has sent information out at irregular intervals ever since. He has sent more information to Kenobi privately. Nobody has reached a settled view if Vos is still their asset. His well-known propensity to disregard Jedi creed is troublesome, but it has given credence to his alibi as a defector. His problematic attachment to [[Asajj Ventress]], and over a dozen unsuccessful attempts to contact her, make his real loyalty and priorities an open question.

The Templars received the [[Order of Voss]]'s warning about [[Baylan Skoll]] in 2 ABY, in time.
