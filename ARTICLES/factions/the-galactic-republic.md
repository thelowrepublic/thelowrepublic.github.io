The **Galactic Republic** holds about eighty billion people in a thousand sectors. Since the death of [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]] in 19 BBY it has been [[The Republic Remade|remade]] under a new charter. Since 0 ABY it has fought the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] against the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], a war the Senate never declared. Eleven sectors have declared [[The Secessions|secession]], and the Republic recognises none of it.

## The charter

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY]]*

The Constitutional Convention of 13 to 11 BBY wrote the charter the Republic now runs under. [[Padmé Amidala]] chaired it. It met under a Security government, which is the main reason it produced rules with teeth instead of a statement of good intentions.

What it achieved: emergency powers now expired automatically after ninety days unless the Senate voted to keep them, with no exceptions written in for any reason. Corporations were barred from holding Senate seats. The army and navy became permanent and answered to a civilian ministry rather than to the Chancellor. The courts were put out of the Chancellor's reach. And a standing Investigative Office was set up with the power to demand any record the government held.

The Convention also chose to say nothing whatsoever about how a sector might leave the Republic. The argument that won was that writing down a way out is an invitation to use it. So nothing was written down, keeping the status quo of no legal way to secede from the Republic. This turned out to prevent nothing.

## The Chancellors

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#Administrations]]*

- 19 BBY (six weeks): [[Mas Amedda]], acting
- 19–14 BBY: [[Bail Organa]]
- 14–11 BBY: Ars Vehn
- 11 BBY – 0 ABY: [[Mon Mothma]]
- 0 ABY – present: [[Sevrin Dal]]

A Chancellor can be removed by the Senate on a two-thirds vote. Vehn is the only one it has ever managed. He was impeached in 11 BBY, when the Investigative Office exposed the [[The Republic Remade#The Geonosis Programme|Geonosis Programme]]. Three motions have been filed against Dal in two years. The best of them reached forty-one percent. All three came from the [[Three Stances of the Senate#The Rim self-rule bloc|Rim self-rule bloc]], and all three raised the number of sectors thinking about leaving.

Dal is a Crystanian Rybet and a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Traditionalist]]. Dal was elected six weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] on an open promise of revenge. He won with one sentence: the Republic would find the people responsible for Yavin and finish them. Two years on he has finished nobody.

## The Senate

*Main article: [[Three Stances of the Senate]]*

The Senate has settled into three political positions since Yavin and the secessions. They are the Traditionalists, the Republic Reformists and the Core Isolationists. None of them is a party in the old sense and all three cut across sector lines. The Rim self-rule bloc votes with the Core Isolationists constantly. The three pull in three directions and nothing moves.

Chancellor Sevrin Dal asked for [[The Shadow War#The Republic at war|emergency powers]] eight days after the last survivors came home from Yavin. The Senate granted them and has renewed them eight times. Every emergency renewal passes because the traditionalists want the war and the other two cannot agree on an alternative.

Because Seswenna never left, in law, its eight sectors are still entitled to eight Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii and the Gordian Reach]] took the opposite view. [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] and [[Oleth Kar|Kar]] sit, speak, table motions and vote. Neither sector has remitted a credit of tax since it declared secession.

## The ministries

The Ministry of the Interior ran the [[The Census of 4 BBY|census]] and reviews the secession petitions. Superweapon plans of [[Geonosis|Geonosian]] design, recovered during the occupation in 18 BBY, became a secret programme under the Ministry of Security, paid for out of a budget line marked planetary defence.

The Senate never declared war and could not have. In 0 ABY there was no state to declare it against. The [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] is the only name anyone has for it. The name came from a news broadcast in the first weeks and moved into the Senate record within the year. By 2 ABY the Ministry uses it in its own paperwork.

## The Investigative Office

The Investigative Office is also called the Office of the Inquiry. The new Investigative Office exposed the [[The Republic Remade#The Geonosis Programme|Geonosis Programme]] in 11 BBY at roughly eleven percent built. The Investigative Office published the [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers|droid papers]] in 6 BBY, together with the memoranda showing who had ordered them suppressed. Nothing has been done with this. There is no committee, no proposal and no law. The question of what exactly was scrapped under the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord of Raxus]] is regarded on Coruscant as settled, in the sense that nobody will raise it.

## The Bureau of Antiquities

[[Maul]] registered a dig on the fourth moon of Yavin Prime with the Republic Bureau of Antiquities in 5 BBY. It went through a shell company, on the correct forms, with the correct fees, renewed every year. It was one of eleven hundred such permits active in the Outer Rim that year and drew no attention whatsoever.

Senator Ilai Vess of the Quelii sector, which contains Dathomir, stood up in the Senate in the second month of 2 ABY and read the Bureau of Antiquities permit for the [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Yavin 4 dig]] into the record. The site had been legally established for five years, registered, renewed and fully in order. It was the start of [[The Secessions#The Vess case|the Vess case]].

## The armed forces

The Senate began raising the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]] in 18 BBY, within weeks of the Kamino findings. The Republic has eight million under arms, one citizen in every ten thousand. A marshal commands the garrison of one sector. Above the marshals sit the generals, who answer to the civilian ministry and do not take the field, although that was not always the case. [[Anakin Skywalker]] commanded at Murkhana as a boots-on-the-ground general in 16 BBY. The Constitutional Convention of 11 BBY ended the practice for good. No general has led troops in person since, with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as the sole exception, and his commission had to be written specially to allow it.

The Core is fully garrisoned, and around half of the Inner Rim is too, mostly along its borders. Beyond that, in the Mid and Outer Rim, the Corps only garrisons tactically vital worlds and chokepoints along important trade lanes. Most of the Corps simply stands guard. The largest postings face the borders of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]] and the Collective foothold in [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii and Gordian Reach]]. The interdiction fleet at [[Yavin 4]] is the only Republic force inside either sector, and it cannot leave. The Shadow War itself is fought mostly by the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers]].

Withdrawing the interdiction fleet from Yavin 4 would be read everywhere as recognition, and recognition concedes that the fleet had been sitting in foreign territory for two years. Leaving it there costs nothing and admits nothing, so it stays.

## Republic Intelligence

*Main article: [[Republic Intelligence]]*

Director [[Wullf Yularen]] of the reformed Strategic Information Service, known to everyone as Republic Intelligence or SIS, opened a file on the Outland Command accounts in 11 BBY and did not close it for nine years. Yularen has directed it since 12 BBY. Inside [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]'s borders the Collective's conduct has been immaculate. No extortion, no seizures, no unexplained deaths. Republic Intelligence has spent two years looking for one clean incident of Collective aggression against the Republic inside Quelii and has not produced one. Outside those borders the syndicate has done all of it, constantly, rampaging through thousands of systems.

## The Rim

The Republic's strength was never its army or fleet. It was that a thousand sectors believed membership was safer than leaving. Nothing enforces that belief. There is no garrison in most of the Rim and there never has been. Order holds because the Republic has spent centuries being the benevolent authority that does not come for you.

After the Convention, the Outer Rim was still short of seats and short of policing, and still right in believing that the Core never thought about it. The Outer Rim sends almost no recruits to the Corps, because working for the Collective just pays better there.

The Republic can say that [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]'s government is exploited by a criminal organisation. This seems entirely true, but forty years of Republic negligence in the Outer Rim would only make that argument a statement of the Republic's own incompetence. Across much of the Rim, joining the Collective has become the only form of protest that gets an answer. The Republic still has people who would die for its noble ideals, and many have, but out there it is the other kind of official that people remember.

## In 2 ABY

Chancellor Dal is blatantly pushing for war against [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]], but has not managed to sway the Senate just yet. By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review, nearly all of them from Outer Rim sectors.

The Republic will not recognise any of the secessions. The refusal cost nothing in 1 ABY and now has become the most expensive decision the Republic has made. Seswenna, Quelii and the Gordian Reach did not petition. They announced. Sixty sectors are currently asking permission, and the day the Republic recognises a sector that never asked, asking stops being necessary. Every course open to the Senate arrives at the same place at a different speed. It has chosen the one that arrives slowest.
