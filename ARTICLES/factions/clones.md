**Clones** were grown on [[Kamino]] to serve as the Grand Army of the Republic. They age twice as fast as ordinary men, and about a million and a half of them are still living.

## The Grand Army of the Republic

*Main article: [[The Kamino Findings]]*

The largest single item in the Republic's history was the Kaminoan clone contract. It had been ordered in the name of the murdered Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas and run after his death by [[The Long War|Dooku]]. Republic auditors reached [[Kamino]] in 18 BBY with the power to demand any record they wanted.

Every clone trooper carried a small organic chip put in during growth, holding one hundred and fifty standing orders. Order 66 told the troops to kill every Jedi officer over them. At once, on a single transmission, with no confirmation and no appeal. The finding was published within six days over the objections of the Ministry of Defence and without their approval. It also settled one thing permanently. The Grand Army of the Republic had been a Sith weapon built to a Sith design, and no amount of surgery would make it anything else. What it also meant is that many people in the galaxy would never trust a clone again.

[[Cody]] learned about the extent of Order 66 in the published findings, like everyone else. The trooper [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|Fives]] was cleared posthumously in 17 BBY. Nobody was punished, since every officer involved in his death had been acting on a faked medical report.

## Manumission

*Main article: [[The Manumission Act]]*

The chips came out between 18 and 17 BBY and every surviving trooper was cleared. Production was stopped in 17 BBY and the Kaminoan contract was cancelled the same year.

The Manumission Act of 16 BBY made every clone a citizen. [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Captain [[Rex]] argued it through the Senate, giving more than four hundred hours of testimony between them. It passed as much out of necessity as conscience. Eight million living beings could not be released from service into nothing at all, with no standing under any law. Two million of them were children.

About half of the adults signed on again as volunteers. They served in the [[The Republic Corps#The Auxiliary Corps|Auxiliary Corps]], beside conscripted citizens who had been given a few weeks of training. The clones fought the rest of the [[The Long War|Long War]] as citizens of a Republic they had already defended for seven years without belonging to it. Most of them died before it ended. A little over three million adult clones were left alive when the Long War was over.

Beside them the Republic began raising a recruited army in 18 BBY, the first since the Ruusan Reformations. It was called the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]]. The Republic has eight million under arms, one citizen in every ten thousand. That is a third more than the clone army numbered in 19 BBY, at three times the cost. A clone cost his growth, his armour and his rations. A commando costs a wage, a veteran pension and an allowance for their family.

## The wards

Production of clone batches stopped in 17 BBY, but a clone takes ten years to reach deployment age. Everyone decanted after 27 BBY was still growing when the contract was cancelled. There were about two million of them, and the youngest were only days old.

The Republic became their guardian overnight and had no plan for it. Although the main production contract was cancelled, the Kaminoans were kept on under a stripped-down agreement. Accusations and allegations about the immorality of their cloning practices were quietly dropped, in exchange for the Kaminoans taking on the duty to see the underaged clones to adulthood, because they already had the facilities and know-how to raise them. The work was done at cost and without enthusiasm on either side. A Ministry of the Interior programme advertised the children for adoption across the Core and the Inner Rim. It ran for nine years, and out of the pool of two million, sixty thousand were adopted by independent families, fifty thousand were adopted by adult clone troopers, and three thousand were adopted directly by the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] when they moved.

Of course, this entire clone cohort was not a group with consistent age. The oldest underwent cadet training before production was stopped, so they were soldiers who were never deployed. Those decanted after 21 BBY were raised as wards and have never received accelerated combat training. All of them share a face and nothing else, aside from the distaste towards the Republic. There were also clone embryos still in the tanks in 17 BBY, but the Republic has never published what happened to them.

## The veterans

The clones had grown fast and they aged fast to match. Troopers decanted in 32 BBY were physically thirty-six by 14 BBY, and fifty-six in 4 BBY. Their pensions had been worked out on the assumption that they would age like ordinary men. They were not corrected until 6 BBY, by which point most of the men they were meant for were dead. Laws to protect clone veterans looking for work were debated in four sessions running and passed in none of them.

Between 16 and 7 BBY the Republic turned more than five million clones out into the galaxy. They were new citizens on paper and more than half were elderly in the body, trained for a job that no longer existed, living on a pension worked out for a man in his thirties. [[Wolffe]] was discharged in 12 BBY at a physical age of about forty. No civilian employer would take him.

## On Ossus

As the Jedi left [[Coruscant]], they left an open invitation for all clone veterans to join them. Several thousand clones went immediately, and it's estimated that over the years [[Ossus]] has been a place where over eighty thousand clones lived out the rest of their days. By 2 ABY, around twenty thousand are still alive, albeit most elderly. Some started families, as several frontier villages sprang up on Ossus, at a respectful distance from the Jedi Temple. Some of the clone children the Order adopted decided to establish the [[Ossus#The clone settlements|Temple Trooper]] regiment and still serve on Ossus.

[[Shaak Ti]] is a liaison between the Temple and clone communities in those settlements, personally overseeing the wellbeing of remaining clones and doing her best to prolong their time with Force rituals. She has seen moderate success at it, though she did determine that children of clones are perfectly healthy and do not suffer from accelerated ageing or other health complications.

## In the Collective

The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]'s strength is not exotic. Its strength is that it hires the people the Republic was finished with. [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] offered contracts, quarters, rank, and medical care suited to men ageing at twice the ordinary rate. By the Republic's own cautious estimate, around ten percent took the deal and by 2 ABY around eighty-six thousand of them still work for the Collective.

A large number of Shadow Collective ground units are commanded by men the Republic grew, freed, and then could not find a use for. Many Collective soldiers are rigorously trained by those same men, and getting deadlier by the year.

The [[The Shadow Troopers|shadow troopers]] were among the first clones to go to [[Maul]], long before [[Wolffe]] began recruiting. Wolffe joined Crimson Dawn in 7 BBY and became a recruiter. Over half the clone veterans on Collective payroll came in, directly or indirectly, through him. Over the years, most clones that joined the Collective took on a tradition to paint over their old legion's colours with the black and red of the shadow troopers.

[[Nadi Orl]] is the Collective's chief of accounts. Thanks to her, the Collective pays on time in hundreds of systems, and over eighty thousand elderly clones receive good pay, expert medical oversight, and retirement to comfortable lives when their contracts run out.

## Ko Sai's procedure

[[Ko Sai]] designed the accelerated ageing and now sells the procedure that undoes it. It is priced at nine thousand credits, paid once, with a maintenance course cheap enough that most can afford it. Hundreds of thousands of clones, Collective or not, have had it.

The Collective also sponsors Ko Sai's procedure for all clones working for it. It is genome work followed by months of drug therapy to make the changes hold. It's done once, and after that every man takes a maintenance course of pills priced so low that nobody notices it. Naturally, the price is not the issue, but the availability is. Even if a clone purchases the procedure with independent funds, he will need to keep returning to the Collective for refills. For an old man, the procedure halves their accelerated ageing. If done when the clone is young enough, it returns him to ordinary ageing altogether.

[[Rex]] refused Ko Sai's life extension procedure until the end. Slowing clone ageing has been possible for years but the Republic has never sponsored it. A man who ages twice as fast draws a pension for half as long.

## In 2 ABY

Around one and a half million of the eight million are still alive in 2 ABY. Most of them never fought. The men who served were those decanted before 27 BBY and most are physically approaching seventy. The ones raised as wards are physically between forty and sixty. The latter are the reason the survivor numbers are as high as they are.

[[Cody]] is physically sixty-eight. He serves on [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]]'s closest retinue, refusing any transfers or retirement.
