The **Hutt Clans** rule [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt Space]], and in all their dealings with the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] they have never bowed to it. After the [[The Long War|Long War]] they moved into the Halla and Kastolar sectors, and the five sectors of what became the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]] needed two years to drive them out again.

## Halla and Kastolar

*Main article: [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition]]*

[[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Halla and Kastolar]] border [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt Space]], and the Hutts moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. [[Nal Kaddan]] fell in 15 BBY. Both sectors begged for help. Nothing came out of [[Coruscant]] except condolences and empty promises.

The Governor of Taldot, [[Jhena Varrin]], called the other four sectors together in 14 BBY. They agreed to pool what was left. The [[Kashyyyk|Wookiees]] had been fighting Trandoshan slavers for centuries without help, and they were eager to strike back at the Hutts. It took two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar.

## The Collective

The Hutt Clans deal with the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] constantly and have never once submitted to it. Jabba Desilijic Tiure has done business with [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]] for eighteen years. He defers to [[Maul]] in nothing and treats the whole relationship as trade between equals, which infuriates Maul and is probably the point.

## The Pykes

Open fights are frequent and settle nothing: contested spice routes, the Nal Hutta dock seizures, and the four-year dispute over who owned the Pykes, which the Pykes ended by selling themselves to both sides at once. That arrangement lasted until 4 BBY. The Hutts survive because they are older, because they are satisfied with steady business, and because Maul was unable to prove that killing them would be more profitable than dealing with them. And on top of that, the galactic chaos makes the Republic completely unable to interfere within Hutt Space.

[[Sarn Vekh]] ran spice distribution into the poorest systems of the Rim for nineteen years and was made a director for it. In 4 BBY the Collective ordered the Syndicate out of those systems entirely. [[Nadi Orl]] had decided that an addicted sector cannot be recruited from or invested in, and both of those options were more profitable in the long run. The Pyke Syndicate had been selling itself to the Hutts and the Collective at once since 8 BBY. Orl's order is what broke this game and resulted in two years of conflict. At the end of it in 2 BBY, the main Syndicate body went to the Hutts. Vekh took about a fifth of it and stayed loyal to Maul as the Shadow Pykes. The Syndicate calls them cast-offs. The Collective calls them turncoats and has never stopped watching them.
