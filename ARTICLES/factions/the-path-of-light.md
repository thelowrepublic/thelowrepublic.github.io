The **Path of Light** is a sect that believes the prophecy and takes [[Anakin Skywalker]] for the Chosen One. Its followers, the Walkers, have killed at least sixty officials of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] since the sect appeared in 1 ABY.

## Origins, 1 ABY

The Path appeared in 1 ABY, about eight months after the last survivors came home from [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]]. They call themselves the Path of Light. Individually they are Walkers. The Republic files them as the Cult of Light and has listed them as a terrorist organisation and a murder cult, both of which are partially accurate.

## Belief

Their belief is straightforward: the [[Anakin Skywalker#The prophecy|prophecy]] is real. Skywalker is the Chosen One. What happened on [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin 4]] was a demonstration. He walked into the worst thing in the galaxy and it could not touch him. He brought six hundred people out of it on his own. He did this without belonging to any order or explaining himself to anyone. The Path holds that this is the path, that it is walkable, and that the way to walk it is to stop deciding and to act.

## The killings

The Walkers have killed at least sixty Republic officials in two years. The number is uncertain because the method varies. The Path claims nothing and does not seek recognition or glory.

[[Mas Amedda]] is the last living person who worked in [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]]'s office. He survived a Path of Light assassination attempt.

## What frightens the Ministry

Two things about the Walkers frighten the Ministry. The first is that they cannot be found. They are not Force-users. Four Walkers have ever been captured alive and all four tested flat. There is no corruption to detect, no Force signature. [[The Jedi Templars|Templar]] investigators have walked through three fresh sites and reported an ordinary run of a room where somebody has died. Whatever the Path is doing, it is not being done by using the Force.

The second is the victims. Of the sixty-odd killings, investigators have gone on to establish that roughly seven in ten of the dead were taking money, running something, or covering for someone who was. This is not a characterisation by the Path's sympathisers. It is the Ministry's own number, produced by its own inquiries, most of which were only opened because there was a body of someone important.

## Coruscant

[[Do'Kass]] is a Jedi Knight who works for [[Wullf Yularen]]. Since 1 ABY he has worked almost only on the Path of Light case on [[Coruscant]]. He does not think it is a network. He thinks that most of it is one person.

## In 2 ABY

Three in ten victims were clean. Those three are why the Path is hunted. The seven are why the hunting goes badly. A working [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|sector ranger]] in the Rim now has to decide how hard to look for people who keep removing their own problems.

[[Anakin Skywalker|Skywalker]]'s own accuracy, across [[Anakin Skywalker#The record|forty-one appearances]], is nearer to a hundred percent. Nobody has been able to ask him what he thinks of the Path. Two Walkers have been recorded saying that he does not need to approve of them. Approval, they said, is a thing that people who choose require.
