**Republic Intelligence** is the intelligence service of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]. It is also called the Strategic Information Service, or the SIS. [[Wullf Yularen]] has directed the reformed service since 12 BBY.

## The Outland Command file, 11–1 BBY

*Main article: [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]*

Yularen opened a file on the Outland Command accounts in 11 BBY and did not close it for nine years. Yularen closed his file on [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] in 2 BBY. In the first weeks of 1 BBY he laid the Outland Command evidence in front of the Chancellor and the investigators: the manifests, the accounts, the pattern of deaths. Yularen has been working with rogue Jedi Knight [[Do'Kass]] for the past decade, the Force-user's unconventional methods producing evidence that would otherwise not see the light of day.

Tarkin understood his position at once. He did not deny the connection. He described it as wartime intelligence work, difficult compromises he was forced to make, and expressed regret that it had been necessary. Within four months he had become the loudest and most convincing voice in the Republic for destroying the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. He was even willing to fully fund and coordinate the manhunt for [[Maul]], as long as the Republic provided troops. He was the man who had seen the danger first, from the inside, and whose warnings had been ignored all this time.

It was an extraordinary performance for the investigators and it mostly worked. He was given command of the operation, and the whole of it would be recorded for Republic supervision. His gracious offer of funding and logistics meant it did not need formal authorisation from the Senate at all. Their hands would be clean, or so they thought.

Several colleagues privately blame him for a share of [[The Battle of Yavin#The decision|Yavin]], on the argument that when cornered in 1 BBY, someone like Tarkin was always going to do something. Yularen has never disputed that accusation, yet did not resign from his post.

## Analysts and agents

[[Do'Kass]] is a Jedi Knight with no rank in either Jedi body since [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|the demilitarisation]]. He works for Yularen on no contract anyone has produced. He walks into a room and simply knows who is lying. He is rarely wrong, but his 'hunch' is not viable evidence for a report. Since 1 ABY he has worked almost only on the [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] case on [[Coruscant]]. He does not think it is a network. He thinks that most of it is one person.

[[Dedra Meero]] meticulously tracked and compiled Anakin Skywalker's [[Anakin Skywalker#The record|forty-one appearances]] in 1 ABY, and presented conclusions about their patterns and effects. The paper was suppressed and she was reassigned to a licensing desk. Yularen read her report anyway and had her recruited into Republic Intelligence within the year. She is thorough, literal and without the ability to quit. Yularen has said she is the only one who truly reads every data point she is given.

## Informants and reports

[[Morgan Elsbeth]] has fed Republic Intelligence since 1 ABY. She is Dathomiri. She joined the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] for Dathomir and not for Maul. She wants the Collective gone and [[The Nightsisters|Nightsisters]] left alone.

[[Hondo Ohnaka]] runs salvage permits out of Florrum now, entirely legally. He has informed for Republic Intelligence eleven times, always for a generous reward.

[[Leia Skywalker]] has received three private invitations from [[Barriss Offee]], the first in 1 ABY. She has declined all three and reported all three to the SIS, which is the only reason anyone knows about them.

## Files and assessments

Members of the [[Order of Voss]] have turned up at four recorded incidents in Republic space since 3 BBY and at least two in [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] territory. The Voss have refused contact with both, consistently and without hostility. Republic Intelligence keeps a thin file. The [[Ossus Order|Ossus Council]] knows they exist, has made three approaches, and has been politely turned down three times.

Inside [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]'s borders the Collective's conduct has been immaculate. No extortion, no seizures, no unexplained deaths. Republic Intelligence has spent two years looking for one clean incident of Collective aggression against the Republic inside Quelii and has not produced one. Outside those borders the syndicate has done all of it, constantly, rampaging through thousands of systems.

Republic Intelligence rates [[Nadi Orl]], the Collective's chief of accounts, the highest-value target in the organisation. [[Barriss Offee]] instructs the Nightborn's Jedi defectors and wrote its doctrine. Republic Intelligence considers her the most influential person in the organisation.

## In 2 ABY

What the Republic has grown since [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] is not the [[The Republic Corps|Corps]]. The [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces]] were revived in 1 ABY after forty years of neglect and the Sector Rangers were properly funded for the first time in a century. The Ministry now spends the most on intel, private detectives, bounty hunters and security funds.

[[The Shadow War#What each side fields|Taozin nodules]], which hide a wearer from Force detection, are prohibited to Republic personnel and are worn by perhaps a third of Republic Intelligence.
