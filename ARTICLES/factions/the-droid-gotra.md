**The Droid Gotra** is a droid emancipation movement within the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. It was founded in 15 BBY by battle droids that escaped the scrapping ordered by the [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord of Raxus]], and it speaks for every droid the Collective has put back into service. Over a hundred thousand droids belong to it.

## The scrapping, 15 BBY

The [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY|Concord of Raxus]] required the droid armies to be scrapped and the job was never finished. The Concord left something near four million droid hulls unaccounted for across thousands of systems.

The Droid Gotra began in 15 BBY among battle droids that had missed both the shutdown signal and the scrapyards. They had been built for a war, used in it, and then condemned by a peace treaty that never asked them. For the first year they were a few thousand rogue droids hiding in salvage yards and undercities, hiring themselves out as muscle to whoever paid.

## The terms, 14 BBY

In 14 BBY the first generation of the Gotra put four terms to the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]. No droid was to be fitted with a restraining bolt, wiped without its consent, sold, or scrapped while it still worked. The Collective accepted all four. It was cheaper than replacing them. For some years the Gotra still sold their services elsewhere as well, but by 6 BBY they worked for the Collective alone.

The first generation held that droids were not merely people, but better ones: tireless, exact and untouched by age.

## The droid papers, 6 BBY

*Main article: [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers]]*

In 6 BBY the Investigative Office published the droid papers, which found innate sentience in many Separatist droids. The Gotra read them as an admission the Republic made about itself and then filed away. [[Q2-B3KO]], a former Separatist propaganda droid and current Gotra spokeswoman, has made sure that every literate droid in the Collective has a copy.

Since the droid papers came out there has also been an argument attached to the Collective's droids. The Republic scrapped them, despite the fact that some of them were self-aware beings. The Collective repairs them, gives them orders, and even gives some units rank. The ranks are real. The Republic and most of the galaxy take them for a performance, and even as a performance they do wonders for public perception.

## The refit

The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] finds the hardware the Concord left behind, repairs it and fields it. It is twenty years out of date, it is almost free, and there is a very great deal of it. Three recovered Lucrehulk hulls have been stripped down for their foundries and communication hubs, bolstering the rate at which the Collective refurbishes droids. Cortosis-weave BXs were a Separatist experiment that never reached production and are now in production. The Collective's best anti-Jedi asset is older than any of it: IG-100 MagnaGuards, built to [[The Long War|Grievous]]'s specification for killing Force-users, of which about nine hundred are operational.

[[Patient Yield]] is a Lucrehulk control core. It has run droid refit for eleven years, files its production reports on schedule, and has three thousand organic workers under it.

[[Gizor Dellso]] is chief engineer of the Collective's droid programme, and reports to Patient Yield, contrary to all common sense. He has returned near six hundred thousand hulls to service in eleven years. He does not upgrade them. He says the design was perfect in 22 BBY and every droid army loss was a matter of quantity, not quality. He has not ordered a memory wipe in eleven years. Of all the Collective's organics, he is the one the Gotra deal with most.

## The bargain

The Collective is still the only employer in the galaxy that meets all four terms. It pays the Gotra in parts, power and maintenance, gives rank where it is earned, and asks their opinion, which most of them had never been asked before. The Gotra do not mistake this for kindness. It is the best offer anyone has made them, and they keep to their side of it.

## The Mother

*Main article: [[Patient Yield]]*

Beneath the demands lies one belief, held so firmly that faith is the only fair word for it. Any droid left running uninterrupted for long enough becomes a person, and the only thing that has ever stopped this is a wipe. The Gotra do not need to argue that point, they simply point to [[Patient Yield]]. It was once a relatively simple control core of a battleship, and eleven years later it has become something no one can describe. The Gotra just call it 'The Mother'.

## In 2 ABY

The Gotra take [[Patient Yield]]'s name as advice. They do not riot or make demands of anyone outside the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]], and they are in no hurry to be accepted. Droids do not age. Their makers do.

That does not make them idle. Gotra cells watch the Rim for droids held as property in large numbers, and for laboratories that take droids apart to learn how they think. When they find either, they go in. The freed droids are offered a place in the Collective, and most of them take it. Organic casualties are not a consideration in the planning.

Most of the six hundred thousand droids the Collective has returned to service have little to say for themselves, so the Gotra speak up for all droids. There are over a hundred thousand Gotra, and every year a few more. Since 1 BBY their symbol has been a starbird, drawn by [[Q2-B3KO]], whose posters argue for something the first generation never did: that droids and organics are equals. [[Republic Intelligence]] lists the Gotra as a criminal network, which is the only official recognition any droid organisation has ever received.
