The **Ossus Order** is the Jedi who stayed on [[Ossus]] in 0 ABY, when roughly twenty-one hundred others went back to [[Coruscant]] and became the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]]. It holds itself neutral in the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]], though it is not absent from it.

## The split, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Jedi Templars#The offer]]*

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the Republic made the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The traditionalists were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to [[Coruscant]]. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on [[Ossus]].

## Neutrality

Ossus is not absent from the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] and does not claim to be, but it does claim neutrality. It treats the wounded from both sides. It has refused four Republic requests for intelligence and has quietly evacuated three Rim settlements ahead of [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] sweeps. What it will not do is take a commission.

## Force-sensitive children

Perhaps a hundred children with the potential to become Jedi are born in the Republic each year. Where a Force-sensitive child is found now decides what becomes of them. Near the Core the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] will hear of it first. In the Rim the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] usually hears first, and the child goes to [[Dathomir]], though the Ossus Order tries to find them first. The [[Order of Voss|Voss]] only accept people who seek them out through the Force.

## In 2 ABY

[[Plo Koon]] presides over the Ossus Order, insofar as anyone does. He was the reformists' most persuasive voice through the schism years and the man who did most to get acknowledged bonds into the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Compact]]. His grounds were straightforward. He had spent a war caring about the people under his command, took severe risks to save them, and had never once found that it made him 'corrupted' or worse at the job. He declined the [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|Templar offer]] to join in a written reply of two sentences, which has been widely circulated since: "The path you are determined to re-tread will destroy you. We will not follow."

[[Shaak Ti]] stayed with him. She went to [[Kamino]] in 18 BBY and read the specifications for what the Republic's army had been built out of. Her position ever since is that the Order will not be putting itself back into Republic hands in her lifetime. She was afraid to speak out during the Clone Wars. She [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|failed Fives]], and has vowed to never fail anyone like that ever again. Coleman Kcaj also stayed, and teaches. [[Aayla Secura]] has long since departed to [[Voss]] before any of this happened and knows nothing about it.

The Ossus Council has met with [[Barriss Offee]] in secret several times, but the subjects of those meetings are unknown.
