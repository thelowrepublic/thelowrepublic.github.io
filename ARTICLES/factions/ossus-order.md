The **Ossus Order** is the Jedi who stayed on [[Ossus]] in 0 ABY, when roughly twenty-one hundred others went back to [[Coruscant]] and became the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]]. It holds itself neutral in the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]], though it is not absent from it.

## The split, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Jedi Templars#The offer]]*

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the Republic made the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The traditionalists were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to [[Coruscant]]. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on [[Ossus]].

## Neutrality

Ossus is not absent from the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] and does not claim to be, but it does claim neutrality. It treats the wounded from both sides. It has refused four Republic requests for intelligence and has quietly evacuated three Rim settlements ahead of [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] sweeps. What it will not do is take a commission. They have also stopped using the symbol of the Jedi Order that was used during the Clone Wars, and instead adopted an older design that the Jedi used during the High Republic era.

## Force-sensitive children

Perhaps a hundred children with the potential to become Jedi are born in the Republic each year. Where a Force-sensitive child is found now decides what becomes of them. Near the Core the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] will hear of it first. In the Rim the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] usually hears first, and the child goes to [[Dathomir]], though the Ossus Order tries to find them first. The [[Order of Voss|Voss]] only accept people who seek them out through the Force.

## Temple Troopers

A Ministry of the Interior programme advertised the [[The Manumission Act#The ones still growing|clone children]] for adoption across the Core and the Inner Rim. It ran for nine years, and out of the pool of two million, sixty thousand were adopted by independent families, fifty thousand were adopted by adult clone troopers, and three thousand were adopted directly by the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] when they moved. Some of the latter decided to establish the Temple Trooper regiment and still serve on [[Ossus]].

They petitioned the Council at Ossus to let them form it out of genuine admiration for the Jedi, and a wish to repay them for their kindness. Many other [[Clones|clones]] have joined since, and the regiment is now about a thousand strong. The newest volunteers are the teenage children of clones from the nearby settlements, who wish to undergo training so that they can serve as Temple Troopers once they are adults. For now the regiment is housed in the Temple itself, but it keeps growing, and the Ossus Council is seriously considering finding it a headquarters of its own.

None of them are Force-sensitive. They wear the combat armour that Jedi commanders wore early in the Clone Wars, which was decommissioned before the war ended, when the Jedi went back to their traditional robes. They are trained to use lightsabers as well as anyone who is not a Jedi can, which turns out to be quite well, and they carry stun blasters as a backup. They are also taught meditative techniques that help them keep a clear head in battle. The regiment guards the Temple on Ossus. On the rare occasions when Jedi Knights are sent into a fight, on Ossus or out in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]], Temple Troopers go with them. None of them are made to serve, and all of them are more than willing to.

## In 2 ABY

[[Plo Koon]] presides over the Ossus Order, insofar as anyone does. He was the reformists' most persuasive voice through the schism years and the man who did most to get acknowledged bonds into the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Compact]]. His grounds were straightforward. He had spent a war caring about the people under his command, took severe risks to save them, and had never once found that it made him 'corrupted' or worse at the job. He declined the [[The Jedi Templars#The offer|Templar offer]] to join in a written reply of two sentences, which has been widely circulated since: "The path you are determined to re-tread will destroy you. We will not follow."

[[Shaak Ti]] stayed with him. She went to [[Kamino]] in 18 BBY and read the specifications for what the Republic's army had been built out of. Her position ever since is that the Order will not be putting itself back into Republic hands in her lifetime. She was afraid to speak out during the Clone Wars. She [[The Kamino Findings#The audit|failed Fives]], and has vowed to never fail anyone like that ever again. Coleman Kcaj also stayed, and teaches. Jocasta Nu brought the Jedi Archives to Ossus and keeps them there, beside the ruins of the old library. Huyang, the ancient droid who has taught younglings to build their first lightsabers for longer than anyone can remember, does the same on Ossus. [[Aayla Secura]] has long since departed to [[Voss]] before any of this happened and knows nothing about it.

The Ossus Council has met with [[Barriss Offee]] in secret several times, but the subjects of those meetings are unknown.

In 2 ABY the [[Order of Voss]] sent word warning Ossus to be wary of [[Baylan Skoll]]. The warning reached Ossus too late. Skoll had already broken into the Temple, harmed no one, and taken only his own old lightsaber and a stone tablet.
