**Mandalore** has been divided since 1 ABY. [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] holds the north, with three quarters of Mandalore's people and most of its industry, and [[Gar Saxon]]'s Death Watch, a part of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], holds the south and every armoury on it.

## Background

Mandalore is a planet of the Outer Rim and the homeworld of the Mandalorians, a warrior people whose armour was often made from beskar. During the Clone Wars it was governed by the pacifist New Mandalorians under Duchess Satine Kryze. Death Watch, warriors who would not give up the old ways, opposed them from exile. Death Watch then joined [[Maul]]'s Shadow Collective, which took the planet, and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] led a resistance against Maul with the Republic's help.

## History

### Maul's capture, 19 BBY

*Main article: [[The Death of Palpatine#The escape of Maul]]*

Four days before Palpatine's killing, the Zabrak warlord [[Maul]] had been captured on Mandalore by Commander [[Ahsoka Tano]] and the 501st Legion. He was put aboard a Venator-class Star Destroyer bound for [[Coruscant]].

### The armouries

Twenty-one hundred trained Force-users went into the field for the Republic in 0 ABY. The [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] number around six hundred. They are on average more skilled individually and they choose where and when to fight. And they will still lose a war of attrition by a wide margin. Everyone involved can do the arithmetic. The Shadow Collective's answer was not to make more Nightborn, but to stop needing them.

A lightsaber will not go through beskar. A solid slug does not stop for a light-blade the way a blaster bolt does. Both facts are a thousand years old and both have been largely forgotten. The great armouries were sealed in the southern ruins of Mandalore, and the Mandalorians who might have opened them had spent forty years losing wars to one another. Gar Saxon's Death Watch fought over the southern half of Mandalore, which is the half that contains the most ruins, and had been working the old vaults without much spare time since 4 BBY.

Bo-Katan Kryze fully took the north in 1 ABY. It was a real choice, made with her eyes open, and she has never doubted her reasoning. The north holds three quarters of Mandalore's people and most of the industrial infrastructure. In every respect except one, that was the right decision. However, with nobody left to contest the south, Death Watch spent the next year doing what they didn't have the leisure to do in a generation. It reopened the armouries. It refitted an army in beskar. It went back to re-learn techniques for killing Force-users that had survived only because a handful of isolationist sects had never stopped practising them.

### The Children of the Watch

One of those sects is the Children of the Watch, who take in orphans and raise them in the creed from infancy. They have done this without a break since before the Clone Wars. Until about 0 ABY most Mandalorians regarded them as a mild embarrassment. They are now the most valuable military asset the Shadow Collective possesses.

## In 2 ABY

Bo-Katan Kryze leads the loyalist coalition and has held the northern half since 1 ABY, with quiet Republic support. In the south, an army clad in a thousand-year-old beskar is training to kill Force-users. It trains in ruins Bo-Katan Kryze gave up so that three quarters of her people could live without fearing for their lives.
