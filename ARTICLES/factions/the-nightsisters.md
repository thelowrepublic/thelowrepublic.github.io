The **Nightsisters** are the order [[Asajj Ventress]] leads in the eastern highlands of [[Dathomir]], holding to the closed rite against [[Merrin]]'s reform. Ventress took back the old clans' name in 3 BBY, when she broke with [[Maul]] at Nightspire and left the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] with twenty Dathomiri witches and two hundred others.

## The old clans

[[Dathomir]] was massacred by General [[The Long War|Grievous]] in 21 BBY. The Nightsister clans were finished as an organised power and the survivors scattered.

## Ventress and the Nightborn, 9–3 BBY

[[Asajj Ventress]] was reported killed during a Jedi operation in 19 BBY. She reappeared in 9 BBY, revealed to have been operating as a bounty hunter under multiple aliases for all this time. The same year she received an invitation from [[Maul]]. The rationale was simple. She and Maul were the same thing, Dathomiri weapons made by one hierarchy and thrown away by another. She was a bounty hunter because she had nowhere else to be and nothing to strive for. Maul could give her both. She accepted. She was one of the order's founders and for six years its best field leader.

## Nightspire

The arrangement failed for the reason such arrangements usually fail. Maul had built it on a promise that nobody in it would be used, and used her exactly as [[The Long War|Dooku]] had. They fought at Nightspire in 3 BBY. Neither died. She left the same night with twenty Dathomiri witches of the fifty present. Two hundred others went with her, Collective personnel, civilians and a handful of former Jedi. She set herself up in the eastern highlands, where she took back the name Nightsisters and the whole of the old practice with it: the closed rite, the female line, the four-thousand-year tradition [[Merrin]] had broken. She holds that the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] are not a restoration of Dathomir but a second theft of it. Not because of the inclusion of men in the rites, but because they don't realise that Maul will forsake all of their principles the moment he needs an army. Neither Maul nor the Republic has been able to shift her, and both have decided it is not worth what it would cost to try.

[[Morgan Elsbeth]] joined the Nightborn for Dathomir and not for Maul. For six years she made sure that Ventress could see what Maul was using her for, and Nightspire followed. She wants the Collective gone and Nightsisters left alone.

## In 2 ABY

Forty kilometres away from Maul in the eastern highlands, Asajj Ventress holds the old rite, attempts a second shot at a peaceful life and diligently avoids contact with [[Quinlan Vos]] for reasons only she knows.

[[Ahsoka Tano]] has been to the edge of Dathomiri space at least twice. A ship matching no registry breached the Nightsister territory and stayed there for five hours in the ninth month of 1 ABY and then left. Nobody came out to see it. Ventress's people have said nothing about the matter, which is what they say about everything.
