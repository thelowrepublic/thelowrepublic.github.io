The **Republic Corps** is the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]'s recruited army, raised from 18 BBY out of the Senate Commandos. Most of it stands guard, and the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] is left largely to the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers]].

## Raising the Corps, 18–16 BBY

The Senate began raising the Republic Corps in 18 BBY, within weeks of the [[The Kamino Findings|Kamino findings]]. It was the first recruited army since the Ruusan Reformations. The work took two years. Cancelling the clone contract in 17 BBY was one step of it.

The Republic Corps was built in the middle of the [[The Long War|Long War]], so it was created out of what already existed and was readily available. And that something was the Senate Commandos, a small elite unit of the Senate Guard. They had never been part of the clone army; all of them were recruited volunteers and none of them had the public perception baggage that came with being a clone. The Republic took their armour blueprints, their training regimens, their colours and their symbol, and simply upscaled all of it into a standing army.

The ceremonial Senate Guard was left as it was. When the Commandos left the Senate building, the Guard took over all security responsibilities.

## The Auxiliary Corps

The Corps was not ready in time for most of the [[The Long War|Long War]], so the war was finished by the Auxiliary Corps. Its regiments were filled with [[Clones|clones]] who had signed on again and conscripted citizens given only a few weeks of training.

About half of the adult clones signed on again as volunteers after the [[The Manumission Act#The Reconstitution, 18–12 BBY|Manumission Act]] of 16 BBY. The clones fought the rest of the Long War as citizens of a Republic they had already defended for seven years without belonging to it. Most of them died before it ended. A little over three million adult clones were left alive when the Long War was over.

The Auxiliary regiments took most of the war's last casualties. They were disbanded between 15 and 12 BBY, though there was not much left to disband.

## What a recruited army changed

A permanent army under civilian control created something that had not existed in 22 BBY: a permanent military interest. Shipyards with contracts. Veterans' associations. Garrisons in vital sectors and along trade lanes. A general staff with opinions about policy. All of it proved lasting.

More importantly, the dead came home. Clone casualties had been a line in a budget. Recruited casualties were somebody's constituents. They went back to their home worlds in numbered caskets and were mourned by voters who had a senator.

This is the main reason the [[The Long War|Long War]] ended when it did. The Separatist remnants were not beaten. By 15 BBY the voters would no longer pay to beat them. The last holdout commands were bought off, bribed, or quietly given the self-rule they had spent seven years fighting for.

## Murkhana, 16 BBY

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

[[Anakin Skywalker]] commanded at [[Murkhana]] as a boots-on-the-ground general in 16 BBY. As a general of the Republic Corps, not a Jedi or a Chosen One, Anakin destroyed a city. Intelligence said that there was a buried droid command node under it and that the population was complicit. The node was there. Whether the city's population knew about it is still debated to this day. About two hundred thousand civilians died and the campaign in that sector ended four months early.

The intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. He said so at the inquiry. He said someone wanted him gone, but he could not prove it, and he sounded exactly like a man making excuses. Of course, nothing has ever been proved. He was discharged with honour in 14 BBY.

The [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]] of 11 BBY ended the practice for good. No general has led troops in person since, with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as the sole exception, and his commission had to be written specially to allow it.

## The commandos

The familiar armour was the point, as the public already trusted it. A recruit in 16 BBY wore not just armour, but a cultural statement. Republic citizens who saw them simply assumed they were elite troops.

Every enlisted soldier holds the rank of commando, and the title is not only for show. A clone had to be only good enough to justify the batch he came from. The Corps could choose the best of the best from a large and varied pool of volunteers. They did and they were not afraid to put the ones they chose through the wringer. About six in ten finish the training, and the rest are sent home. On average a commando is better at the job than the clone he replaced. Whatever else the Republic economised on, it did not spare any expense on the Republic Corps.

## Ranks and colours

Ranks are marked as increasingly elaborate white markings on the armour. The fancier the design, the more important a commando is. Marshals adopt some elements from the Senate Guard, adding a cape over the left arm, a large crest on top of their helmets and a short ceremonial veil attached magnetically to the bottom of the helmet. The blue has never changed for any rank. Enlisting is called 'going blue'. The soldiers are 'the blues', or the 'boys and girls in blue'. Every commando also has the symbol of the Galactic Senate emblazoned on their pauldron, indicating that they are a force backed by a galactic authority, not just regional laws.

Field command has four ranks: commando, sergeant, captain and marshal. A marshal commands the garrison of one sector. Above the marshals sit the generals, who answer to the civilian ministry and do not take the field, although that was not always the case.

Ten commandos make a squad, led by a sergeant. A hundred sergeants and their squads make a squadron, led by a captain, and ten squadrons make a division, led by a marshal. A marshal therefore leads a small army of about ten thousand troops, and the Corps has about eight hundred of them. Every rank up to and including marshal is an active combatant. Everything above that chain of command is left to the generals, who do not fight and can each command several divisions at once, as many as a conflict requires.

## Recruitment

Any citizen of a member world may enlist at the age of maturity for their species. The first term is six years. Since [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] every recruit is tested for Force sensitivity, and so was everyone already serving. Those who test positive are offered to the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] and most are recruited, though some reject the offer and come back to the Corps. Most recruits come from the Mid Rim and the poorer worlds of the Core. The Outer Rim sends almost none, because working for the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] just pays better there.

## In 2 ABY

The Republic has eight million under arms, one citizen in every ten thousand. That is a third more than the clone army numbered in 19 BBY, at three times the cost. A clone cost his growth, his armour and his rations. A commando costs a wage, a veteran pension and an allowance for their family. If they die, they will need a funeral, and such a funeral will make their neighbours think about the cost of war.

Spread across a thousand sectors, the Corps would come to eight thousand soldiers each, but that is not how they are distributed in reality. The Core is fully garrisoned, and around half of the Inner Rim is too, mostly along its borders. Beyond that, in the Mid and Outer Rim, the Corps only garrisons tactically vital worlds and chokepoints along important trade lanes. Most of the Corps simply stands guard.

The largest postings face the borders of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]] and the Collective foothold in [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii and Gordian Reach]]. The interdiction fleet at [[Yavin 4]] is the only Republic force inside either sector, and it cannot leave. The garrisons that were inside [[The Greater Seswenna Union#Seswenna|Seswenna]] were absorbed by the Union. The [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] itself is fought mostly by the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers]]. The Corps is called in when a raid needs numbers or a gang war escalates into an actual planetary war.
