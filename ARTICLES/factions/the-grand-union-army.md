The **Grand Union Army**, or the Union Army, is the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]]'s army, and the Union keeps no other force on the ground. It was built in 1 ABY out of [[The Republic Corps|Corps]] garrisons that stayed behind, [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s own sector troops, and local conscripts.

## The garrisons, 1 ABY

The [[The Republic Corps|Corps]] garrisons stationed in the eight sectors of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]] did not come home. There were about a hundred thousand commandos in them, which was much more than the sectors reasonably needed. Officers from [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s old Outland Command had spent ten years arranging postings there, quietly accumulating military presence exactly where they wanted it to be. When the Union was declared, the soldiers were offered a universal one-step rank promotion and double the pay. Nobody could find a single person that rejected that offer. The Republic has never recognised the secession, so it has never listed those soldiers as deserters. In law they are still at their posts.

## Conscription and training

Naturally, a hundred thousand soldiers do not make an army, but they can make its officers. The sector forces [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] had raised as governor were folded in, and the rest was conscripted from the local population. From the age of twelve, every child in the Union spends one day a week in cadet training. Every able adult in the Union is liable to be called, if they do not already hold a job within the Union government or under Union-owned industry sectors. The training is much longer than the Corps' and far harsher, with a full-course success rate of only about ten percent. Those who fail are not punished, just sent home. But those that succeed are rewarded with so many privileges that failing might as well be resigning yourself to be a second-class citizen. The result is written into a citizen's papers, and stays there until the citizen voluntarily re-enlists and passes training the second time. The Union holds a little over seven hundred million people. By 2 ABY close to three million of them were under arms, with more in training. That is one citizen in every two hundred and fifty, while in the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] it is one in ten thousand.

## The only force on the ground

The Union Army is the state's only force on the ground. There are no [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Sector Rangers]] and no Judicial Forces under [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s new government. The same elite soldiers who hold the sector borders are also the ones that patrol the streets of [[Eriadu]]. That is how the Union found the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s people inside it, and no other state has managed the same. The campaign that did it held whole streets until someone talked, and detained about ten people for every agent it found. The Union Army kept the Corps ranks from commando to marshal and added one above them, the First Marshal, which [[Deran Kolt]] holds.

The units are those of the [[The Republic Corps#Ranks and colours|Corps]]: a squad of ten commandos under a sergeant, a squadron of a hundred squads under a captain, and a division of ten squadrons, about ten thousand troops, under a marshal. The Union Army has about two hundred and ninety marshals, and generals who do not fight can command several divisions at once. The First Marshal can command both the generals and the marshals in the field.

## The armour

The Union did not keep the blue uniforms of the [[The Republic Corps|Corps]]. Its soldiers wear white plates over a black bodyglove. The design began as a prototype third pattern of [[Clones|clone]] armour. It was in field trials with the Outland Command when the clone contract was cancelled in 17 BBY, and the local garrisons simply decided to keep it instead of sending it back to Republic armouries. Seswenna's engineers then spent ten years improving it in secret, and preparing the factories with proper blueprints. The new and improved Union Commando armour went into full production the same month that the Union was declared, in 1 ABY. Armour and weapons are identical throughout the army, with any kind of personalisation being against the army's strict policy. The only thing that tells one Union soldier from another is a small badge printed on both the chest plate and back-plate, which details a soldier's rank and identification number. Every single Union soldier is intensively trained to be able to read that identification in a split-second, even in minimal visibility conditions. 

The one exception to the uniform is the Elite Guard, which protects the Union's highest officials. Its members wear all-black cortosis-weave armour with a cape, and carry cortosis spears. They are suspected of being Force-sensitive to some degree, which the Union has neither confirmed nor denied.

## In 2 ABY

[[Deran Kolt]] is First Marshal of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]], commander of the Grand Union Army, and [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s presumed successor. He ran the sectors' campaign against [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] infiltration and rooted them all out, brutally, which nobody else in the galaxy has managed.
