The **Shadow Troopers** were the Republic's secret [[Clones|clone]] units, kept for work it could not admit to. After their discharge they went over to [[Maul]], and their black and red is now worn by most clones in the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]].

## History

The Republic kept a small number of [[Clones|clone]] units for covert and illegal operations. They were called shadow troopers, equipped with the best weapons and experimental black-and-red armours. They were sent against targets that the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces]] or Clone Army could not be seen near: Republic politicians, inconvenient planetary lords and allied infrastructure that needed to be blown up so that its owner would bend to the demands of the Senate. They were used often and without care for casualties, because a man with no next of kin generates no questions and might as well have never existed. By the end of the [[The Long War|Long War]], the shadow troopers knew more about how the Republic actually worked than most senators did.

And for all their work and discretion, they were discharged just like everybody else in 16 BBY. Those that were not terminated for knowing too much, that is. Understandably, they were among the first clones to go to [[Maul]], long before [[Wolffe]] began recruiting, and they decided to keep the name and the armours. The joke was that they would be a walking testament and mockery of the Republic's corruption. They would go on doing the same work the Republic commanded them to, just this time out in the open, for people who actually hired and compensated them.

## In 2 ABY

The original companies were small and they were old before the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]] began. By 2 ABY very few of them are still alive. They either retired, became drill instructors or are coordinating [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] operations in purely tactical capacity. Over the years, most clones that joined the Collective took on a tradition to paint over their old legion's colours with the black and red of the shadow troopers. It's a gesture of both solidarity under a new employer and a way to honour those who were the first to throw a middle finger into the Republic's face. Even if most clones that do this have never met a real shadow trooper.
