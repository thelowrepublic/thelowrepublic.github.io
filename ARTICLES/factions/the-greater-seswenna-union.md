The **Greater Seswenna Union** is the sector confederation that Seswenna and eight neighbouring sectors formed after they voted to leave the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] in 1 ABY. [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], who had governed Seswenna since 10 BBY, holds the title of Governor-General.

## Seswenna

[[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] resigned his commission in 10 BBY, went home to Eriadu, and was elected governor of the Seswenna sector. Over the following decade he drew the neighbouring frontier sectors into an efficient, prosperous and orderly bloc. Elections went on being held and stopped being competitive. He kept being re-elected term after term, through an overwhelming majority. [[Coruscant]] put up with the arrangement because Seswenna kept the shipping lanes safe, paid what it owed, and cleaned up its own droid remnants without asking for Republic troops. Director [[Wullf Yularen]] of the reformed Strategic Information Service, known to everyone as [[Republic Intelligence]] or SIS, opened a file on the Outland Command accounts in 11 BBY and did not close it for nine years.

## The secession, 1 ABY

*Main article: [[The Secessions#Seswenna, 1 ABY]]*

The [[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing|bad briefing]] was established in the last months of 0 ABY. The investigators worked from the log of what had been withheld rather than from the missing pages themselves. The Outland Command's secret arrangement with [[Maul]] was published alongside it. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] was summoned to answer for both, but Seswenna did not hand him over.

The sector had been run by Tarkin for eleven years. It was prosperous, orderly and safe. It had cleaned up its own droid remnants without Republic help. It had successfully pushed back against [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] and Hutt incursions. The people of Seswenna saw the Core's investigation as an attempt to tarnish the name of the man who had given them almost twenty years of stability. The Republic lost almost ten thousand soldiers due to its own incompetence and needed someone to blame. Most of the sector believed this sincerely, and all news that reached Eriadu through legal channels reinforced their stance. Seswenna's government voted to leave the Republic in the fourth month of 1 ABY. Eight neighbouring sectors supported it and voted for their own secessions as well, soon after forming a sector confederation, the independent Greater Seswenna Union.

There was no clause in the charter for the Republic to point at and none to break, so there was also no real way to stop it. Tarkin had spent twenty years building the largest concentration of military strength outside the Core. The Republic was fighting a new kind of war, and mounting a retaliatory force against Seswenna would only make things worse. The Senate condemned the withdrawal, refused to recognise it, and did nothing else. Almost all of the Core still trades with the Union, as the Eriadu hyperspace crossroads is too vital to simply abandon. The trade runs mostly one way. The Union buys more shipments from the Inner Rim every quarter, much of it rare minerals.

Everyone with adequate political acumen knows why the Union works, and it is not because it's an autocratic military state (though many wannabe tyrants claim it is). It succeeds because it is younger than the Republic, its structures are appropriate for its smaller size, and it has focused on internal subjugation instead of outward expansion. So far.

## The Senate seats

*Main article: [[The Secessions#The standoff]]*

The Republic will not recognise any of the secessions. The refusal cost nothing in 1 ABY and now has become the most expensive decision the Republic has made. Because Seswenna never left, in law, its nine sectors are still entitled to nine Senate seats, though they have sent nobody since 1 ABY. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] does not need the room anymore, though it is a weapon that is loaded for him to use.

## The Grand Union Army

*Main article: [[The Grand Union Army]]*

The [[The Republic Corps|Corps]] garrisons stationed in the nine sectors of the Greater Seswenna Union did not come home. There were about a hundred thousand commandos in them, which was much more than the sectors reasonably needed. Officers from [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s old Outland Command had spent ten years arranging postings there, quietly accumulating military presence exactly where they wanted it to be. When the Union was declared, the soldiers were offered a universal one-step rank promotion and double the pay. Nobody could find a single person that rejected that offer. The Republic has never recognised the secession, so it has never listed those soldiers as deserters. In law they are still at their posts.

Naturally, a hundred thousand soldiers do not make an army, but they can make its officers. The sector forces Tarkin had raised as governor were folded in, and the rest was conscripted from the local population. The Union holds a little over seven hundred million people. By 2 ABY close to three million of them were under arms, with more in training. That is one citizen in every two hundred and fifty, while in the Republic it is one in ten thousand.

The Union Army is the state's only force on the ground. There are no [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Sector Rangers]] and no Judicial Forces under Tarkin's new government. The same elite soldiers who hold the sector borders are also the ones that patrol the streets of Eriadu. That is how the Union found the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s people inside it, and no other state has managed the same. The Union Army kept the Corps ranks from commando to marshal and added one above them, the First Marshal, which Deran Kolt holds.

## In 2 ABY

[[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] is now sixty-six. He holds the title of Governor-General of the Greater Seswenna Union and has never been convicted of anything by any court.

Tarkin's bargain with [[Maul]] ended in 1 BBY and neither man has moved against the other since. [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] and Seswenna sit at opposite ends of the Rim. Beyond that, each is useful to the other. Prosperous Rim sectors are the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s best argument that the Republic is not needed. A Republic coming apart is Seswenna's best argument that local authority is the best choice.

Grand Admiral [[Thrawn]] has commanded the Union's fleet since the secession and has not lost an engagement with the Collective. [[Deran Kolt]] is First Marshal of the Greater Seswenna Union, commander of the [[The Grand Union Army|Grand Union Army]], and Tarkin's presumed successor. He ran the sectors' campaign against Collective infiltration and rooted them all out, brutally, which nobody else in the galaxy has managed. He has a better public image than Tarkin and holds the same views. Coruscant's quiet hope is that the Union falls apart when the old man dies. Kolt and Thrawn are the two reasons why it won't.
