The **Nightborn** are a Force order with its home on [[Dathomir]], and they form one part of [[Maul]]'s [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]. [[Merrin]] opened the old Nightsister practice to men and gave the order its name, and it joins three traditions: her magick, Sith techniques from Maul, and Jedi training taught by [[Barriss Offee]].

## Founding

The order's name was a statement of intent. The Nightsister tradition had been closed to men for four thousand years. The Nightbrothers of the Dathomiri villages had been breeding stock and labour for it. [[Maul]] himself had been born into that arrangement, one he would not be willing to fund, so [[Merrin]] opened the practice to them. She chose Nightborn because Nightsister no longer described what she was building, and the word spread outward from her branch to cover the whole order. 

Three traditions were braided together. The magick came from Merrin: illusion, spirit-work, ichor, and the raising of the dead. Sith techniques came from Maul and from what he dug up at over a dozen ancient ruins. Jedi training, meditation, the lightsaber forms and a working knowledge of how the Jedi actually operate were brought in by defectors and taught by [[Barriss Offee]]. 

Offee had bombed the Jedi Temple in 19 BBY and was tried for it. At her trial she argued that the Order had become an army and had stopped listening to the Force. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation of 16 BBY]] proved her right, and her life sentence was appealed to a decade. She was released in 9 BBY, and within a month, Maul managed to contact her and sway her to his side. Apparently they talked over some tea. Barriss has not carried a lightsaber since her imprisonment and refuses to be called a Sith or a Jedi. By every account she believes in the Nightborn values. She is the reason the order has discipline, method and a curriculum.

## The founding principle

The Nightborn's founding principle was the rejection of the Rule of Two and dark side fanaticism of the old Sith order. No Nightborn would ever again be anyone's tool. Dathomir was remade as a place to realise your own potential, discover your personal purpose, and share what you have discovered with those you wish to teach. No ranks, no orders and no duty to take part in anything. Every collective action has to be argued for with whichever Nightborn are present and willing to listen. There is only one rule: a Nightborn who finds another in danger is obligated to help. If you break the only rule set in place, you forfeit being a Nightborn. And the order owes nothing to strangers.

## Nightspire, 3 BBY

[[Asajj Ventress]] founded the Nightborn with Maul and broke with him at [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire|Nightspire]] in 3 BBY. She leads the restored [[The Nightsisters|Nightsisters]] in the Dathomiri eastern highlands, holding to the closed rite against Merrin's reform.

## Yavin, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin#Six days]]*

At Yavin-4, the Republic forces went in badly briefed. What [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] knew about Nightborn capabilities was extensive, but he could not pass it on without further incriminating himself. So the briefing described a fortified criminal stronghold held by an army of [[Mandalore|Mandalorians]] and mercenaries. It did not describe a Sith nexus or the witches. The relevant pages of the intelligence file were held back by Tarkin's people on the grounds of protecting a source. So working off of this, the Republic sent a force of eleven thousand four hundred elite troopers, the best of what they had available and what they could spare. The landing on [[Yavin 4]] was unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day at minimal cost. On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify Dathomiri magick.

The sixteen hundred survivors give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. [[Mace Windu]] was supposedly among them.

On the sixth day Tarkin ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most Nightborn left the moon hours earlier. Maul was already gone by day four.

One of the reasons the Republic wanted [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] as its general went unstated. Jedi defectors that joined the Nightborn fought on Yavin 4 and the images had been broadcast. The Republic needed a Jedi standing on its side of the line, visibly and in uniform, and it needed one before the public settled into the view that the Jedi and the Nightborn were the same thing in different colours. Kenobi was the best candidate anybody could name.

## Magick

By 2 ABY, there are roughly six hundred Nightborn, of whom around four hundred are former Jedi. This is one of the most feared groups in the galaxy and they would all fit inside a small transport cruiser. Importantly, Nightborn are not exclusively dark side users. Each of them wields the Force in whichever way suits them most, though in practice there are almost none that completely abstain from the dark side. As such, many use sorcery to infuse their kyber crystals with Dathomiri ichor, turning them into ichor crystals that emit green smoke and a sickly glow when ignited. They are better at channelling the dark side than regular crystals and much easier to produce than bleeding a crystal the Sith way, though some Nightborn still choose that path. Many of the Nightborn that came from the Jedi Order still use their old Jedi sabers, and some new recruits elect to use vibro-weapons enhanced with Dathomiri magick.

## Recruitment

The Nightborn take in Force-sensitives much as the Jedi do, but are far less careful about who they take. Adults are accepted and so are children, the latter of which are cared for by Barriss personally. Maul's standing order to the Collective is to watch for the signs and to test for midi-chlorians. Potential prospects are sent on to Dathomir. The Collective recruits in the poorest systems in the galaxy, so for most of those families this offer is the best one they will ever receive. That is why, even with the lax recruitment rules of the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]], the Nightborn are still the fastest growing Force-user order in the galaxy.

## In 2 ABY

### Within the Collective

*Main article: [[The Shadow Collective#What it is]]*

The Shadow Collective is Maul's whole organisation and his own name for it. It takes in the criminal syndicates, meaning Black Sun, the trading arm that works under the name Crimson Dawn and formerly the Pyke Syndicate, now its cast-offs known as Shadow Pykes. It also takes in the Mandalorian Death Watch under Gar Saxon, its salvaged droid formations, its hired ground troops, and the Nightborn. 

The Nightborn are only one part of it: the order of Force-users based on Dathomir. They are not the Shadow Collective and they do not run it, though they are its most feared element and the part the Republic talks about most. The difference matters, because the Republic is at war with the Shadow Collective. It has never been at war with the Nightborn and has never outlawed them by name. In practice the distinction has mattered less than it sounds. Nearly every Nightborn the Republic has arrested was taken during a Collective operation. They are charged as criminals or as terrorists, like anyone else on the raid. The exceptions are few and they are the ones that matter. A small number were arrested for what they were and not for anything they had done. Those are the cases Senator [[Ilai Vess|Vess]] keeps track of.

### In the Shadow War

Twenty-one hundred trained Force-users went into the field for the Republic in 0 ABY. The Nightborn number around six hundred. They are on average more skilled individually and they choose where and when to fight. And they will still lose a war of attrition by a wide margin. Everyone involved can do the arithmetic. The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]'s answer was not to make more Nightborn, but to [[Mandalore#The armouries|stop needing them]].

Collective field operations are coordinated out of proportion by Nightborn, who know Republic doctrine, Jedi tactics, and how to stay hidden from people they often studied beside. Then there are all the ancient Sith treasures that Maul dug up. Only a select few Nightborn know all the specifics, but they are known to be in possession of holocrons dating back to the ancient Sith Empire.
