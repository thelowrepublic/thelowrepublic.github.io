The [[The Galactic Republic|Senate]] has settled into **three stances** since [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] and the [[The Secessions|secessions]]. They are the Traditionalists, the Republic Reformists and the Core Isolationists. None of them is a party in the old sense and all three cut across sector lines. Separately, the Rim self-rule bloc votes with the Core Isolationists.

## The Traditionalists

The Traditionalists hold that the Republic works. [[Sevrin Dal]] is one of them. The charter is sound and the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Convention]] fixed what needed fixing. The only reason anything fails now is that sectors keep walking out. Bring them back and the problem ends.

They are right that the charter works as written and right that the secessions broke the budget. They speak of the Rim as territory mislaid rather than as members who left. What they do not say is that bringing sectors back by force means a full civil war, and it means ruling by fear and threat.

## The Republic Reformists

The Republic Reformists hold that the idea of the Republic is sound but its machinery is not. Centuries of law have compounded into something nobody can read, and it fails more the closer to the edge of the galaxy you are. Their answer is to rebuild the system from the ground up, so that distance from the Core stops deciding how fairly a world is treated. Sectors would then want to stay. [[Bail Organa]], Chancellor from 19 to 14 BBY, is of the Republic Reformist stance. So is [[Mon Mothma]], Chancellor from 11 BBY to 0 ABY.

They have real results behind them, and several hundred archaic rules have been replaced with ones people can follow. In private they admit what finishing this process would cost. A generation of work, and the money that the Republic is spending on the war. They have never said what all the sectors that want to leave are supposed to do in the meantime.

## The Core Isolationists

The Core Isolationists agree that the system is broken and add that it is too small for what it carries. Their answer is full withdrawal of the Republic to the [[Galactic regions#The Core|Core]] and the [[Galactic regions#The Inner Rim|Inner Rim]], focus shift towards self-sufficiency of the Core, and free trade with everything beyond it. They want the Mid and Outer Rim to gain full autonomy. They point at [[The Greater Seswenna Union#Seswenna|Seswenna]], which left, still trades with the Core, and is doing better than ever. They point at the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region]], which rebuilt itself on just a tax cut. Core Isolationists include [[Lux Bonteri]], the senator for [[Raxus]], and [[Ilai Vess]], the senator for [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]. [[Quarsh Panaka]], the Governor of [[Naboo]], is another. So is [[Rrask Doln]], the third minister of the Kashyyyk Region Coalition.

Their case is the most honest about capacity and the least honest about consequence. None of them says aloud what everyone understands. Free trade between the technologically superior Core and the underdeveloped Rim is not equalising. It will result in flagrant abuse, just without laws to protect from it.

## The Rim self-rule bloc

Lux Bonteri leads the Rim self-rule bloc. It votes with the Core Isolationists constantly. Three motions have been filed against Dal in two years. The best of them reached forty-one percent. All three came from the Rim self-rule bloc, and all three raised the number of sectors thinking about leaving.

## In 2 ABY

The three pull in three directions and nothing moves. Every emergency renewal passes because the traditionalists want the war and the other two cannot agree on an alternative. [[Leia Skywalker]] votes with the reformists on every law. She votes with the isolationists on every question of who should govern the Rim. She has never joined either.
