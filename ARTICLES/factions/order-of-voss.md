The **Order of Voss** is a Force order of perhaps six hundred, which keeps to the veil around [[Voss]] and holds nothing beyond it. It grew out of the arrival of roughly two hundred of the withdrawalists who left the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] in 8 BBY, and two hundred of its members are former Jedi.

## The withdrawalists

The six hundred and thirty-two Jedi who resigned after the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY|Ossus Compact]] in 8 BBY had lost an argument they thought was the only one that mattered. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]]'s ruin came from serving a government, from becoming soldiers instead of monks, and no amount of tinkering with doctrine could help while it went on doing so. Having lost it, they stopped arguing. They gave up their rank, their access, their sabers and they scattered into the galaxy.

Over the next two years most of them arrived, by separate roads, at the same practice. A stripped-down discipline with no institution behind it and no membership. They had no purpose beyond silencing themselves and, for once, paying attention to what the Force willed. What many of them reported separately, between 6 and 5 BBY, was a place in the Force where their attention would not go. A picture drawn not by paint, but by patches of blank canvas. Some went there. Roughly two hundred of them made the crossing.

## The order

The welcome was not warm. The [[Voss]] had spent four thousand years making sure this exact thing would not happen, and the arrivals were, whatever they called themselves now, Jedi. There was confinement, a long argument, and at least one death that neither side has ever discussed. What settled it was a vision. [[Ren-Vashal|A Voss mystic]] had seen them coming some years earlier and had chosen not to mention it until they arrived. The Voss treat visions as instructions, but revealing them too early can cause undue steps and excessive worry. Mystics reveal their visions when they deem the time is right. The arrivals were let in on that basis and on no other.

What has grown out of it in the years since is the Order of Voss: perhaps six hundred members now, Voss and human and a dozen other species, holding no territory outside the veil and claiming none. They take no commissions and accept no government's authority. They are not an army in any sense. They go out in ones and twos as healers, peacemakers and protectors. They work wherever they find work. They do not say where they come from and do not go back anywhere that can be followed. Every single one of them has given up their lightsabers, but foolish are those that equate being unarmed with being defenceless. Two hundred are former Jedi. The rest are Voss, and a number of people the members brought back with them since. The order takes in adults who ask and who are then vetted by visions of the Mystics. It seeks out nobody. Almost half of the members have never held a lightsaber and would not know what to do with one.

Master [[Aayla Secura]] is among them and is understood to have some standing there. She has not been seen in Republic space since 8 BBY and has sent no word to anyone she left behind. They have turned up at four recorded incidents in Republic space since 3 BBY and at least two in [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] territory. The Voss have refused contact with both, consistently and without hostility. [[Republic Intelligence]] keeps a thin file. The [[Ossus Order|Ossus Council]] knows they exist, has made three approaches, and has been politely turned down three times.

## In 2 ABY

Perhaps a hundred children with the potential to become Jedi are born in the Republic each year. Where a Force-sensitive child is found now decides what becomes of them. Near the Core the [[The Jedi Templars|Templars]] will hear of it first. In the Rim the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] usually hears first, and the child goes to [[Dathomir]], though the [[Ossus Order#Force-sensitive children|Ossus Order]] tries to find them first. The Voss only accept people who seek them out through the Force.
