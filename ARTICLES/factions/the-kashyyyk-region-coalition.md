The **Kashyyyk Region Coalition** is a bloc of five sectors: Taldot, Mytaranor, Lantillian, Halla and Kastolar. Since joining forces in 14 BBY, and thanks to a tax cut granted for the sake of the Perlemian Trade Route, they have grown wealthier than they were before the [[The Long War|Long War]].

## The Long War

The Long War was fought hardest in the [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]]. The Taldot sector lost most of its industry and fourteen of its worlds were left completely uninhabitable. The damage spread into the Mytaranor, Lantillian, Halla and Kastolar sectors. [[Kashyyyk]] had been held at enormous cost in 19 BBY and was not rebuilt either. No reconstruction money came, because all of it was going into [[The Republic Remade|government reforms]] and the [[The Republic Corps|recruited army]].

Halla and Kastolar border [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt Space]], and the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. [[Nal Kaddan]] fell in 15 BBY. Both sectors begged for help. Nothing came out of [[Coruscant]] except condolences and empty promises.

## The founding, 14 BBY

The Governor of Taldot called the other four together in 14 BBY. They agreed to pool what was left. Shipping, yards, food, and whatever militia each of them could still raise. Kashyyyk brought most of the latter. The Wookiees had been fighting Trandoshan slavers for centuries without help, and they were eager to strike back at the Hutts. It took two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar.

Then they all went to the Senate and asked for their taxes to be cut to a fraction. They got it, because the Perlemian Trade Route runs through their territories and the Senate could not risk them blockading it. The Coalition became official.

## In 2 ABY

The five sectors have been rebuilt since. They are richer now than they were before the war. They have a combined military force on standby that nobody around them wants to test. The Wookiees have never shown anyone their secret hyperspace routes, but they navigate Coalition ships through them for efficient intra-region travel.

None of the five sectors has ever filed any complaint to the Senate. The Coalition's third minister, [[Rrask Doln]], is a [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationist]]. In public he calls the Coalition loyal and says it has no complaints. He also keeps reminding everyone about the Perlemian Trade Route that runs through the Coalition's territories. And about the opinionated Wookiees that are a part of the Coalition. The Core Isolationists point at the Kashyyyk Region, which rebuilt itself on just a tax cut.

No other sector bloc has been offered the same special treatment and the Ministry has found plenty of excuses to not give anyone else the same deal. In reality, the difference is that the others do not have a trade route that the Republic needs kept open.
