**Mustafar** is where the Separatist Council survived in 19 BBY. Dooku was dead, and Grievous was killed on [[Utapau]] within hours of [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]]. The Confederacy had been beheaded, not defeated, and it fought on in [[The Long War|the Long War]].

## Background

Mustafar is a volcanic world in the Outer Rim. In the last days of the Clone Wars the Separatist Council was moved there.
