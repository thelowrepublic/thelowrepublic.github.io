The **galactic regions** are the Deep Core, the Core, the Colonies, the Inner Rim, the Expansion Region, the Mid Rim and the Outer Rim. Beside them are Hutt Space and Wild Space. The galaxy is very large and almost entirely empty. Perhaps one star system in a hundred has a world anyone can live on, and most inhabited worlds hold a single town, a mine or a few farms.

## Background

The regions form rings around the centre of the galaxy: the Deep Core at its heart, then the Core, the Colonies, the Inner Rim, the Expansion Region and the Mid Rim, with the Outer Rim farthest out. Hutt Space lies on the border of the Mid and the Outer Rim, and Wild Space beyond the Outer Rim.

## The Core

The Core is a ring of worlds around the Deep Core, at the heart of the galaxy, and is itself ringed by the Colonies. It holds [[Coruscant]], the capital of the Republic, and many of the galaxy's wealthiest and most prestigious worlds, among them [[Alderaan]] and Corellia. Its place on the great hyperspace routes has made it the galaxy's hub of fashion, education, finance and technology. Core-built ships are known for their modular construction, and elsewhere Core citizens are thought of as rich and snobbish, with a clipped, practised accent. The gap between the rich Core and the poor Outer Rim has fed more than one war, the Clone Wars among them.

The Core is fully garrisoned by the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]]. In the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]], a citizen of the Core can go a lifetime without seeing a battle and will pay Collective money every week without knowing it, in the price of shipping and the cost of a permit.

At the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]], the Core worlds voted down more Senate seats for the Outer Rim. Almost all of the Core still trades with the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Union]], as the Eriadu hyperspace crossroads is too vital to simply abandon.

The [[Senate Stances|Core Isolationists]]' answer is full withdrawal of the Republic to the Core and the Colonies, focus shift towards self-sufficiency of the Core, and free trade with everything beyond it.

### The Deep Core

The Deep Core lies at the very heart of the galaxy, around the supermassive black hole that holds it together. It is brightly lit and densely packed with stars, dangerous to navigate and thinly populated, and explorers have largely left it alone. It still holds city-worlds such as Troithe, once a rival to Coruscant, and the swamp world of [[Crystan V]], where the Republic found kolto early in the [[The Long War|Long War]]. Some Jedi scholars hold that the Order's first temple stood on Tython, in the Deep Core.

### The Colonies

The Colonies are a ring-shaped region between the Core and the Inner Rim, and are counted among the inner worlds. They lie along major hyperspace trade routes, among them the Perlemian Trade Route, the Corellian Run and the Hydian Way, which have made their worlds hubs of education, fashion, finance and technology. Some of those worlds are extremely wealthy. Explorers from the Colonies and the Core settled the Inner Rim, and in the overcrowded centuries of the High Republic many of the Colonies' poor left for new lives in the Outer Rim. [[Devaron]], Carida and Cato Neimoidia are Colonies worlds. The Colonies are only sparsely garrisoned by the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], as their worlds feel protected by the Core garrisons on one side and the Inner Rim border garrisons on the other.

## The Inner Rim

The Inner Rim is a ring of worlds between the Colonies and the Expansion Region. It was settled by colonists from the Core and the Colonies and was then known simply as 'the Rim', and for centuries it was the frontier of galactic civilisation. As settlement pushed on into the Mid and Outer Rim, the Inner Rim stayed prosperous. In the Clone Wars several of its worlds sided with the Confederacy, among them Onderon, the homeworld of [[Lux Bonteri]]. Denon and Jakku are Inner Rim worlds too.

Around half of the Inner Rim is garrisoned by the Republic Corps, mostly along its borders.

### The Expansion Region

The Expansion Region is a ring of worlds between the Inner Rim and the Mid Rim. Lying between the two, its worlds were battlegrounds in the Clone Wars: clones fought the Separatists on Mimban, and in 20 BBY the Republic fought a long battle against the Umbarans, who had joined the Confederacy, and occupied Umbara afterwards. Shili, the Togruta homeworld, lies in the Expansion Region.

## The Mid Rim

The Mid Rim is a ring of worlds between the Expansion Region and the Outer Rim. It is the farthest of the regions still counted among the inner worlds. Its worlds are varied, and many have stable governments and thriving economies, but they lie too far from the Core to share its fame. [[Kashyyyk]], [[Naboo]], [[Ord Mantell]], Bothawui, Ithor and Malastare are Mid Rim worlds, and Hutt Space lies on its border with the Outer Rim.

The [[The Long War|Long War]] was fought hardest in the Mid Rim. The Taldot sector lost most of its industry and fourteen of its worlds were left completely uninhabitable. The damage spread into the Mytaranor, Lantillian, Halla and Kastolar sectors. In 14 BBY those five sectors and Numesira formed the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]]. Taldot and its worlds still lie largely barren.

Most recruits for the Corps come from the Mid Rim and the poorer worlds of the Core. In the Mid and Outer Rim, the Corps only garrisons tactically vital worlds and chokepoints along important trade lanes. There is no garrison in most of the Rim and there never has been. The Core Isolationists want the Inner, Mid and Outer Rim to gain full autonomy.

By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review. The Chommell sector, containing [[Naboo]], filed one of those petitions, and is the only Mid Rim sector to do so.

## The Outer Rim

The Outer Rim is the largest region of the galaxy and the most sparsely populated, bordered by the Mid Rim, Wild Space and the Unknown Regions. Its worlds are diverse, and many are rugged, isolated frontier planets. So far from the Core, it has never had a stable central authority, and pirates, smugglers, slavers and crime syndicates have always worked there with impunity. Slavery has stayed common despite the Republic's ban, and many of its worlds have stayed outside the Republic altogether. [[Geonosis]], [[Ryloth]], [[Utapau]], [[Mustafar]], [[Raxus]], [[Eriadu]], [[Ferrix]], [[Dathomir]] and [[Yavin 4]] are Outer Rim worlds.

After the Convention, the Outer Rim was still short of seats and short of policing, and still right in believing that the Core never thought about it. The Outer Rim sends almost no recruits to the Corps, because working for the Collective just pays better there.

Nearly all of the secession petitions in review come from Outer Rim sectors. The Republic can say that [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]'s government is exploited by a criminal organisation. This seems entirely true, but forty years of Republic negligence in the Outer Rim would only make that argument a statement of the Republic's own incompetence.

## Hutt Space

*Main article: [[The Hutt Clans]]*

Hutt Space is an autonomous region on the border of the Mid and the Outer Rim, to the galactic east of the Core, ruled by the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutt Clans]]. It has long been a haven for the galaxy's disreputable. Its worlds include Nal Hutta, the Hutts' homeworld, and Toydaria.

Halla and Kastolar border Hutt Space, and the Hutts moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. [[Nal Kaddan]] fell in 15 BBY. It took the Kashyyyk Region Coalition two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar.

The Constitutional Convention failed to write any law against slavery that could actually be enforced in or near Hutt Space.

## Wild Space

Wild Space lies beyond the Outer Rim, 'off the edge of the map'. Its star clusters and dangerous worlds have never been charted, unlike the Unknown Regions, whose borders are mapped even though their interiors are largely unexplored. People of the Core often look down on anyone from Wild Space as a backwater yokel, fellow humans included. [[Kamino]] lies in Wild Space, as do Teth and Lira San, the homeworld of the Lasat.

[[Thrawn]] was found adrift in Wild Space by an Outland Command survey in 14 BBY.
