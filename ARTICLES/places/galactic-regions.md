The **galactic regions** are the Core, the Inner Rim, the Mid Rim and the Outer Rim. Beside them are Hutt Space and Wild Space. The galaxy is very large and almost entirely empty. Perhaps one star system in a hundred has a world anyone can live on, and most inhabited worlds hold a single town, a mine or a few farms.

## Background

The regions form rings around the centre of the galaxy. The Core lies nearest the centre and the Outer Rim farthest out. The Core holds Coruscant and many of the wealthiest worlds. The Outer Rim is the largest region and the most thinly populated. Its isolated frontier worlds have long been home to pirates, smugglers and crime syndicates. Hutt Space lies on the border of the Mid and the Outer Rim, under the rule of the Hutt clans. Wild Space lies beyond the Outer Rim and is almost wholly unmapped.

## The Core

The Core is fully garrisoned by the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]]. In the [[The Shadow War|Shadow War]], a citizen of the Core can go a lifetime without seeing a battle and will pay Collective money every week without knowing it, in the price of shipping and the cost of a permit.

At the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]], the Core worlds voted down more Senate seats for the Outer Rim. Almost all of the Core still trades with the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Union]], as the Eriadu hyperspace crossroads is too vital to simply abandon.

The [[Three Stances of the Senate|Core Isolationists]]' answer is full withdrawal of the Republic to the Core and the Inner Rim, focus shift towards self-sufficiency of the Core, and free trade with everything beyond it.

## The Inner Rim

Around half of the Inner Rim is garrisoned by the Republic Corps, mostly along its borders.

## The Mid Rim

The [[The Long War|Long War]] was fought hardest in the Mid Rim. The Taldot sector lost most of its industry and fourteen of its worlds were left completely uninhabitable. The damage spread into the Mytaranor, Lantillian, Halla and Kastolar sectors. In 14 BBY those five sectors formed the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]].

Most recruits for the Corps come from the Mid Rim and the poorer worlds of the Core. In the Mid and Outer Rim, the Corps only garrisons tactically vital worlds and chokepoints along important trade lanes. There is no garrison in most of the Rim and there never has been. The Core Isolationists want the Mid and Outer Rim to gain full autonomy.

By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review. The Chommell sector, containing [[Naboo]], filed one of those petitions, and is the only Mid Rim sector to do so.

## The Outer Rim

After the Convention, the Outer Rim was still short of seats and short of policing, and still right in believing that the Core never thought about it. The Outer Rim sends almost no recruits to the Corps, because working for the Collective just pays better there.

Nearly all of the secession petitions in review come from Outer Rim sectors. The Republic can say that [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]]'s government is exploited by a criminal organisation. This seems entirely true, but forty years of Republic negligence in the Outer Rim would only make that argument a statement of the Republic's own incompetence.

## Hutt Space

*Main article: [[The Hutt Clans]]*

Halla and Kastolar border Hutt Space, and the Hutts moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. [[Nal Kaddan]] fell in 15 BBY. It took the Kashyyyk Region Coalition two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar.

The Constitutional Convention failed to write any law against slavery that could actually be enforced in or near Hutt Space.

## Wild Space

[[Thrawn]] was found adrift in Wild Space by an Outland Command survey in 14 BBY.
