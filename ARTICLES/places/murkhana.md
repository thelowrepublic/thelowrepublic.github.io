**Murkhana** is a planet where [[Anakin Skywalker]] destroyed a city in 16 BBY, as a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], on intelligence that a droid command node lay buried under it. About two hundred thousand civilians died, and the inquiry that followed reached no conclusion.

## History

*Main article: [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]]*

As a general of the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]], not a Jedi or a Chosen One, [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] destroyed a city. Intelligence said that there was a buried droid command node under it and that the population was complicit. The node was there. Whether the city's population knew about it is still debated to this day. About two hundred thousand civilians died and the campaign in that sector ended four months early. The intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. He said so at the inquiry. He said someone wanted him gone, but he could not prove it, and he sounded exactly like a man making excuses.

Skywalker's and [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s war theatres overlapped for three years and that was enough to sign the former Jedi's sentence. Skywalker fought to finish the war and Tarkin's position depended on the war continuing. The most effective commander in the Republic was in the wrong theatre, moving too fast, closing campaigns that had been budgeted for years. Of course, nothing has ever been proved. Outland Command has no surviving record of sending the reports. The inquiry reached no conclusion.
