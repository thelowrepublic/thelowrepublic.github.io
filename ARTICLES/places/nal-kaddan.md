**Nal Kaddan** is a planet at the very edge of the Kastolar sector, in the [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]], on the border of [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt Space]], and so part of the [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Kashyyyk Region Coalition]]. It was the first world to fall when the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] moved in after the [[The Long War|Long War]], and the last to be retaken by the Coalition.

## History

[[Nyra Sull]] was serving on Nal Kaddan when the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY|demilitarisation]] pulled her off it with four days' notice. She spent two of those days explaining to the population that it was an administrative decision. Nal Kaddan fell to the Hutts a year after she left it.

[[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition|Halla and Kastolar]] border [[Galactic regions#Hutt Space|Hutt Space]], and the [[The Hutt Clans|Hutts]] moved in while the sectors were still counting their dead. Nal Kaddan fell in 15 BBY. Both sectors begged for help. Nothing came out of [[Coruscant]] except condolences and empty promises.

In 14 BBY Kastolar and the four other sectors of what became the Kashyyyk Region Coalition agreed to pool what was left. It took two years to push the Hutts back out of Halla and Kastolar. Nal Kaddan was the last world to be retaken.
