**Ord Mantell** is a world of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]. In 16 BBY a magistrate on Ord Mantell died while examining the [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Outland Command]] accounts. One of [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Anakin Skywalker#The record|confirmed appearances]] was on Ord Mantell, eleven days before the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] moved on the shipyards. A [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Sector Ranger]] hired on Ord Mantell works on Ord Mantell, and no Chancellor can send that person to [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]].

## Background

Ord Mantell is a planet of the Mid Rim.
