**Utapau** is where Grievous was killed in 19 BBY, within hours of [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine]]. With Dooku also dead, the Confederacy was beheaded rather than defeated. The Separatist Council survived on [[Mustafar]], and the war went on as [[The Long War|the Long War]].

## Background

Utapau is a remote world of deep sinkholes in the Outer Rim. Its peoples are the Pau'an and the Utai. In the last days of the Clone Wars, Separatist droids occupied it and Grievous took refuge there. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was sent after him.
