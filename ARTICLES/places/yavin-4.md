**Yavin 4**, the fourth moon of Yavin Prime, lies in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]]. Its Massassi temples date from the Sith of Naga Sadow's day, and in 0 ABY it was the site of [[The Battle of Yavin]].

## History

### The temples and the dig

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig]]*

[[Maul]] registered a dig on the fourth moon of Yavin Prime with the Republic [[The Galactic Republic|Bureau of Antiquities]] in 5 BBY. It went through a shell company, on the correct forms, with the correct fees, renewed every year. It was one of eleven hundred such permits active in the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] that year and drew no attention whatsoever. The Massassi temples there were raised by the Sith of Naga Sadow's day and abandoned for four thousand years. They are also where [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig|Exar Kun]] put his spirit at the end of the Great Sith War. He escaped the destruction of his body by pouring himself into the stone, and he stayed there for four thousand years. Maul had not gone to Yavin for riches or power. He had gone to find out how Kun did it.

### The battle, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[The Battle of Yavin]]*

The landing on Yavin 4 was unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day at minimal cost. On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify [[The Nightborn#Magick|Dathomiri magick]].

The sixteen hundred survivors give accounts that are fragmentary and do not agree with one another. The Jedi who were there have refused to describe it without consulting the Order first. What is established is that the ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and that the dead did not stay down. Republic soldiers killed in the assault were raised and turned on the second wave, which had landed at dawn expecting to reinforce a victory. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. [[Mace Windu]] was supposedly among them.

On the sixth day [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. He did not pull anyone out first. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area. The order could be defended in military terms and was. Most [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] left the moon hours earlier. [[Maul]] was already gone by day four.

Nine thousand eight hundred elite Republic troops were dead, of whom over a thousand were killed by Republic guns. Yavin 4 was never retaken and is still surrounded by an interdiction fleet. The tragedy was devastating because it was recorded and the footage was all over HoloNet within hours. For the past nineteen years, the Republic tried to build an image of itself in which a great danger had been faced, defeated and legislated against. Yavin ended that narrative in six days, which the Republic will never recover from. The calendar was reset within the year, first by the news services and finally by law. There was no longer any useful way to describe the period that had just closed except as the time before this tragedy.

## In 2 ABY

Yavin 4 lies in the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Gordian Reach]], which seceded in the third month of 2 ABY quietly and without fanfare. Its Governor-Senator, [[Oleth Kar]], has issued one statement since: that the interdiction fleet in his territory is welcome to leave whenever it likes.

Withdrawing the interdiction fleet from Yavin 4 would be read everywhere as recognition, and recognition concedes that the fleet had been sitting in foreign territory for two years. Leaving it there costs nothing and admits nothing, so it stays. Oleth Kar's invitation to remove it is not just a taunt. It is an offer he knows cannot be accepted.
