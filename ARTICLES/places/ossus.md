**Ossus** is the world to which the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] withdrew from [[Coruscant]], starting in 15 BBY. Since 0 ABY it has been the seat of the [[Ossus Order]].

## History

### The move, 15 BBY

*Main article: [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The move to Ossus, 15 BBY]]*

The Temple on [[Coruscant]] had been an excavation since 18 BBY. Investigators had been through every level of it. The shrine underneath had been opened, catalogued, photographed and argued about in the Senate. The Jedi Archives were turned upside down for any trace of Sith manipulation. The Council voted to leave in 15 BBY and the move took two years. The [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Order]] went to Ossus. It had been a Jedi world when the Republic was young, it held the ruins of the old library, and it had the considerable advantage of belonging to nobody. The Temple was left standing empty under Republic caretakership, and it stayed empty for thirteen years.

### The clone settlements

As the Jedi left Coruscant, they left an open invitation for all [[Clones|clone veterans]] to join them. Several thousand clones went immediately, and it's estimated that over the years Ossus has been a place where over eighty thousand clones lived out the rest of their days. By 2 ABY, around twenty thousand are still alive, albeit most elderly. Some started families, as several frontier villages sprang up on Ossus, at a respectful distance from the Jedi Temple. [[Shaak Ti]] is a liaison between the Temple and clone communities in those settlements, personally overseeing the wellbeing of remaining clones and doing her best to prolong their time with Force rituals. She has seen moderate success at it, though she did determine that children of clones are perfectly healthy and do not suffer from accelerated ageing or other health complications.

The settlements were made both by clones and by [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]] refugees seeking the protection of the Jedi.

Three thousand of the [[The Manumission Act#The ones still growing|clone children]] the Republic put up for adoption were adopted directly by the Jedi Order when it moved. Some of them founded the [[Ossus Order#Temple Troopers|Temple Troopers]], who still guard the Temple.

### The split, 0 ABY

*Main article: [[Ossus Order#The split, 0 ABY]]*

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the Republic made the Order an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The traditionalists were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to Coruscant. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on Ossus.

## In 2 ABY

Ossus has recovered from the cataclysm it suffered in the ancient past. It is now mostly wide open plains and sparse forests, and it is not exceedingly fertile. Patches of truly fertile land are rare, and settlers usually have to acquire agritech to make farming possible. Some small areas of the planet are still wasteland. Settlements close to them are at risk whenever high winds blow radioactive dust towards them.

The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] has worked its way subtly onto Ossus. The only thing it does there is charity work for the new settlements, such as supplying agritech, and offhand offers of jobs and transport off-world.

The [[Ossus Order]] has sent one Jedi into every settlement. Officially each is a 'temple liaison', meant to live in the settlement full-time, be part of the community and alert the Temple if there is danger. Unofficially they are called 'Jedi sheriffs', and they help their communities in whatever way they can. Some of them are fully morally upstanding. Some have taken to working with the Collective for the benefit of their community.
