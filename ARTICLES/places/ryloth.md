**Ryloth** is the homeworld of [[Nadi Orl]] of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]], who has come back to it and become the head of the ruling council of the city of Kala'uun. [[Ahsoka Tano]] was confirmed there nine days after [[Anakin Skywalker]].

## Background

Ryloth is a planet of the Outer Rim, the homeworld of the Twi'leks. In the Clone Wars the Separatists occupied it. [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Mace Windu]], with the Twi'lek freedom fighters of Cham Syndulla, retook it for the Republic.

## In 2 ABY

[[Nadi Orl]] has since come back to her homeworld of Ryloth, using the immense wealth she accrued from running the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]] to set herself up publicly as a philanthropist entrepreneur. She has invested immense funds into the city of Kala'uun, turning it from a meagre starport into a bustling resort metropolis, with her as the head of the ruling council. 

Most in the Collective simply regard this as 'Orl's pet project', but the woman herself seems to be completely genuine in her desire to elevate Ryloth into prominence. She outright banned Collective activity on Ryloth unless she authorises it personally, and sent very violent warnings to several corporations that wished to extract Ryloth's natural resources. She even attempts to integrate indigenous Twi'lek clans into her plans as tourist guides and spiritual gurus for hire, though she has immense difficulties trying to come to any sort of agreement with the Clan Assembly.
