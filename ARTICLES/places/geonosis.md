**Geonosis** is the world of the Geonosians. Superweapon plans found there under the Republic's occupation became the secret [[The Republic Remade#The Geonosis Programme|Geonosis Programme]].

## Background

Geonosis is a desert world in the Outer Rim. Its foundries built battle droids for the Confederacy. In 22 BBY the first Battle of Geonosis opened the Clone Wars. The Republic later invaded the planet again.

## History

[[Gizor Dellso]], a Geonosian, was a Techno Union designer until Geonosis fell. He was never charged, having held no rank the tribunals recognised. He is now chief engineer of the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]]'s droid programme.

Superweapon plans of Geonosian design, recovered during the occupation in 18 BBY, became a secret programme under the Ministry of Security, paid for out of a budget line marked planetary defence. The new [[The Galactic Republic#The Investigative Office|Investigative Office]] exposed it in 11 BBY at roughly eleven percent built. The hull was scrapped.
