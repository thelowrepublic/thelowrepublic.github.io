**Voss** is a world, and the people who live on it. It has been on no Republic chart for four thousand years, because its mystics have kept up a concealment over the entire system, worked through the Force. Since roughly two hundred withdrawalists from the [[The Reckoning of the Jedi|Jedi Order]] made the crossing, it has also been the home of the [[Order of Voss]].

## History

### The veil

The world is on no Republic chart and has not been for four thousand years. Its people are unusually strong in the Force. Their mystics have kept up a concealment over the entire system, without a break, since the Old Republic. It is not a jamming field and it is not a scrambled navigation chart. It is worked directly through the Force. It turns attention away from that stretch of space without leaving any sign that it has done so. Ships do not fail to find Voss; they fail to look for it.

The Voss did this after the Sith and the Jedi both came for them in the ancient wars and neither left anything behind worth keeping. They decided to be absent from galactic history, and they held to it for four thousand years. This deception would have failed, if there was still someone in the Galaxy who understood the Force to the same degree that the Voss did.

### The arrivals

*Main article: [[Order of Voss]]*

Roughly two hundred of the [[Order of Voss#The withdrawalists|withdrawalists]] made the crossing.

The welcome was not warm. The Voss had spent four thousand years making sure this exact thing would not happen, and the arrivals were, whatever they called themselves now, Jedi. There was confinement, a long argument, and at least one death that neither side has ever discussed. What settled it was a vision. The Voss treat visions as instructions, but revealing them too early can cause undue steps and excessive worry. Mystics reveal their visions when they deem the time is right. The arrivals were let in on that basis and on no other.

## In 2 ABY

[[Ren-Vashal]] saw the Jedi coming years before they arrived and said nothing until they stood on the surface of his planet. For the Voss, a vision tells a mystic what will happen but does not license them to interfere with how it will happen. He is the reason the [[Order of Voss]] exists and yet he holds no position in it.
