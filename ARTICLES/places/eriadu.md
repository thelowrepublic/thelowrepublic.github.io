**Eriadu** is a planet in the Seswenna sector. It is the home of [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], who was elected governor of the sector after he came home in 10 BBY. Seswenna voted to leave the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] in 1 ABY, and Eriadu is now part of the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]]. Its hyperspace crossroads is too vital for the Core to simply abandon, and the [[The Grand Union Army|Union Army]] patrols its streets.

## Background

Eriadu is a terrestrial planet in the Eriadu system of the Seswenna sector, located on the Outer Rim. The planet features terrain such as mountains and jungles, and has a black pall. 

The world's chief industry and export revolves around lommite ore, which is abundant. More than its industry, Eriadu is known as a hyperspace crossroads. A hub of trade born from two intersecting super-hyperroutes: the Hydian Way and the Rimma Trade Route. Eriadu is also the homeworld of the Tarkin family.

## History

[[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] resigned his commission in 10 BBY, went home to Eriadu, and was elected governor of the Seswenna sector. Over the following decade he drew the neighbouring frontier sectors into an efficient, prosperous and orderly bloc. Elections went on being held and stopped being competitive. He kept being re-elected term after term, through an overwhelming majority. Coruscant put up with the arrangement because Seswenna kept the shipping lanes safe, paid what it owed, and cleaned up its own droid remnants without asking for Republic troops.

[[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing|The bad briefing]] was established in the last months of 0 ABY. The Outland Command's secret arrangement with [[Maul]] was published alongside it. Tarkin was summoned to answer for both, but Seswenna did not hand him over.

The people of Seswenna saw the Core's investigation as an attempt to tarnish the name of the man who had given them almost twenty years of stability. The Republic lost almost ten thousand soldiers due to its own incompetence and needed someone to blame. Most of the sector believed this sincerely, and all news that reached Eriadu through legal channels reinforced their stance. Seswenna's government voted to leave the Republic in the fourth month of 1 ABY. Eight neighbouring sectors supported it and voted for their own secessions as well, soon after forming a sector confederation, the independent Greater Seswenna Union.

There was no clause in the charter for the Republic to point at and none to break, so there was also no real way to stop it. The Senate condemned the withdrawal, refused to recognise it, and did nothing else.

## In 2 ABY

Almost all of the Core still trades with the Union, as the Eriadu hyperspace crossroads is too vital to simply abandon.

The [[The Grand Union Army|Union Army]] is the state's only force on the ground. There are no [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Sector Rangers]] and no Judicial Forces under [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]'s new government. The same elite soldiers who hold the sector borders are also the ones that patrol the streets of Eriadu. That is how the Union found the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s people inside it, and no other state has managed the same.

In the shipyards of Eriadu there are at least ten new drydocks, each of them nineteen kilometres long. The Union has not stated what they are for.
