**Ferrix** is a world on the Hydian Way, run by corporate authority. The [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] took over its corporate offices from within, after the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] had answered four years of complaints with instructional videos. Since Quelii seceded, a Republic garrison has cleared the Collective out and returned the world to its corporate administrators.

## History

*Main article: [[Cassian Andor]]*

[[Cassian Andor]] spent four years asking the Republic to look into the blatant corruption of corporate authority on Ferrix. The only answer he received was instructional videos on 'how to mitigate friction between local workers and corporate oversight'. When all other options ran dry he bargained with [[The Shadow Collective#Crimson Dawn|Crimson Dawn]], and they showed up after two months, in force. After four months, the Collective assets were embedded deep inside Ferrix's corporate offices and the population was no longer worked to the bone.

After [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] seceded, the Republic finally showed up on Ferrix to set up a garrison. The [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]] found out about the criminals running the planet and quickly exterminated all Collective assets on Ferrix, handing the planet back to a new batch of paper pushers from the Corporate Sector. It was clear they needed a foothold along the Hydian Way, but they defended their actions as 'following up on requests for aid from years ago'.
