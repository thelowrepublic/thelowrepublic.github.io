**Myrkr** is a planet where the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] gathers ysalamiri for its [[The Shadow War#What each side fields|anti-Force programmes]], the strangest line in its budget. Over a year and a great deal of money have gone into securing and cordoning off the planet Myrkr to gather ysalamiri, a species of lizards that push the Force out of a bubble around them, and roughly two hundred of those are currently alive off-world. The programmes are fuelled by the paranoia of the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] rising to prominence.
