**Dathomir** was the home of the Nightsister clans until General [[The Long War|Grievous]] broke them in a massacre in 21 BBY. Rebuilt by [[Maul]], it is now the nearest thing to a capital that the [[The Shadow Collective|Shadow Collective]] has, and the heart of the [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]].

## Background

Dathomir is a remote planet in the Quelii sector of the [[Galactic regions#The Outer Rim|Outer Rim]], strongly influenced by the dark side of the Force. It is dimly lit in blood-red light, with swamps and forests, and dangerous predators such as rancors. Several clans of witches there practised magick, the Nightsisters among them, and they ruled over the Nightbrothers, the men of the planet, who were their warriors.

## History

### The massacre

Dathomir was massacred by General [[The Long War|Grievous]] in 21 BBY. The Nightsister clans were finished as an organised power and the survivors scattered.

### The return

[[Maul]] was a son of that world, taken from it as a child and raised as a weapon by a master who threw him away. He came back in 10 BBY and spent four years rebuilding. He cleared the ruins and found survivors across a dozen sectors. The most important of them was [[Merrin]], who had been seven years old when the massacre happened and had spent eleven years as the last practising witch of her clan.

The Nightsister tradition had been closed to men for four thousand years. The Nightbrothers of the Dathomiri villages had been breeding stock and labour for it. Maul himself had been born into that arrangement, one he would not be willing to fund, so Merrin opened the practice to them. She chose [[The Nightborn|Nightborn]] because Nightsister no longer described what she was building, and the word spread outward from her branch to cover the whole order.

## In 2 ABY

*Main article: [[The Shadow War#Why Quelii cannot be attacked]]*

[[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] is the one place that looks like a target. Dathomir is the closest thing the Collective has to a capital and everything the Nightborn are is made there. Taking it would be expensive but it would not be impossible. If the Republic committed itself, it could probably do it. That is not what stops it. The Republic's strength was never its army or fleet. It was that a thousand sectors believed membership was safer than leaving. Nothing enforces that belief. There is no garrison in most of the Rim and there never has been. Order holds because the Republic has spent centuries being the benevolent authority that does not come for you.

A fifty-six-year-old Maul amasses power on Dathomir, when he is there at all. He presides over six hundred people who have stopped needing him. His order was founded on a promise that none of its members would ever be a tool. It is run day to day by Merrin who merely wants her people to be allowed to exist, and by [[Barriss Offee]] who has been making the same argument towards the Jedi Order since she was nineteen. He has told neither of them what he was looking for at Yavin. Forty kilometres away in the eastern highlands, [[Asajj Ventress]] holds the [[The Nightsisters|old rite]], attempts a second shot at a peaceful life and diligently avoids contact with [[Quinlan Vos]] for reasons only she knows. Maul cares for none of that, his own paranoia causing him to be more distant and reckless with each passing month. [[Luke Skywalker]] went rogue and is currently on Maul's trail.
