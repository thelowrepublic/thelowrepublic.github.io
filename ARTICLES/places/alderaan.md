**Alderaan** is the home of [[Bail Organa]], Chancellor from 19 to 14 BBY. He went home to it after his years in office, and he is still active in its government. Alderaan is intact.

## Background

Alderaan is a world of the Core. Bail Organa represented it in the Senate during the Clone Wars.
