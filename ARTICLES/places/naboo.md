**Naboo** is a world of the Chommell sector, in the [[Galactic regions#The Mid Rim|Mid Rim]]. In 2 ABY its governor filed a petition for the Chommell sector to secede.

## Background

Naboo is home to the Gungans and to a human people, the Naboo. The Gungan city, Otoh Gunga, lies beneath one of its lakes. Palpatine represented it in the Senate before he became Chancellor. In 32 BBY the Trade Federation invaded it. Queen [[Padmé Amidala]] freed it with the help of the Gungans, and she later became its senator.

## History

Naboo recalled [[Jar Jar Binks]] in 18 BBY. He performed for children in Otoh Gunga, turned down every interview request, and never spoke publicly about the war.

[[Padmé Amidala]] chaired the [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]] of 13 to 11 BBY. Through it all, she had to fight from the senatorial seat of Naboo, a place that has been more scorned by the day, a place that produced Palpatine and Binks. Her career ended in 9 BBY when the [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana|Murkhana]] compromise finally caught up with her. She went home to Naboo, represented it in the Senate, and raised the twins largely alone.

[[Ruko Tass]] left Naboo at fifteen, a world that had produced [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|a Sith Lord and a galactic clown]], a fact which Tass was always reminded of.

[[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Quelii]] left the Republic in 2 ABY. The Governor of Naboo, [[Quarsh Panaka]], filed the Chommell sector secession petition seven weeks after Quelii left and has never explained it past the filing.

## In 2 ABY

By the end of 2 ABY the Ministry of the Interior had over sixty formal secession petitions sitting in review, nearly all of them from Outer Rim sectors. Each of them is being reviewed, because refusing to review them would itself be a decision. The Chommell sector, containing Naboo, filed one of those petitions, and is the only Mid Rim sector to do so. [[Leia Skywalker]] holds one of the sector's two Senate seats.
