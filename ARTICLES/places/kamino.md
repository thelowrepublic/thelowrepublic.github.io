**Kamino** is the home of the Kaminoans, who grew the Republic's clone army. The clone contract was cancelled in 17 BBY, but the Kaminoans were kept on to raise the clones still growing.

## Background

Kamino is an ocean world beyond the Outer Rim. It is the home of the Kaminoan cloners. In 22 BBY [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] found it, though it was missing from the Jedi Archives. He found the clone army being grown there.

## History

The largest single item in the Republic's history was the Kaminoan clone contract. It had been ordered in the name of the murdered Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas and run after his death by [[The Long War|Dooku]].

Republic [[The Kamino Findings|auditors]] reached Kamino in 18 BBY with the power to demand any record they wanted. Jedi Master [[Shaak Ti]] went with them. The Kaminoans had broken no law of their own. They kept meticulous paperwork proving they had followed their client's instructions exactly, and they produced those instructions.

Production of [[Clones|clone]] batches stopped in 17 BBY, but a clone takes ten years to reach deployment age. Everyone decanted after 27 BBY was still growing when the contract was cancelled. There were about two million of them, and the youngest were only days old. Although the main production contract was cancelled, the Kaminoans were kept on under a stripped-down agreement. Accusations and allegations about the immorality of their cloning practices were quietly dropped, in exchange for the Kaminoans taking on the duty to see the [[The Manumission Act#The ones still growing|underaged clones]] to adulthood, because they already had the facilities and know-how to raise them. The work was done at cost and without enthusiasm on either side.
