**Crystan V** is a swamp world in the Crystan system, in the Deep Core, and the home of the Crystanian Rybets. Its swamps yield kolto, a healing agent the galaxy had not seen for centuries. Since the Republic found it early in the [[The Long War|Long War]], the Crystan system has grown rich, and its best-known native, [[Sevrin Dal]], is now Chancellor of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]].

## The Crystanian Rybets

The natives of Crystan V are the Crystanian Rybets, metre-tall amphibian sentients and one of several Rybet peoples in the galaxy. They were already harvesting kolto from the swamps of their world when the Republic found them.

## History

### Discovery

Early in the [[The Long War|Long War]], a Separatist general tried to reach [[Coruscant]] by way of the Deep Core, as General Grievous had done in the Clone Wars. The attempt failed badly. The Republic intercepted the fleet near the Crystan system, and its own ships came out of the fighting in need of repairs and put in at Crystan V. There their crews met the Crystanian Rybets and saw what they took from their swamps.

Kolto had not been seen in the galaxy for centuries. Bacta had largely replaced it, and its main sources on Manaan had dried up. The news reached the Senate as fast as it could travel, and Chancellor [[Bail Organa]] quickly arranged trade talks with the Rybets. A less scrupulous Chancellor would have seen a world to exploit. Organa oversaw the agreement personally, made sure it was fair, and saw that it benefited the Rybets greatly.

### The kolto boom

The kolto of Crystan V was the main reason the Republic could fight the [[The Long War|Long War]] for as long as it did. The Crystan system became prosperous in a very short time, and kolto barons sprang up within months. One of them, [[Sevrin Dal]], used his wealth and influence to become senator for the Crystan system, and in 0 ABY he was elected Chancellor.

## In 2 ABY

Crystan V is still the only known source of kolto in the galaxy, and the Crystan system remains a loyal and prosperous member of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]. Its best-known native, [[Sevrin Dal]], has been Chancellor since 0 ABY.
