**Crystan V** is a swamp world in the Crystan system, in the Deep Core, and the home of the six-armed Crystanian Rybets. Its swamps yield kolto, a healing agent the galaxy had not seen for centuries. Since the Republic found it early in the [[The Long War|Long War]], the Crystan system has grown rich, and its best-known native, [[Sevrin Dal]], is now Chancellor of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]].

## The world

Most of Crystan V is a single gigantic ocean. Its one landmass is swamp from end to end, denser in some places than in others, and broken in some areas by hot springs and geysers. No other planet in the system is habitable by nature. Crystan I to IV are gas giants, and the system's only other rocky planet, Crystan VI, has been the object of efforts to terraform and settle it for the past decade.

## The Crystanian Rybets

The natives of Crystan V are the Crystanian Rybets, metre-tall, six-armed amphibian sentients and one of several Rybet peoples in the galaxy. Their culture has no notion of the Force, and tests suggest they have the lowest Force-sensitivity ever recorded in a sentient organic being. That explains how they have survived in the Deep Core, a region notoriously unstable in both the physical and the mystical sense.

The Rybets live in small clans, each in a stilt-town above the swamp. Their technology ran on steam until the Republic's accidental first contact, and they have caught up quickly since. The clans have grown, but their structure has not changed. The Rybets are intensely pragmatic, social and communal, and a clan shares its wealth among all its members. Few of them ever leave Crystan V, traders apart.

## Kolto

The Rybets gather kolto from what they call 'giving pools': waterlogged tunnels deep in the swamps that stretch far down into the planet. It cannot be taken in large batches. Once a pool has been drawn from, the harvesters must wait for the kolto to 'float up' again, so there is no fear of it running out.

The Rybets harvest it without trouble. Any other organic being who goes deep into the swamps suffers intense hallucinations and panic, worse the further they go. The few Jedi who have experienced it say it is the work of the Force. Sceptics say it is local toxic flora to which the Rybets are immune.

## History

### Discovery

Early in the [[The Long War|Long War]], a Separatist general tried to reach [[Coruscant]] by way of the Deep Core, as General Grievous had done in the Clone Wars. The attempt failed badly. The Republic intercepted the fleet near the Crystan system, and its own ships came out of the fighting in need of repairs and put in at Crystan V. There their crews made the Rybets' first contact with the galaxy, by accident, and saw what they took from their swamps.

Kolto had not been seen in the galaxy for centuries. Bacta had largely replaced it, and its main sources on Manaan had dried up. The news reached the Senate as fast as it could travel, and Chancellor [[Bail Organa]] quickly arranged trade talks with the Rybets. A less scrupulous Chancellor would have seen a world to exploit. Organa oversaw the agreement personally, made sure it was fair, and saw that it benefited the Rybets greatly.

With the Republic at war with the corporations, Organa wrote the agreement to keep the planet's wealth out of their reach. The local communities hold complete, exclusive and perpetual rights to the natural resources of Crystan V. They may sell to whomever they like, but kolto sold directly to the Republic's military or its healthcare institutions is exempt from tax altogether.

### The kolto boom

The kolto of Crystan V was the main reason the Republic could fight the [[The Long War|Long War]] for as long as it did. The Crystan system became prosperous in a very short time, and kolto barons sprang up within months. 'Kolto baron' is an outsiders' term. The Rybets call such a person, in rough translation, a 'provider of great fortune', and the fortune is shared across the clan. One of them, [[Sevrin Dal]], used his wealth and influence to become senator for the Crystan system, and in 0 ABY he was elected Chancellor.

## In 2 ABY

Crystan V is still the only known source of kolto in the galaxy, and the Crystan system remains a loyal and prosperous member of the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]]. Organa's agreement still stands. Many have tried to change it since, and it has held against every attempt. For all his faults, [[Sevrin Dal]] is one of its fiercest defenders.
