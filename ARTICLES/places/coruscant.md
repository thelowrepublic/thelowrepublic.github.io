**Coruscant** is the world from which the [[The Galactic Republic|Republic]] is governed. After the Jedi Order left for [[Ossus]], its Jedi Temple stood empty for thirteen years, and since 0 ABY it has been the base of the [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]].

## Background

Coruscant is a planet of the Core, covered by a single city. It is the capital of the Republic, and the seat of the Galactic Senate and the Supreme Chancellor. The Jedi Temple stood there, home of the Jedi Order and its High Council.

## History

### The Jedi Temple

For eleven hours on the second night after [[The Death of Palpatine|Palpatine's death]], the Coruscant Guard ringed the Jedi Temple while the Senate argued about whether it had just watched a coup. A later inquiry established that the Guard's commander had received no orders of any kind. He had acted on his own initiative to make sure nobody would try to escalate the situation.

Digging beneath the Jedi Temple turned up an ancient Sith shrine over which the Temple had been deliberately built. The Council chamber stood directly above it. The Order had a little over ten thousand members when the Clone Wars began, and close to two thousand of them died in the two wars. Five hundred Jedi resigned within eighteen months. The question that drove them out was not why the Order had failed to spot Sidious; that was already obvious. It was what the Order had been meditating on top of for a thousand years.

The Temple on Coruscant had been an excavation since 18 BBY. Investigators had been through every level of it. The shrine underneath had been opened, catalogued, photographed and argued about in the Senate. The Jedi Archives were turned upside down for any trace of Sith manipulation. The Council voted to leave in 15 BBY and the move took two years. The Order went to [[Ossus]]. It had been a Jedi world when the Republic was young, it held the ruins of the old library, and it had the considerable advantage of belonging to nobody. The Temple was left standing empty under Republic caretakership, and it stayed empty for thirteen years.

As the Jedi left Coruscant, they left an open invitation for all [[Ossus#The clone settlements|clone veterans]] to join them.

Fifteen weeks after [[The Battle of Yavin|Yavin]] the Republic made the Order an offer: rank restored, the Coruscant Temple returned as a base of operations, and a formal place in the war. The Council at Ossus did not put it to a vote, since it was obvious within a day that a vote would break the Order in half whichever way it went. The Council ruled instead that every member would answer for themselves. The traditionalists were gone after eleven days, and roughly twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to Coruscant. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on Ossus.

The Coruscant body took the name [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] within the year. The Templars moved back into a Temple with a [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The shrine, 18 BBY|Sith shrine]] under it. The shrine was sealed and reconsecrated in the first month and the Templars consider the matter closed. [[Ossus Order|Ossus]] raises it in every communication between the two bodies without fail, and has been asked to stop pestering.

## In 2 ABY

The Senate judges the [[The Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers|Judicial Forces and the Sector Rangers]] only by two numbers. One is how much money went into a sector, and the other is how fast its crime rate fell. Nothing else reaches Coruscant. Where the local authority is honest, the arrangement works very well. Where it is not, the Republic is unknowingly paying for the [[The Shadow Collective|Collective]]'s police, and the crime is still kept in check, just for a different reason.
