The dated events of the Low Republic, from the ancient past to the end of 2 ABY. Years count from the Battle of Yavin. Where the history gives only a span of years, the event is listed under the first of them. Within a year, entries follow the history's order where it gives one. Entries dated only by the year come last.

## Before 19 BBY

### Four thousand years ago

- The Sith and the Jedi both came for the [[Voss]] in the ancient wars. Neither left anything behind worth keeping. The Voss mystics hid their system behind a concealment worked through the Force. They have held it without a break ever since.
- At the end of the Great Sith War, Exar Kun escaped the destruction of his body. He poured himself into the stone of the Massassi temples on [[Yavin 4]]. He stayed there for four thousand years.

### A thousand years ago

- During the Republic Dark Age, the Candorian plague killed two-thirds of the galaxy's population. Every generation since has been smaller than the one before, until the last few decades. See [[The Census of 4 BBY]].

### Several centuries ago

- The High Republic: a confident, expanding, brightly lit age. The Jedi still taught it to their younglings.

### 22 BBY

- [[Jar Jar Binks]] moved the motion that gave Palpatine emergency powers.

### 21 BBY

- General Grievous massacred [[Dathomir]]. The Nightsister clans were finished as an organised power. [[Merrin]] was seven years old.
- [[Shaak Ti]] was assured that the clone trooper Fives was suffering from a tumour. See [[The Kamino Findings#The audit]].
- [[Quinlan Vos]] went undercover against Dooku. He did not come back the same man.
- Between 21 and 15 BBY, at least nine independent studies concluded that many Separatist droids displayed sentience. See [[The Republic Remade#The droid papers]].

## The Long War, 19–15 BBY

### 19 BBY

- Four days before Palpatine's death, [[Ahsoka Tano]] and the 501st Legion captured [[Maul]] on [[Mandalore]].
- Four Jedi entered the Chancellor's office to arrest him. Agen Kolar, Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were dead within nine seconds. [[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived and did not intervene. [[Mace Windu]] [[The Death of Palpatine|killed Palpatine]].
- Grievous was killed on Utapau within hours. Dooku was dead. The Separatist Council survived on Mustafar.
- At dawn Windu presented himself at the Judiciary Building and handed over his lightsaber.
- [[Mas Amedda]] became acting Chancellor for six weeks.
- On the second night the Coruscant Guard ringed the Jedi Temple for eleven hours.
- Skywalker's testimony destroyed Palpatine's reputation within a week. His marriage to [[Padmé Amidala]] became public.
- In the confusion Maul escaped and reached Mandalorian space within the month.
- The Confederacy broke into two dozen regional commands and fought on. [[The Long War]] had begun.
- Windu's trial began. It lasted two years. See [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The Windu trial, 19–17 BBY]].
- [[Bail Organa]] became Chancellor.
- Four months after Palpatine's death, Padmé Amidala survived the birth of twins, [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker]].
- [[Barriss Offee]] bombed the Jedi Temple.
- Asajj Ventress was reported killed during a Jedi operation.
- Kashyyyk was held at enormous cost.

### 18 BBY

- Republic auditors reached Kamino, and Shaak Ti went with them. Their findings were published within six days, over the objections of the Ministry of Defence. Nobody has established who gave the order to release them. See [[The Kamino Findings]].
- Within weeks of the findings, the Senate began raising the [[The Republic Corps|Republic Corps]]. The work took two years.
- The clones' chips came out between 18 and 17 BBY, and every surviving trooper was cleared.
- Digging beneath the Jedi Temple turned up an ancient Sith shrine. Five hundred Jedi resigned within eighteen months. See [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The shrine, 18 BBY]].
- Superweapon plans of Geonosian design were recovered during the occupation of [[Geonosis]]. They became a secret programme under the Ministry of Security. See [[The Republic Remade#The Geonosis Programme]].
- Shu Mai vanished with a great deal of the Commerce Guild's ready money. She was never found.
- Naboo recalled Jar Jar Binks.

### 17 BBY

- Clone production stopped, and the Kaminoan contract was cancelled. The Kaminoans were kept on to raise the clones still growing, about two million of them. See [[The Manumission Act#The ones still growing]].
- Fives was cleared posthumously.
- Windu was acquitted. He resigned from the Council.
- Anakin Skywalker left the Order. He was hired again as a civilian and made a general in the Republic Corps.
- [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] held the Outland Command. He made contact with Maul. The earliest surviving record is a supply manifest from that year, though the arrangement was almost certainly older. It lasted until 1 BBY.
- A prototype third pattern of clone armour was in field trials with the Outland Command. The local garrisons kept it. See [[The Grand Union Army]].

### 16 BBY

- The [[The Manumission Act|Manumission Act]] made every clone a citizen. Eight million clones were living, and two million of them were children.
- About half the adult clones signed on again and served in the Auxiliary Corps. Most of them died before the Long War ended.
- Jedi military rank was abolished. Several thousand Jedi were called home from the people who had come to rely on them. See [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#Demilitarisation, 16 BBY]].
- The Republic's shadow troopers were discharged like everybody else. See [[The Shadow Troopers]].
- At [[Murkhana]], Anakin Skywalker destroyed a city as a general of the Republic Corps. About two hundred thousand civilians died. He said at the inquiry that the intelligence had come to him through Outland Command. Nothing has ever been proved. See [[Anakin Skywalker#Murkhana]].
- A magistrate on [[Ord Mantell]] died while examining the Outland Command accounts. Two admirals who asked about the same accounts died within the year.

### 15 BBY

- The Concord of Raxus ended the Long War. [[Lux Bonteri]] led the negotiations. See [[The Long War#The Concord of Raxus, 15 BBY]].
- Senator Fang Zar of Sern Prime was shot in a transit tunnel. He was due to give evidence the following week.
- [[Nal Kaddan]] fell to the Hutts.
- The Jedi Council voted to leave Coruscant. The move to [[Ossus]] took two years. The Temple stood empty for thirteen years.
- The Auxiliary Corps began to stand down.
- The Murkhana inquiry called [[C-3PO]]. Data pulled from him ended two careers.

## 15 BBY to 0 ABY

### 14 BBY

- Nute Gunray and Rune Haako were tried and convicted.
- Bail Organa stepped down. Ars Vehn became Chancellor.
- The Governor of Taldot called four neighbouring sectors together. They agreed to pool what was left. See [[The Kashyyyk Region Coalition]].
- Anakin Skywalker was discharged with honour.
- The schism over attachment opened in the Order. See [[The Reckoning of the Jedi#The schism, 14–8 BBY]].
- Lux Bonteri became senator for [[Raxus]].
- An Outland Command survey found [[Thrawn]] adrift in Wild Space.

### 13 BBY

- The [[The Republic Remade#The Constitutional Convention, 13–11 BBY|Constitutional Convention]] met, with Padmé Amidala in the chair.

### 12 BBY

- The last Auxiliary regiment stood down.
- [[Wullf Yularen]] became director of the reformed [[Republic Intelligence]].
- From this year the Collective absorbed several hundred local syndicates.
- [[Wolffe]] was discharged. No civilian employer would take him.

### 11 BBY

- The Convention closed. Emergency powers now expired after ninety days unless the Senate voted to keep them. No general has led troops in person since, with Obi-Wan Kenobi the sole exception.
- [[Mon Mothma]] became the first Chancellor to serve under the new charter.
- The Investigative Office exposed the Geonosis Programme at roughly eleven percent built. Vehn was impeached. The hull was scrapped.
- Yularen opened a file on the Outland Command accounts.

### 10 BBY

- Tarkin resigned his commission and went home to [[Eriadu]]. He was elected governor of the Seswenna sector.
- Maul came back to Dathomir and spent four years rebuilding.
- [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] retired to Ossus and taught.
- [[Nyra Sull]] left the Order without warning.

### 9 BBY

- Barriss Offee was released. Within a month, Maul had swayed her to his side.
- [[Asajj Ventress]] reappeared after years as a bounty hunter under several aliases. She accepted an invitation from Maul.
- Padmé Amidala's career ended when the Murkhana compromise caught up with her.
- [[Morgan Elsbeth]] came back to Dathomir for the rebuilding. Nyra Sull arrived in Barriss Offee's first intake.
- A Trade Federation battleship, gutted in 19 BBY, was towed into the Gordian Reach and stripped to its foundries. Its control core, [[Patient Yield]], was never shut down.

### 8 BBY

- The Ossus Compact partly settled the schism. Six hundred and thirty-two Jedi resigned in the eleven weeks after it, [[Aayla Secura]] among them.
- Ahsoka Tano withdrew to the Outer Rim. [[Rex]] lived with her.
- The Pyke Syndicate began selling itself to the Hutts and the Collective at once.

### 7 BBY

- [[Yoda]] died at Ossus, a few years short of nine hundred.
- Wolffe joined Crimson Dawn and became a recruiter.

### 6 BBY

- The pension correction for the clones passed. By then most of the men it was meant for were dead.
- The Investigative Office published the droid papers, together with the memoranda showing who had ordered them suppressed.
- Anakin Skywalker was in the building when the Coruscant procurement fraud broke. He was the reason the auditor reached a magistrate alive.
- [[Qi'ra]] took over Crimson Dawn.
- Between 6 and 5 BBY, many withdrawalists reported a place in the Force where their attention would not go. About two hundred of them made the crossing to Voss. See [[Order of Voss#The withdrawalists]].

### 5 BBY

- Maul registered a dig on the fourth moon of Yavin Prime with the Bureau of Antiquities, through a shell company. See [[The Battle of Yavin#The dig]].
- Rex died in his sleep, in Ahsoka Tano's care.
- Anakin Skywalker made his first confirmed use of Force lightning, against Collective enforcers on Nar Shaddaa.

### 4 BBY

- The census report was published. See [[The Census of 4 BBY]].
- [[Kalani]] was found on Agamar with a small army of fully functional battle droids.
- Death Watch began working the old vaults of Mandalore.
- A ranger on Vandor asked Anakin Skywalker what he was doing there. He answered that he did not know.
- The Collective ordered the Pyke Syndicate out of the poorest systems of the Rim. Two years of conflict followed. See [[Sarn Vekh]].

### 3 BBY

- Maul and Ventress fought at Nightspire. Neither died. Ventress left the same night with twenty of the fifty Dathomiri witches present and two hundred others. See [[The Nightsisters#Nightspire]].
- Anakin Skywalker walked into the hostage situation on Cato Neimoidia.
- The [[Order of Voss]] began to appear at incidents in Republic space.
- Crimson Dawn priced [[Hondo Ohnaka]] out of four routes.

### 2 BBY

- Yularen closed his file on Tarkin.
- The conflict over the Pyke Syndicate ended. Its main body went to the Hutts, and [[Sarn Vekh]] took about a fifth of it and stayed loyal to Maul as the Shadow Pykes.

### 1 BBY

- In the first weeks of the year, Yularen laid the Outland Command evidence before the Chancellor and the investigators. Tarkin did not deny the connection. His arrangement with Maul ended.
- Within four months Tarkin had become the loudest voice in the Republic for destroying the Shadow Collective. He was given command of the operation.
- [[Han Solo]] ran four loads out to the Yavin 4 dig.
- Three months before Yavin, Leia Skywalker took the first Chommell seat in the Senate, at eighteen.

## The Shadow War, 0–2 ABY

### 0 ABY

- The Jedi Council debated for four days and refused to be involved. Thirty-one Jedi went anyway. The Council disowned them on the day the fleet jumped. See [[The Battle of Yavin#The Jedi who went]].
- Anakin Skywalker arrived on Yavin 4 a day ahead of the fleet, uninvited and unannounced.
- Eleven thousand four hundred Republic troops landed unopposed. The temple complex was taken on the second day.
- On the third night the Sith nexus was used to amplify Dathomiri magick. The ground force was destroyed over about nine hours, and the dead were raised against the second wave. See [[The Battle of Yavin#Six days]].
- Maul was gone by the fourth day.
- Obi-Wan Kenobi held the northern landing zone for nineteen hours.
- On the sixth day Tarkin ordered the whole temple valley shelled from orbit. More than a thousand living Republic soldiers were still inside the target area.
- Nine thousand eight hundred Republic troops died, over a thousand of them by Republic guns. Sixteen hundred survived, six hundred of them because of Anakin Skywalker. Twenty-six of the thirty-one Jedi were killed. All twenty-six were raised. Mace Windu was supposedly among them.
- Yavin 4 was never retaken. An interdiction fleet has surrounded it ever since. Within the year the calendar was reset, first by the news services and finally by law. See [[The Battle of Yavin#Aftermath]].
- [[The Shadow War]] took its name from a news broadcast in its first weeks.
- [[Sevrin Dal]] was elected Chancellor six weeks after Yavin, on an open promise of revenge.
- Eight days after the last survivors came home, Dal asked for emergency powers. The Senate granted them.
- From Yavin on, every recruit to the Republic Corps was tested for Force sensitivity.
- In the eleventh week after Yavin, Obi-Wan Kenobi was made a general of the Republic.
- Fifteen weeks after Yavin, the Republic made the Order an offer. It promised restored rank, the return of the Coruscant Temple and a formal place in the war. About twenty-one hundred Jedi went back to Coruscant. About twenty-nine hundred stayed on Ossus. The Coruscant body took the name [[The Jedi Templars|Jedi Templars]] within the year.
- In the sixth month Ahsoka Tano left the place she had been living. She has not been found since.
- In the last months of the year, the bad briefing was established. The Outland Command's secret arrangement with Maul was published alongside it. Tarkin was summoned to answer for both. Seswenna did not hand him over. See [[The Battle of Yavin#The briefing]] and [[The Secessions#Seswenna, 1 ABY]].

### 1 ABY

- In the fourth month Seswenna voted to leave the Republic. Eight neighbouring sectors followed, and together they formed the [[The Greater Seswenna Union|Greater Seswenna Union]].
- The Corps garrisons in the nine sectors did not come home. The Union's armour went into full production in the month the Union was declared.
- Thrawn took command of the Union's fleet.
- [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] fully took the north of Mandalore. Over the following year, Death Watch reopened the armouries in the south.
- The Judicial Forces were revived after forty years of neglect.
- The Templars embedded Quinlan Vos inside the Shadow Collective. They did not inform Ossus.
- Luke Skywalker left Kenobi's command and was listed as rogue. In reality, Kenobi sent him.
- [[Dedra Meero]] compiled Anakin Skywalker's forty-one confirmed appearances. The paper was not published, and she was reassigned. See [[Anakin Skywalker#The record]].
- About eight months after the last survivors came home, the [[The Path of Light|Path of Light]] appeared.
- Barriss Offee sent Leia Skywalker the first of three private invitations. Leia declined it and reported it.
- Morgan Elsbeth began feeding Republic Intelligence.
- In the ninth month a ship matching no registry breached Nightsister territory and stayed for five hours.
- By the end of the year, at least seventy-eight sectors had asked whether they could follow Seswenna out. They asked their own lawyers and Senate representatives.

### 2 ABY

- In the second month Senator [[Ilai Vess]] read the Yavin 4 dig permit into the Senate record. Quelii voted to leave the Republic the same month. See [[The Secessions#The Vess case]].
- In the third month the Gordian Reach seceded, quietly and without fanfare.
- Seven weeks after Quelii left, the Chommell sector filed a petition to secede.
- In the sixth month Quelii and the Gordian Reach formed the [[The Secessions#Quelii and Gordian Reach, 2 ABY|Dathomir-Yavin Accord]].
- After Quelii seceded, the Republic came to [[Ferrix]] and exterminated every Collective asset there. More than seven hundred civilians died in what news channels outside the Republic called the Ferrix Massacre. See [[Ferrix#Ferrix Massacre, 2 ABY]].
- In the ninth month Thesme, D'Astan and Kalamith stopped remitting taxes to the Republic. None of the three had been given a seat in the Senate for decades.
- By the end of the year, more than sixty secession petitions were in review. The emergency powers had been renewed eight times. Three motions had been filed to remove Dal. See [[The Secessions#The petitions]].
