The **Low Republic** is the name of the age that followed the death of Palpatine. It is also the name of the Republic that lived through it. The name was coined by Coruscanti journalists during the reform years. The people it described adopted it without much protest. It was meant as a contrast with the High Republic of several centuries earlier, that confident, expanding, brightly-lit age the Jedi still taught to their younglings. What came after Palpatine was poorer in certainty and smaller in ambition. It was also permanently conscious of how narrowly it had survived. Several of the people who wrote the new charter used the term themselves in debate.

A new galactic epoch was pronounced later, and the calendar was reset not by the Republic's own wishes.

This wiki describes the galaxy from 19 BBY to the end of 2 ABY.

> The galaxy is bleak, on the edge of a precipice. Everyone has an idea on how to save it, but nobody can agree on what that is. This is the Low Republic, its fate is unwound, and it needs heroes more than ever.

## The history

1. [[The Death of Palpatine]]. In 19 BBY Mace Windu killed the Chancellor. Anakin Skywalker did not intervene.
2. [[The Long War]]. The Confederacy was beheaded in 19 BBY, not defeated. It fought on for four more years.
3. [[The Kamino Findings]]. In 18 BBY auditors found the orders built into every clone.
4. [[The Manumission Act]]. In 16 BBY every clone became a citizen.
5. [[The Republic Remade]]. The Chancellors, the new charter and what it did not repair.
6. [[The Census of 4 BBY]]. The first full count of the Republic in four centuries.
7. [[The Reckoning of the Jedi]]. A trial, a shrine, the move to Ossus and the schism, from 19 to 8 BBY.
8. [[The Battle of Yavin]]. Six days on the fourth moon of Yavin Prime, in 0 ABY.
9. [[The Secessions]]. Eleven sectors left the Republic in 1 and 2 ABY.
10. [[The Shadow War]]. The war against the Shadow Collective, which the Senate never declared.
